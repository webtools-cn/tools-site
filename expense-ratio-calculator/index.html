基金费率计算器的计算逻辑

本工具的核心计算逻辑：

  • r = returnRate/100
  • fA = feeA/100
  • fB = feeB/100
  • pv = principal*(1-purchaseFee/100)
  • valueA = finalValue(r-fA, years)
  • valueB = finalValue(r-fB, years)

计算原理

本工具的核心计算逻辑：

  • marketCap = price*shares
  • r = wacc/100
  • g = terminal/100
  • mid = (lo+hi)/2
  • intrinsic = computeDCF(fcf, expectedGrowth/100, r, g, 10)
  • intrinsicPerShare = intrinsic/shares

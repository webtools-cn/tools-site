## 二、竞品分析

### 1. calculator.net/calorie-calculator → 无AB测试计算器
### 2. abtestguide.com/calc/
- 功能: 输入control/variant的visitors+conversions
- 输出: 转化率、提升、z-score、p-value、显著性判断
- 置信度: 90%/95%/99%可选
### 3. vwo.com/ab-test-significance-calculator/

- 支持多variant
### 4. optimizely.com/sample-size-calculator/
- 侧重样本量计算

FAQ

Why does driving cost more than just fuel?

The true cost of driving includes fuel, parking, tolls, insurance, maintenance, and depreciation. This calculator includes fuel, parking, tolls, and estimates maintenance/depreciation at $0.08/km. Insurance and registration are not included.

What's the cheapest way to commute?

For short distances (under 2km), walking is free. For medium distances (2-8km), cycling or e-bike offers the best value. For longer distances (8km+), public transit is typically most economical. Driving is best when you have free parking or carpool.

What about mixed-mode commuting?

Many people combine modes (e.g. bike to station + train). Calculate each leg separately and add them. This calculator focuses on single-mode comparison for simplicity.

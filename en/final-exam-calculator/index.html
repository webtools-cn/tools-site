FAQ

How to calculate the grade I need on my final exam?

Formula: Final Score Needed = (Target Grade - Current Grade × Current Weight) / Final Exam Weight. Example: Current 85 (70% weight), Target 90, Final exam 30% weight: Need ≈ (90-85×0.7)/0.3 ≈ 101.7 (nearly impossible without extra credit).

How do different weights affect my final grade?

Weights determine each component's importance. Typical: Homework 10-20%, Midterm 20-30%, Final Exam 30-50%, Participation 5-10%. Higher final exam weight means it has more impact on your final grade.

Can I still pass if I fail the final exam?

It depends on your accumulated grades and component weights. This tool lets you run scenarios - enter different final exam scores to see your final grade. If still failing, check for makeup exams or extra credit opportunities.

Is my grade data uploaded to a server?

No. This tool runs entirely in your browser. All grade data stays in your device's memory and is cleared when you close the page. No data is uploaded anywhere.

📖 Understanding Marginal Tax Rate

Marginal vs Effective Rate

Marginal rate is the tax rate on your next dollar of income. For example, earning an extra $10,000 might be taxed at 22%. Effective rate is your total tax divided by total income — usually lower due to progressive brackets.

Why Marginal Rate Matters

Knowing your marginal rate helps: 1) Negotiate salary knowing take-home pay; 2) Decide whether to take extra work; 3) Plan bonuses; 4) Optimize retirement contributions and investments.

About Refinance Breakeven Calculator

What is the refinance breakeven point?

The breakeven point is when cumulative monthly savings equal refinancing costs. After this point, you start saving money net of costs.

How much rate reduction makes refinancing worthwhile?

Generally, a rate drop of 0.5% or more with plans to hold past breakeven justifies refinancing. Larger balances mean larger savings.

What fees are included in refinancing?

Common closing costs: appraisal, attorney fees, title insurance, origination fees, prepaid interest. Typically 2-5% of loan amount in the US market.

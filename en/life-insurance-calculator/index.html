📘 DIME Method Explained

The DIME method breaks down life insurance needs into four categories:

  • D (Debt): All debts to be paid off, including funeral costs
  • I (Income): Income replacement for your family, typically 10-15 years
  • M (Mortgage): Remaining mortgage balance
  • E (Education): College and future education costs for children

Formula: Recommended Coverage = D + I + M + E - Existing Assets

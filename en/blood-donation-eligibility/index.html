Answer a few simple health questions to quickly check if you may qualify to donate blood. This tool is for reference only — final determination is made by medical staff.

For Reference Only

🩸 Question 1/8

Basic Info
What is your age?
US: 16+ (some states), most countries: 18-65

🩸 Question 2/8

Basic Info
What is your weight in kilograms?
Minimum: 50 kg (110 lbs) for most countries

🩸 Question 3/8

Health Status
Are you feeling healthy today? (No cold, fever, cough, or other acute symptoms)

🩸 Question 4/8

Health Status
Have you had any surgery, blood transfusion, tattoo, or piercing in the last 6 months?

🩸 Question 5/8

Medical History
Do you have any of the following conditions? (Heart disease, uncontrolled hypertension, hepatitis, HIV, epilepsy, cancer, etc.)

🩸 Question 6/8

Medications
Have you recently taken antibiotics or blood thinners (such as aspirin, warfarin)?

🩸 Question 7/8

Travel History
Have you traveled to a malaria-endemic area in the last 12 months? (e.g., parts of Africa, Southeast Asia)

🩸 Question 8/8

Lifestyle
Recent donation history?

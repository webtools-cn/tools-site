📱 Social Media Image Size Guide

Quickly look up image size specs for major social platforms. Covers Instagram, Facebook, X/Twitter, LinkedIn, YouTube, TikTok, Pinterest with profile, cover, post, and ad sizes. Click a platform to expand and see recommended dimensions and aspect ratios.

What is Social Media Image Size Guide?

Social Media Image Size Guide is a free online tool that runs entirely in your browser. No download or signup needed — enter Social Media Image Size Guide and other values to get results instantly. All data is processed locally.

Use Cases

  • Resize or convert images quickly before uploading
  • Compress photos for social media or email attachments
  • Generate QR codes for sharing links or contact info
  • Process images on mobile without downloading a dedicated app

Key Features

  • Completely Free: No registration, no limits
  • Privacy Safe: Data never leaves your browser
  • Cross-Platform: Any modern browser
  • Zero Dependencies: Open and use

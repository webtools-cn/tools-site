📱 Social Media Image Size Guide

Online social media image size reference. Covers Instagram, Facebook, X/Twitter, LinkedIn, YouTube, TikTok, Pinterest with profile, cover, post, and ad sizes.

Quickly look up image size specs for major social platforms. Click a platform to expand and see recommended dimensions and aspect ratios for avatars, covers, posts, and ads.

What is Social Media Image Size Guide?

Social Media Image Size Guide is a free online tool that runs entirely in your browser. No installation, no registration, just open and use. All data is processed locally.

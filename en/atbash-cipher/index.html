About This Tool

The Atbash cipher originated from ancient Hebrew and is a simple monoalphabetic substitution cipher. It works by completely reversing the alphabet: A becomes Z, B becomes Y, C becomes X... and so on. Since it's symmetric, encryption and decryption use the same algorithm. This tool also supports digit reversal (0↔9). All processing is done locally in your browser.

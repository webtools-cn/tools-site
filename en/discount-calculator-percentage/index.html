💡 Smart Shopping Tips

  • Compare unit prices: Don't just look at the discount — calculate price per unit for real value
  • Watch minimum spend thresholds: $30 off $200 sounds great, but if you only need $205 worth, you're saving just ~14.6%
  • Stack discounts: Many platforms allow coupon + store discount + member pricing stacking
  • Reverse comparison: If you have a budget cap, use reverse mode to find the original price you can afford
  • Beware fake discounts: Track prices before sales with this tool to avoid inflated "original" prices

FAQ

How do I calculate the sale price?

Sale Price = Original Price × (1 - Discount%/100). For example, a $200 item with 20% off = $200 × (1 - 20/100) = $160.

Which is better: percentage off or fixed discount?

It depends on the amount. A $30 off $200 is 15% off at exactly $200, but only 10% off at $300. This calculator lets you compare both.

Can I calculate the original price from the sale price?

Yes. Switch to Reverse mode, enter the sale price and discount percentage. Original Price = Sale Price ÷ (1 - Discount%/100).

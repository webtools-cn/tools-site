FAQ

What are the body shape types?

The main body shape types are: Inverted Triangle (broad shoulders, narrow hips), Rectangle (similar shoulder/waist/hip), Apple (larger waist), Pear (wider hips, narrower shoulders), and Hourglass (balanced shoulders/hips with defined waist).

How to measure body circumferences accurately?

Measure shoulder at the widest point, bust at the fullest part, waist at the narrowest point above the navel, and hips at the widest point. Keep the tape level and not too tight.

Does body shape affect clothing choices?

Yes! Knowing your body shape helps choose flattering styles. Inverted triangles suit V-necks and A-line skirts, pears benefit from emphasizing the upper body, and hourglass shapes look great in waist-defining designs.

🌐 Favicon Usage Guide

After downloading, place the ICO file in your website root directory and add to your HTML:

<link rel="icon" type="image/x-icon" href="/favicon.ico">
<link rel="shortcut icon" type="image/x-icon" href="/favicon.ico">

💡 Tip: Use a multi-size ICO (with at least 16x16 and 32x32) for the best compatibility.

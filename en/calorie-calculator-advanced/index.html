How It Works

• BMR = Basal Metabolic Rate — calories burned at complete rest.

• TDEE = BMR × Activity Multiplier — total daily energy expenditure.

• Weight Loss = TDEE - 20%, Muscle Gain = TDEE + 20%.

• Macros split: 50% carbs / 25% protein / 25% fat.

Why Mifflin-St Jeor?

The Mifflin-St Jeor equation is the most accurate BMR formula, recommended by the Academy of Nutrition and Dietetics. It has lower error than Harris-Benedict.

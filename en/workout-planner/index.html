💡 Training Tips:

    What is Workout Plan Generator?

    Workout Plan Generator is a free online tool that creates personalized weekly training plans based on your fitness goal, experience level, and available days per week. No download or signup needed — all processing runs locally in your browser.

    Use Cases

    • Create a structured weekly workout plan for fat loss or muscle gain
    • Get personalized routines based on your fitness level
    • Plan gym sessions with proper exercise splits and rest days
    • Build a home workout schedule without a personal trainer

    Key Features

    • 4 goal modes: Fat Loss, Muscle Gain, Endurance, General Health
    • 3 fitness levels with appropriate exercise selection
    • Customizable days per week (3-6 days)
    • One-click copy to save your training plan
    • Print-friendly output for gym reference

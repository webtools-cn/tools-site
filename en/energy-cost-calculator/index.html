💡 About the Energy Cost Calculator

This energy cost calculator helps you quickly estimate the electricity cost of your appliances. Simply enter the wattage, daily usage hours, and your local electricity rate to calculate daily, monthly, and yearly costs.

🔍 Formula

Daily Cost = Watts × Hours ÷ 1000 × Rate per kWh

Monthly Cost = Daily Cost × 30

Yearly Cost = Daily Cost × 365

📝 Energy Saving Tips

  • Raising AC temperature by 1°C saves ~6-8% energy
  • LED bulbs use ~80% less energy than incandescent
  • Appliances in standby still consume power — unplug when not in use
  • Choose high energy-efficiency rated appliances for long-term savings

About List Deduplicator

Free online list deduplication tool. Quickly remove duplicates and keep unique values. Case-sensitive/insensitive options, preserve empty lines. No signup required.

FAQ

How does deduplication work?

The tool reads input line by line, uses a hash set to detect duplicates, keeps only the first occurrence, and preserves original order.

What does case sensitivity mean?

When enabled, 'Apple' and 'apple' are treated as different. When disabled, they're treated as the same.

Is my data safe?

Completely safe. All computation happens on your device — Paste list..., other values and other inputs stay in your browser — no data is uploaded.

About Discount Calculator

The Discount Calculator is a free online tool that computes the final price after applying a percentage discount and any additional fixed-amount reduction. Perfect for shopping sales, comparing deals, and knowing exactly how much you save. Works on mobile and desktop, all calculations run locally in your browser.

Discount Calculator in 3 steps

  1. Enter the original price of the item
  2. Enter the discount percentage (e.g. 25 for 25% off)
  3. Add any extra fixed-amount off, then click "Calculate"

How is the final price calculated?

The final price equals the original price minus the percentage discount, minus any additional fixed-amount off. The result is never negative.

Can I use this for stacked discounts?

This calculator supports one percentage discount plus one fixed-amount reduction. For multiple percentage discounts, calculate them one at a time.

Is this calculator free?

Yes, completely free. No sign-up or download needed.

Is my data safe?

Absolutely. All calculations run locally in your browser. No data is uploaded to any server.

Too many discounts to track? See everything clearly: original price, discount rate, final price, and savings — no matter how complex the deal.

How to Use

FAQ

20% off = 80% of original?

Yes, both are ×0.8. But '$40 off $200' requires minimum spend.

How to calculate discount %?

Discount% = Final/Original × 100%. Paying $75 on $100 = 25% off.

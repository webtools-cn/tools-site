What is eGFR Calculator?

eGFR Calculator is a free online tool that runs entirely in your browser. No download or signup needed — enter Serum Creatinine and Age to get results instantly. All data is processed locally.

Use Cases

  • Monitor kidney function trends for chronic kidney disease (CKD) patients
  • Estimate GFR to help guide medication dosing adjustments in clinical practice
  • Screen for early signs of kidney impairment during routine health checks
  • Track eGFR changes over time during treatment follow-up

Key Features

  • Uses the 2021 CKD-EPI formula (no race adjustment)
  • Completely Free: No registration, no limits
  • Privacy Safe: Data never leaves your browser
  • Instant CKD stage classification with color-coded results

📖 Roman Numeral Rules

Base symbols: I=1, V=5, X=10, L=50, C=100, D=500, M=1000

Rule 1: Symbols are written from highest to lowest value, and their values are added (III=3, XXX=30).

Rule 2: A smaller number placed before a larger one is subtracted (IV=4, IX=9); placed after, it is added (VI=6, XI=11).

Rule 3: A symbol may appear at most three times in a row (CCC=300, but CCCC is not allowed).

About Painting Cost Calculator

The Painting Cost Calculator is a free online tool that estimates the amount of paint needed and total cost based on wall area, paint coverage rate, and price per liter. Works on mobile and desktop, all calculations run locally in your browser.

Painting Cost Calculator in 3 steps

  1. Enter the wall area in square meters
  2. Enter the paint coverage (m² per liter)
  3. Enter the price per liter of paint
  4. Click "Calculate" to see paint quantity and cost

How is paint quantity calculated?

Paint needed = wall area ÷ coverage rate. Cost = liters needed × price per liter.

Is this tool accurate?

Yes, the calculation uses standard formulas. Results are rounded up to whole liters.

Do I need to download anything?

No download needed — it runs entirely in your browser.

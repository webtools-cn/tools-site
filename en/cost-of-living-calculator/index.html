FAQ

What is a cost of living calculator?

A cost of living calculator helps you compare living expenses between two cities across 8 categories: housing, food, transportation, utilities, entertainment, healthcare, shopping, and education. It shows you exactly how much you'd save or spend extra by moving.

Where can I get cost data?

We recommend Numbeo (numbeo.com) or Expatistan (expatistan.com) for global city cost data. These sites provide detailed category-level prices you can plug into this calculator.

Is the calculator accurate?

Accuracy depends on the data you input. Everyone's spending habits differ, so we recommend entering your actual or estimated expenses. The calculator does the math; results are for reference only.

Why compare cost of living?

When considering a job offer or relocation, salary alone doesn't tell the full story. A 30% higher salary means nothing if living costs are 50% higher. This tool quantifies the real difference.

❓ FAQ

What is social media engagement rate?

Social media engagement rate is a key metric that measures how much users interact with your content. Interactions include likes, comments, shares, and saves. Higher engagement means more appealing content and typically more algorithmic promotion.

How many ways to calculate engagement rate?

Three main methods: (1) By Impressions: ER = Interactions / Impressions × 100%; (2) By Reach: ER = Interactions / Reach × 100%; (3) By Followers: ER = Interactions / Followers × 100%.

What is a good engagement rate?

Instagram: 1-3% average, 3-6% high, 6%+ excellent. TikTok: 5-10% normal, 10%+ excellent. Facebook: 0.5-1% normal. More followers typically means lower engagement rate.

How can I improve my engagement rate?

Post high-quality original content, use trending hashtags, post at optimal times, actively reply to comments, use interactive stickers, create video content, and maintain a consistent posting schedule.

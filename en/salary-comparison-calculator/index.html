What is Salary Comparison Calculator?

Salary Comparison Calculator is a free online tool that runs entirely in your browser. No download or signup needed — enter Annual Salary and Annual Bonus to get results instantly. All data is processed locally.

Use Cases

  • Compare two job offers before making a career decision
  • Evaluate salary packages with bonuses, transport, and meal benefits
  • Calculate total compensation differences between employers
  • Make informed salary negotiation decisions with data

Key Features

  • Completely Free: No registration, no limits
  • Privacy Safe: Data never leaves your browser
  • Cross-Platform: Any modern browser
  • Zero Dependencies: Open and use

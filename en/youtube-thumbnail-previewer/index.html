📖 How to Use

  1. Enter a link: Paste a YouTube video URL (e.g. youtube.com/watch?v=xxx) or an image URL.
  2. Auto-extract: For YouTube links, the tool automatically fetches the highest resolution thumbnail.
  3. Switch scenes: Click the tabs to preview thumbnails in Search Results, Sidebar, Mobile View, and more.
  4. Resolution comparison: View all available thumbnail resolutions to check visual quality at different sizes.
  5. Compare mode: Load two thumbnails side-by-side to pick the best version.
  6. Download & share: Click Download to save the current thumbnail, or Copy URL to share it.

❓ FAQ

What is the YouTube Thumbnail Previewer?▼
It's a free online tool that extracts YouTube video thumbnails and previews how they look in simulated YouTube interfaces — search results, sidebar recommendations, and mobile views. Helps creators evaluate thumbnail visual appeal before publishing.
What thumbnail resolutions are supported?▼
All official YouTube thumbnail resolutions: 120×90 (Default), 320×180 (MQ), 480×360 (HQ), 640×480 (SD), and 1280×720 (HD/Max).
Can I upload custom images for preview?▼
Yes! Paste any image URL to preview how it looks in simulated YouTube search results and sidebar recommendations, helping you choose the best thumbnail before uploading.
Is this tool free?▼
Yes, completely free. No sign-up required, no downloads — use it directly in your browser.
Why is thumbnail optimization important?▼
Thumbnails are the 'storefront' of your YouTube videos. A great thumbnail significantly boosts Click-Through Rate (CTR), directly impacting recommendations and view counts. This preview tool lets you simulate how thumbnails appear across different scenarios before publishing.

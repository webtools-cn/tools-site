📖 How to Use

Enter a force value and select its unit. The tool will instantly convert it to all supported units: Newtons (N), kilonewtons (kN), pound-force (lbf), kips, dynes (dyn), kilogram-force (kgf), tonne-force (tf), and ounce-force (ozf).

All calculations happen locally in your browser — no data is uploaded.

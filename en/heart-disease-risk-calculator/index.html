    📖 About CHD Risk Assessment

    What is Coronary Heart Disease?

    CHD occurs when coronary arteries become narrowed by plaque buildup, reducing blood flow to the heart. It's the leading cause of death worldwide (~9 million annually). Early risk assessment and management are key to prevention.

    Cholesterol & Heart Health

    LDL (bad): Lower is better, ideal <100 mg/dL.
    HDL (good): Higher is better, men >40, women >50 mg/dL.
    TC/HDL ratio: Ideal <3.5, >5.0 is high risk. Each 1 unit reduction lowers CHD risk ~20%.

    Modifiable vs Fixed Risk

    Modifiable: Hypertension, high cholesterol, smoking, diabetes, obesity, inactivity, poor diet.
    Fixed: Age, gender, family history. The good news: 80% of heart disease events are preventable through lifestyle changes!

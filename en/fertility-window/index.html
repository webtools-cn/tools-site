💡 Conception Tips

• 1-2 days before ovulation is the best time to conceive (~25-30% chance)

• Sperm can survive in the female body for 3-5 days, egg survives 12-24 hours

• Have intercourse every 1-2 days to cover the entire fertile window

• Stress, illness, and medication may affect ovulation timing

• Use ovulation test strips or basal body temperature for better accuracy

📖 About Fertility Window

The fertility window is the time during a menstrual cycle when pregnancy is most likely. This calculator uses standard cycle algorithms with ~80% accuracy. If your cycles are irregular, use the average of your last 3-6 cycles.

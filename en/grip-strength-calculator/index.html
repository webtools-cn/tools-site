FAQ

Why is grip strength important?

Grip strength is a key indicator of overall muscle strength and health. Research links it to cardiovascular health, longevity, and daily function.

What is a normal grip strength?

Adult males average ~45-55kg, females ~25-35kg. Grip strength peaks around age 30-40 then gradually declines.

How to improve grip strength?

Use grip trainers, farmer's walks, pull-ups, and dumbbell training. Aim for 2-3 sessions/week, 3-5 sets, holding 10-15 seconds each.

FAQ

How do you calculate the cost of a meeting?

Meeting Cost = Number of Attendees × Average Hourly Rate × Duration (hours). For example, 10 people in a 1-hour meeting at $50/hr = 10 × $50 = $500. This tool also calculates weekly and yearly cumulative costs.

What is considered a high meeting cost?

A 2-hour meeting with 10 highly-paid employees can easily exceed $2,000. If held weekly, annual costs can reach over $100,000. Use this calculator to quantify costs and consider shortening time, reducing attendees, or using async communication.

How can I reduce meeting costs?

1) Set clear agendas and shorten duration; 2) Invite only essential participants; 3) Replace status syncs with emails/docs; 4) Establish 'no-meeting days'; 5) Change 1-hour meetings to 25 or 50 minutes; 6) Show team the costs with this tool to boost efficiency awareness.

How to calculate hourly rate from salary?

Quick estimate: Annual Salary ÷ 2000 (approx 2000 working hours/year). More precise: Annual Salary ÷ (52 weeks × weekly work hours). Example: $100,000/yr ÷ 2000 = $50/hour.

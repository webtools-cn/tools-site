FAQ

How is meeting cost calculated?

Formula: Attendees × Hourly Rate × (Duration ÷ 60). Example: 10 people at $50/hr for 1 hour = 10×50×1 = $500. This excludes preparation time and meeting room costs.

Why calculate meeting costs?

Research shows middle managers spend 35% of their week in meetings, and executives 50%+. Quantifying costs helps reduce unnecessary meetings, control attendee count, and shorten duration — boosting organizational efficiency.

How to estimate hourly rate?

Rough formula: Annual salary ÷ 2000 hours (250 working days × 8 hours). E.g. $100K/year ≈ $50/hr. You can also use role-based averages: Junior $25-50/hr, Mid $50-100/hr, Senior $100-200/hr.

Macronutrients are the main sources of energy for your body — scientifically distributing protein, carbs, and fat helps you achieve your fitness goals

Macronutrients include protein (4 kcal/g), carbohydrates (4 kcal/g), and fat (9 kcal/g). Different diet types correspond to different macro ratios: balanced diet for daily maintenance, high protein for muscle building, low carb for fat loss, and keto diet with fat as the primary energy source.

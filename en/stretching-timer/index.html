FAQ

How long should stretching last?

Hold each stretch 15-30 seconds, total routine 5-15 minutes. Dynamic stretching before exercise, static stretching after for best results.

Can stretching prevent injuries?

Yes. Regular stretching improves joint range of motion, muscle elasticity, and blood circulation, reducing strain and sprain risks.

When is the best time to stretch?

Dynamic stretches before workout as warm-up, static stretches after as cool-down. Avoid intense static stretching on cold muscles.

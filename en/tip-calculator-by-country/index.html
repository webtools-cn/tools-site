📖 Global Tipping Guide

Tipping customs vary dramatically worldwide: the US expects 15-25%, Japan considers tipping rude, and many European countries include service charges in the bill. This tool compiles the latest traveler feedback and local customs to help you avoid awkward moments.

❓ Why do tipping customs vary so much?

It comes down to local wage structures and service culture. US servers earn below minimum wage and depend on tips. Japan practices "omotenashi" (hospitality) where excellent service is expected without extra pay. Nordic countries have high wages and strong social safety nets, making tips unnecessary.

❓ Will I be chased down for not tipping?

In the US and Canada, possibly — servers may follow you to ask if something was wrong. In Asia and Northern Europe, not at all. The best approach is researching customs before you travel.

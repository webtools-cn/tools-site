📖 How to Use

• Enter the total tip amount for the shift (supports any currency).

• Use Quick Presets to instantly load common team configurations, or add staff members manually.

• Add staff members by role. System auto-assigns fair weights — Server (5), Bartender (4), Runner/Kitchen (3), Host/Busser (2).

• Select "Custom" to manually set weight values (0.5-10) for flexible distribution.

• Click "Calculate" to distribute tips proportionally by weight, rounded to the nearest cent. Any rounding discrepancy is automatically corrected.

• Click "Copy Results" to export the distribution details to clipboard in one click.

• All calculations are done locally in your browser — no data is ever uploaded.

❓ FAQ

What is a Tip Pool?

A Tip Pool is a common practice in the restaurant and service industry: all tips collected during a shift are pooled together and distributed fairly among staff based on each role's contribution weight. Servers who directly interact with guests typically receive the highest weight, while kitchen and support staff receive slightly lower weights.

How should weights be set?

Common practice: Server(5) > Bartender(4) > Runner/Kitchen(3) > Host/Busser(2). You can also choose "Custom" for flexible adjustments. Higher weight means a larger share — someone with weight 5 earns 2.5x more than someone with weight 2.

Are calculations accurate?

The system calculates proportionally by weight, rounded to the nearest cent. Due to rounding, totals may occasionally differ by ±0.01 — the system auto-detects and corrects any discrepancy.

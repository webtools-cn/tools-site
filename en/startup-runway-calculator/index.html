📖 What is Startup Runway?

Startup runway is the number of months a company can continue operating before depleting its cash reserves — the most critical survival metric for startups. Net Burn Rate = Monthly Expenses - Monthly Revenue. It's generally recommended to start a new funding round when runway reaches 6-12 months. If monthly revenue covers monthly expenses (runway = ∞), the company has achieved breakeven — one of the most important milestones for any startup.

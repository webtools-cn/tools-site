📖 Cat Age Conversion Rules

  • 1st year = 15 human years (rapid growth)
  • 2nd year = 24 human years (+9 years)
  • Year 3+ = +4 human years per cat year
  • Indoor cats live 12-18 years on average (64-88 human years)

What is Cat Age Calculator - Cat to Human Years Converter?

Cat Age Calculator - Cat to Human Years Converter is a free online tool that runs entirely in your browser. No download or signup needed — enter Cat Age and Months to get results instantly. All data is processed locally.

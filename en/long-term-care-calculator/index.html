How to Use

1. Select care type (In-Home Care/Assisted Living/Nursing Home)

2. Enter your current age and expected age when care is needed

3. Set expected care duration and current monthly care cost

4. Adjust the annual inflation rate

5. Click "Calculate" to see the total future care cost

Use Cases

Scenario 1: Retirement Planning

Middle-aged individuals can use this tool to estimate future long-term care costs and incorporate them into retirement savings goals.

Scenario 2: Care Option Comparison

Compare costs across in-home care, assisted living, and nursing home options to find the most suitable arrangement.

Scenario 3: Insurance Needs Assessment

Based on estimated care costs, evaluate whether long-term care insurance is needed and determine appropriate coverage amounts.

Additional Information

Long-term care costs are among the most overlooked aspects of retirement planning. Data shows that 70% of people over 65 will need some form of long-term care. Care cost growth rates typically exceed general inflation by 3-5%. Early planning and saving are essential.

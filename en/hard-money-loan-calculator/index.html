FAQ

Do I need to install anything?

No. The hard money loan calculator is a pure front-end tool that runs directly in your browser.

What is a hard money loan?

A hard money loan is a short-term, asset-based loan commonly used for fix-and-flip projects. Rates and fees are higher than traditional loans, with terms typically 6-24 months.

Is my data secure?

Completely secure. All data processing happens in your browser's local memory.

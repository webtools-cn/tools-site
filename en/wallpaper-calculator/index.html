About Wallpaper Calculator

The biggest worry buying wallpaper is overbuying or running short. Our calculator estimates exact rolls based on wall dimensions and wallpaper size, deducting doors and windows.

How to Use

  1. Enter wall length and height
  2. Select wallpaper width
  3. Click Calculate to see rolls needed

FAQ

How much does one roll cover?

Standard: 0.53m×10m ≈ 5.3㎡. With pattern waste 10-15%, effective ≈ 4.5㎡.

How much extra to buy?

10-15% extra. Complex patterns need 15-20% buffer.

📊 Excel to PDF

Free online Excel to PDF converter for XLS/XLSX files. Client-side processing preserves table formatting, cell styles, and column widths. Files stay local, fast and secure.

📊

Click to upload or drag & drop files here

Batch supported, max 50MB per file

📖 How to Use

1. Upload Excel: Select an XLS/XLSX file or drag it to the upload area.

2. Page Settings: Choose paper size (A4/Letter/Legal) and orientation (Portrait/Landscape).

3. Convert & Download: Click 'Convert to PDF' and download automatically when ready.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to install any software?

No. Excel to PDF is a web-based tool — just open it in your browser and start using it. No downloads, plugins, or extensions required.

Are there any usage limits for Excel to PDF?

Excel to PDF has no usage limits — it's free and unlimited. Since processing is done client-side, speed depends on your device. For very large files, we recommend processing in batches for the best experience.

Is Excel to PDF free to use?

Yes, Excel to PDF is completely free to use. No registration or login required — all features are available immediately.

Is my data safe when using Excel to PDF?

Absolutely. Everything runs locally: Paper and Orientation are processed on your device, never sent to a server. No data is sent to any server, so there's zero risk of data leaks. Your information stays completely under your control.

Does Excel to PDF upload my data to a server?

No. Excel to PDF uses client-side processing — everything runs in your browser. Your data never leaves your device, and the tool works even without an internet connection.

Can I use Excel to PDF on my phone?

Yes. Excel to PDF is fully responsive and works on phones, tablets, and desktops. The interface adapts automatically to your screen size.

Will the PDF quality decrease after processing?

We preserve original quality whenever possible. For compression, you can choose the compression level to balance file size and quality.

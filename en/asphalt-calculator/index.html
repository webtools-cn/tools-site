📖 Asphalt Calculator Guide

How to Calculate Asphalt Quantity

The basic formula for calculating asphalt is: Volume = Area × Thickness, then Tons = Volume × Density × Compaction Factor.

  • Rectangle: Area = Length × Width
  • Circle: Area = π × Radius²
  • Volume: Area × Thickness (ensure consistent units)
  • Tonnage: Volume × Asphalt Density (hot mix ≈ 145 lb/ft³ or 2,323 kg/m³)

Recommended Asphalt Thickness

  • 🚗 Residential driveway: 2-3 inches (5-7.5 cm)
  • 🅿️ Commercial parking lot: 3-4 inches (7.5-10 cm)
  • 🛣️ Road surface: 2-4 inches (5-10 cm)
  • 🚛 Heavy-duty areas: 4-6 inches (10-15 cm)

Asphalt Density Reference

  • Hot Mix Asphalt (HMA): 145 lb/ft³ (2,323 kg/m³)
  • Warm Mix Asphalt: 137-147 lb/ft³ (2,200-2,350 kg/m³)
  • Cold Mix Asphalt: 125-137 lb/ft³ (2,000-2,200 kg/m³)
  • Recycled Asphalt: 131-144 lb/ft³ (2,100-2,300 kg/m³)

Compaction Factor Explained

The compaction factor converts loose asphalt volume to compacted volume. Hot mix asphalt is compacted during installation, typically reducing volume by 10%-25%. If your thickness refers to the compacted thickness (which is standard practice), set the factor to 1.0. If you need to calculate loose material for ordering, set it to 1.1-1.25.

Multi-Zone Calculation

If your project includes multiple areas with different shapes or thicknesses (e.g., a driveway plus a parking pad), use the "Add this area to total" feature. Calculate each zone separately and the tool will automatically combine the totals.

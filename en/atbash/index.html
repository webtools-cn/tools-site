What is Atbash Cipher?

Atbash Cipher is a free online tool that runs entirely in your browser. No download or signup needed — just paste your text and convert it instantly. All data is processed locally.

Use Cases

  • Reverse plaintext alphabet substitution to encrypt or decrypt messages
  • Solve puzzles and riddles that use the Atbash cipher
  • Decode historical texts or cipher challenges
  • Teach the basics of monoalphabetic substitution ciphers

Key Features

  • Completely Free: No registration, no limits
  • Privacy Safe: Data never leaves your browser
  • Cross-Platform: Any modern browser
  • Zero Dependencies: Open and use

📖 About Hiccups

What are hiccups?

Hiccups (singultus) are involuntary spasms of the diaphragm followed by sudden closure of the vocal cords. Common triggers: eating too fast, carbonated drinks, excitement.

How long do they last?

Transient hiccups typically last from a few minutes to under an hour, rarely exceeding 48 hours. Persistent hiccups beyond 48 hours warrant medical attention.

Quick remedies

Hold your breath for 30 seconds, drink a glass of cold water slowly, swallow a teaspoon of sugar, bend forward and drink water, or breathe into a paper bag.

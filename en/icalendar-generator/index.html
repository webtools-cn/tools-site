What is iCalendar Generator?

iCalendar Generator is a free online tool that runs entirely in your browser. No download or signup needed — enter eventtitle and description to get results instantly. All data is processed locally.

Use Cases

  • Calculate time intervals when scheduling events
  • Convert time zones for international communication
  • Track important dates like age or countdowns
  • Quick time checks on mobile without calendar apps

Key Features

  • Completely Free: No registration, no limits
  • Privacy Safe: Data never leaves your browser
  • Cross-Platform: Any modern browser
  • Zero Dependencies: Open and use

📖 Understanding Financial Freedom

What is a FI Number?

FI Number = Annual Expenses ÷ Safe Withdrawal Rate. At 4% SWR, FI Number = Annual Expenses × 25. This is the total assets needed so passive income covers all living expenses.

How savings rate affects timeline

Savings rate is the #1 factor in reaching FI. At 50% savings rate: ~17 years. At 75% savings rate: ~7 years. Increasing savings rate is more controllable than chasing high returns.

What is the FIRE movement?

FIRE = Financial Independence, Retire Early. Core philosophy: achieve financial independence before traditional retirement age through high savings rates (50-70%+) and rational investing, gaining freedom to choose your lifestyle.

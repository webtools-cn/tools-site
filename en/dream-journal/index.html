What is Dream Journal?

Dream Journal is a free online tool that runs entirely in your browser. No download or signup needed — record the date, title, content, mood, and tags of your dreams, then review your dream records and statistics. All data is stored locally on your device.

Use Cases

  • Track recurring dream themes and symbols over time
  • Discover patterns between your mood and dream content
  • Keep a private journal without installing any app
  • Review your most common dream tags at a glance

Key Features

  • Completely Free: No registration, no limits
  • Privacy Safe: Data never leaves your browser
  • Cross-Platform: Works on any modern browser
  • Zero Dependencies: Open and use

What is HSA Calculator?

HSA Calculator is a free online tool that runs entirely in your browser. No download or signup needed — enter Plan Type and Age to get results instantly. All data is processed locally.

Use Cases

  • Check the maximum amount you can contribute to your HSA each year
  • Estimate the tax savings of HSA contributions based on your marginal tax rate
  • Compare individual vs. family plans and catch-up limits for ages 55+
  • Model long-term growth to plan HSA-funded medical and retirement expenses

Key Features

  • Completely Free: No registration, no limits
  • Privacy Safe: Data never leaves your browser
  • Cross-Platform: Any modern browser
  • Zero Dependencies: Open and use

About Lemonade Stand Profit Calculator

The Lemonade Stand Profit Calculator is a free online tool that helps calculate the costs, revenue, and profit of a lemonade stand. It's perfect for kids learning basic business skills. Works on both mobile and desktop, all calculations run locally in your browser.

Lemonade Stand Profit Calculator in 3 steps

  1. Enter the selling price per cup (e.g. $2)
  2. Enter the cost per cup (lemons, sugar, cups, e.g. $0.5)
  3. Enter the expected number of cups sold (e.g. 100), then click "Calculate" to see your profit

Is this tool accurate?

Calculations are based on standard business formulas, results are precise and reliable.

Do I need to download anything?

No download needed, just open the page and start calculating. Everything runs in your browser.

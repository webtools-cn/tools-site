About Coffee Cost Calculator

Coffee Cost Calculator is a free online tool that compares the cost of making coffee at home versus buying it from a cafe. See exactly how much you save per day, month, and year. All calculations run locally in your browser.

How to Use

  1. Enter the cost per cup when making at home
  2. Enter the cost per cup at a cafe
  3. Enter how many cups per day

Is this tool accurate?

Yes, it uses simple multiplication based on your input values.

Does it account for equipment costs?

No, it only compares per-cup costs. Factor in equipment separately for full analysis.

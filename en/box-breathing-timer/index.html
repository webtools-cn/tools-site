What is Box Breathing?

Box breathing is a simple yet powerful relaxation technique used to reduce stress and improve focus. You inhale, hold, exhale, and hold again — each step for the same length of time. This timer guides you through the rhythm so you can breathe mindfully without counting.

Use Cases

  • Reduce anxiety before exams, meetings, or public speaking
  • Calm down before sleep for better rest
  • Improve focus and concentration during work or study
  • Lower stress during workouts or recovery periods

📖 FAQ

What is HbA1c?

HbA1c (glycated hemoglobin) reflects average blood sugar over the past 2-3 months. Normal: below 5.7%, Prediabetes: 5.7%-6.4%, Diabetes: ≥6.5%.

What is eAG?

eAG (estimated Average Glucose) converts HbA1c percentage to daily blood sugar monitoring units. Formula: eAG(mg/dL) = 28.7 × A1C - 46.7.

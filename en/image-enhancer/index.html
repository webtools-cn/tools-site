How to Use

Upload an image, adjust brightness, contrast, saturation, temperature, exposure, highlights, shadows, and sharpness with sliders, then download the result. Everything runs locally: your image and settings are processed on your device, never sent to a server.

❓ FAQ

Is Image Enhancer free to use?

Yes, Image Enhancer is completely free to use. No registration or login required.

Does Image Enhancer upload my images?

No. All processing happens in your browser locally. Your images never leave your device.

What adjustments does Image Enhancer support?

Image Enhancer supports: Brightness, Contrast, Saturation, Temperature, Exposure, Highlights, Shadows, and Sharpness. Each parameter has a slider control with real-time preview.

What image formats are supported?

Supports JPG/JPEG, PNG, WebP, BMP, and other common formats. Enhanced images can be downloaded as PNG or JPEG.

Can I use Image Enhancer on mobile?

Yes. The tool is fully responsive and works on phones, tablets, and desktops.

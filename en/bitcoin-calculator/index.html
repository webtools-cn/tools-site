📋 FAQ

How do I use the Bitcoin calculator?

Enter the BTC amount and select your target fiat currency to see real-time conversion. Use the ROI calculator to analyze your investment performance.

Is the price data accurate?

We pull weighted average prices from multiple exchange APIs. Data refreshes every 60 seconds. Actual trading prices on your exchange may differ slightly.

Which currencies are supported?

USD, CNY, EUR, JPY, GBP, KRW, AUD, CAD, CHF, HKD, SGD, INR, TWD, BRL, RUB and more.

Is mining profit calculation accurate?

This is a simplified estimate based on current network difficulty and price. Actual profits depend on difficulty adjustments, miner efficiency, pool fees, and market conditions.

Is my data secure?

Yes. All calculations run locally in your browser. No data is uploaded or collected. We only fetch public price data from APIs.

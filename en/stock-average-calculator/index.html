💡 What is Average Cost?

When you buy the same stock at different prices, your average cost basis = total amount invested ÷ total shares owned. Knowing your average cost helps you assess whether you're in profit or loss, enabling smarter buy/sell decisions.

❓ FAQ

Does this include commissions?

This calculator uses net prices. To include commissions, add them to your buy price.

How many trades can I add?

Up to 20 trades.

📖 About Date Time Calculations

Date Difference

Calculates the total days between two dates, with week/month/year approximations, plus weekend and workday breakdowns.

Add / Subtract

Add or subtract days, weeks, months, or years from a base date to get the target date and day of week.

Time Difference

Precise time difference in seconds, minutes, hours, and formatted breakdown (days, hours, minutes, seconds).

Workdays

Counts business days between two dates, automatically excluding Saturdays and Sundays.

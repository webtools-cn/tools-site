❓ FAQ

Can this replace medical advice?

Absolutely not. This tool provides reference information only. Always follow your doctor's prescription or the medication label.

How is pediatric dosage calculated?

Pediatric dosage is typically weight-based (mg/kg), some by body surface area. This calculator provides weight-based estimates. Always verify age restrictions on the medication label.

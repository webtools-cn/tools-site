FAQ

What's the difference between FHA MIP and PMI?

FHA MIP is required for FHA loans, while PMI is for conventional loans. FHA MIP is generally more expensive and lasts longer.

Can FHA MIP be canceled?

FHA MIP can be canceled after 11 years if down payment >= 10%; with <10% down, MIP typically lasts the entire loan term.

Which loan type is better?

It depends: FHA is better for lower credit scores, conventional loans are better for good credit with sufficient down payment.

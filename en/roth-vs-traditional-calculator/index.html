About Roth vs Traditional

Roth and Traditional IRAs are the two most common individual retirement accounts in the US. Key difference: Roth = "pay tax now, tax-free later"; Traditional = "deduct now, pay tax later."

Selection Guide

  • •Retirement rate > current rate → Choose Roth
  • •Retirement rate < current rate → Choose Traditional
  • •Uncertain → Both (tax diversification)

Does Roth IRA have income limits?

Yes. In 2024, single filers with MAGI over $161,000 or married over $240,000 cannot contribute directly to Roth IRA, but can use the Backdoor Roth strategy.

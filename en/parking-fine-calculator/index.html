❓ What happens if I pay late?

Most cities impose late payment penalties. Chinese cities add 3% per day (capped at original amount). US cities may double the fine after 30 days. London doubles after 14 days. Always pay on time to avoid surcharges.

❓ Can I appeal a parking ticket?

Yes. If you believe the ticket was issued in error (unclear signage, broken meter), take photos as evidence and appeal through the local transportation authority. Success rates vary — pay the fine first and appeal afterwards.

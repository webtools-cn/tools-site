What is the Digital Clock?

The Digital Clock is a free online tool that displays real-time hours, minutes, and seconds on your screen. It supports 12/24 hour format switching, five color themes (dark, light, neon, forest, sunset), and a fullscreen mode. Perfect for desktop widgets, projector displays, screen recording, and presentations. Everything runs locally in your browser — no data is ever uploaded.

Use Cases

  • Keep track of time during screen recordings and presentations
  • Display the current time on a second monitor or projector
  • Use as a large, readable clock on desktop or mobile
  • Pick a theme that matches your workspace lighting

Key Features

  • Completely Free: No registration, no limits
  • Privacy Safe: Data never leaves your browser
  • Cross-Platform: Works on any modern browser
  • Zero Dependencies: Open and use

Frequently Asked Questions

What is superfat?

Superfat keeps a portion of oil un-saponified, leaving the bar more moisturizing and adding a safety buffer against lye-heavy mistakes. Typical range is 3%–8%; above 10% the bar may be soft, oily, or prone to rancidity. This calculator reduces lye by the superfat percentage automatically.

What is the difference between NaOH and KOH?

NaOH (sodium hydroxide) makes solid bars; KOH (potassium hydroxide) makes liquid soap/shampoo. Their saponification values differ — KOH is about 1.403× NaOH, and the calculator converts automatically.

How much water should I use?

Commonly 30%–40% of oil weight. More water means slower trace and longer curing; less water means faster trace and shorter working time. Beginners should use 35%–40%.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to install any software?

No. Svg To Base64 is a web-based tool — just open it in your browser and start using it. No downloads, plugins, or extensions required.

Is Svg To Base64 free to use?

Yes, Svg To Base64 is completely free to use. No registration or login required — all features are available immediately.

Does Svg To Base64 upload my data to a server?

No. Svg To Base64 uses client-side processing — everything runs in your browser. Your data never leaves your device, and the tool works even without an internet connection.

Can I use Svg To Base64 on my phone?

Yes. Svg To Base64 is fully responsive and works on phones, tablets, and desktops. The interface adapts automatically to your screen size.

Is my data safe when using Svg To Base64?

Absolutely. Everything runs locally: Enter SVG Code and Data URI are processed on your device, never sent to a server. No data is sent to any server, so there's zero risk of data leaks. Your information stays completely under your control.

What image formats are supported?

Common formats including PNG, JPEG, WebP, GIF, BMP, and SVG are supported. The exact formats depend on the specific tool.

Will image quality decrease after processing?

For lossless formats (like PNG), quality is preserved. For lossy formats (like JPEG), you can choose the output quality.

Are converted files compatible with other software?

Yes. Converted files follow standard format specifications and are compatible with mainstream software and platforms.

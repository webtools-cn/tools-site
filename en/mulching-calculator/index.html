❓ FAQ

How to calculate mulch quantity?
Mulch quantity = Area × Depth. For example, a 10×3 ft flower bed with 3 inches depth: volume = 30 × 0.25 = 7.5 cubic feet ≈ 0.28 cubic yards. You can then calculate weight based on mulch density.

What is the recommended mulch depth?
Flower beds: 2-3 inches, around trees: 3-4 inches, pathways: 4-6 inches. For first-time application, use the thicker end; for replenishing, use the thinner end.

How much area does one bag cover?
A standard 2 cubic foot bag covers approximately 12 sq ft at 2 inches deep, 8 sq ft at 3 inches deep, or 6 sq ft at 4 inches deep.

What are the differences between mulch types?
Wood chips are most common and retain moisture well; bark is attractive and long-lasting; pine needles suit acid-loving plants; stone is durable and won't decompose; rubber mulch is resilient, best for playgrounds.

Use Cases

Color Extraction Algorithm

The tool uses a color quantization algorithm. Images are scaled down to 200×200 and processed, then a bit-based segmentation algorithm groups colors into classes. After sorting by frequency, the most dominant colors are output.

Privacy Protection

All images are processed locally in your browser using canvas. Images are never uploaded to any server. After processing completes, the canvas data is cleared, ensuring your images stay secure.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data safe when using Color Palette Extractor?

Absolutely. Everything runs locally: Colors to extract and other values are processed on your device, never sent to a server. No data is sent to any server, so there's zero risk of data leaks. Your information stays completely under your control.

Does Color Palette Extractor upload my data to a server?

No. Color Palette Extractor uses client-side processing — everything runs in your browser. Your data never leaves your device, and the tool works even without an internet connection.

Can I use Color Palette Extractor on my phone?

Yes. Color Palette Extractor is fully responsive and works on phones, tablets, and desktops. The interface adapts automatically to your screen size.

Are there any usage limits for Color Palette Extractor?

Color Palette Extractor has no usage limits — it's free and unlimited. Since processing is done client-side, speed depends on your device. For very large files, we recommend processing in batches for the best experience.

Do I need to install any software?

No. Color Palette Extractor is a web-based tool — just open it in your browser and start using it. No downloads, plugins, or extensions required.

Are the color values accurate?

Color values are based on standard color space conversion algorithms with high precision. Display may vary slightly depending on monitor calibration.

📖 FAQ

What is self-employment tax?

Self-employment tax covers freelancers, independent contractors and other self-employed individuals must pay for Social Security and Medicare taxes. 2026 Self-Employment Tax Rate is 15.3%, Social Security tax: 12.4% (Income Cap $176,100), Medicare Tax 2.9% (No Cap).

Who Needs to Pay Self-Employment Tax?

Annual net self-employment income exceeding $400 individuals must pay self-employment tax. Including freelancers, independent contractors, small business owners, etc.

How to Use

  1. Enter Amount and Rate
    Enter the amount and tax rate percentage.
  2. Select Mode
    Choose add-tax or remove-tax mode.
  3. View Results
    See net price, tax amount, and gross price.

FAQ

What is sales tax?

Sales tax is an indirect tax on goods and services, typically calculated as a percentage of the price.

Add-tax vs remove-tax?

Add tax: enter net price, calculate tax and gross price. Remove tax: enter gross price, reverse-calculate net price.

Is my data uploaded?

No. All calculations happen locally in your browser.

❓ FAQ
What canvas sizes are supported?
Supports 8x8, 16x16, 32x32, 64x64 presets plus custom sizes. Small sizes for icons/avatars, large for complex artwork.
What export formats?
PNG export with transparent background. Also copy as Data URL for direct web use.
Are there eraser and eyedropper tools?
Yes! Eraser mode, eyedropper (click to pick color), undo/redo, clear canvas.

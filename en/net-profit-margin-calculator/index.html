📋 What Is Net Profit Margin?

Net Profit Margin is a core financial metric that measures a company's bottom-line profitability — how much of every dollar in revenue actually becomes profit.

Formula: Net Profit Margin = (Net Profit ÷ Total Revenue) × 100%

Where: Net Profit = Total Revenue − COGS − Operating Expenses − Taxes − Interest − Other Expenses

💡 How to Improve Your Net Margin

  • Increase revenue: Raise prices, expand sales volume, develop higher-margin product lines
  • Reduce COGS: Optimize supply chain, buy in bulk, find alternative suppliers
  • Control operating expenses: Streamline processes, boost efficiency, digitize operations
  • Optimize taxes: Leverage tax incentives and plan strategically
  • Cut interest costs: Restructure debt, lower your debt-to-equity ratio

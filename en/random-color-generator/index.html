Random Color Generator

Free online random color generator. Generate random colors with one click. Supports HEX, RGB, HSL output formats with real-time preview. Lock hue range for harmonious palettes. Perfect for designers seeking inspiration and UI color references.

Frequently Asked Questions

What color formats are supported?

HEX (e.g., #FF5733), RGB (e.g., rgb(255,87,51)), HSL (e.g., hsl(9,100%,60%)) — switch between them with one click.

Can I lock the hue?

Yes. Select a hue range (Red/Orange/Yellow/Green/Blue/Purple) to generate colors within that range only.

Can I generate color schemes?

Yes. Preview monochromatic, complementary, triadic, and analogous color schemes to find matching colors quickly.

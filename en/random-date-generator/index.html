Random Date Generator

Free online random date generator. Generates random dates within a specified time range. Supports multiple date formats (YYYY-MM-DD, MM/DD/YYYY, DD/MM/YYYY, etc.). Great for test data generation, raffles, and schedule simulation.

Frequently Asked Questions

How large can the date range be?

Any range between 1970 and 2099. Default is the past year to today.

What date formats are supported?

ISO (YYYY-MM-DD), US (MM/DD/YYYY), EU (DD/MM/YYYY), Chinese (YYYYyearMMmonthDDday), and more.

Can weekends be excluded?

Yes. Enable 'Weekdays Only' to exclude Saturday and Sunday.

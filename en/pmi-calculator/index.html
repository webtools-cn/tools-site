FAQ

What is PMI?

PMI (Private Mortgage Insurance) is typically required when your down payment is less than 20%.

When can PMI be canceled?

PMI can be canceled when your loan balance drops below 80% of the original home value. It automatically cancels at 78%.

What is the PMI rate?

PMI rates typically range from 0.5% to 2% of the loan amount, depending on down payment and credit score.

💡 Tips

  • Beginners should start with 60-62% hydration — it's easier to handle. Increase gradually as you gain confidence.
  • Fresh yeast to dry yeast conversion is 3:1 (3g fresh = 1g dry yeast)
  • Sourdough starter is typically 100% hydration. Set yeast percentage to 10-20% when using sourdough.
  • Olive oil is common in NY, Detroit, and Sicilian styles. Traditional Neapolitan does not use oil.
  • Keep salt below 3% of flour weight — too much inhibits yeast fermentation
  • Always use a kitchen scale for accuracy. Measuring cups are too imprecise for dough.

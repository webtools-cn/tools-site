FAQ

Q1: What are the image requirements?

Upload a square image of at least 512x512 pixels for best results (1024x1024 PNG recommended). PNG, JPG, SVG, and WebP formats are supported. Non-square images are automatically center-cropped to a square.

Q2: What icon sizes are generated?

iOS: 180x180 (@3x), 167x167 (iPad Pro), 152x152 (iPad), 120x120 (@2x), 87x87 (iPad @2x), 80x80 (iPad), 76x76 (iPad), 60x60 (@2x), 58x58 (@2x), 40x40, 29x29.Android: 512x512 (Play Store), 192x192 (xxxhdpi), 144x144 (xxhdpi), 96x96 (xhdpi), 72x72 (hdpi), 48x48 (mdpi).Web: 512x512, 192x192, 180x180, 32x32, 16x16.

Q3: Is data uploaded to servers?

No. All image processing happens locally in your browser using the Canvas API. Images are never uploaded to any server — your files stay on your device and remain completely private and secure.

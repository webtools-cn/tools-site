❓ FAQ

What are social share links?

Social share links are pre-built URLs that open a platform's share dialog with your content pre-filled. They're commonly used for social sharing buttons on websites.

Which platforms are supported?

Twitter/X, Facebook, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Telegram, Reddit, Email, and a direct copy-to-clipboard option.

Is my data uploaded?

No. All link generation happens locally in your browser. No data is sent to any server.

How do I use the generated links?

Copy the link and use it as the href in your share buttons, or click "Open" to test the share dialog directly.

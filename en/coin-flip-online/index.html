❓ FAQ
Is the coin flip truly random?
Uses JavaScript Math.random(). Not cryptographically random, but fair enough for everyday use.
Can I flip multiple times?
Yes, click 'Flip Coin' continuously. Statistics accumulate across flips.
What's the heads/tails probability?
Theoretically 50% each. As flips increase, results converge toward 50%.

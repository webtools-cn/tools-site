FAQ
Does domain age affect SEO?
Domain age is one ranking signal but not decisive. Older domains may have more backlinks and trust, but new domains can rank well with quality content too.
Does this tool use WHOIS data?
Yes, it queries public WHOIS services for registration and expiration dates. Some domains may hide details via privacy protection.
Which TLDs are supported?
All common TLDs are supported, including .com, .org, .net, .io, .co, and country-code TLDs.

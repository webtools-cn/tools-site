Frequently Asked Questions

How many watts in one horsepower?

Mechanical horsepower (hp): 1 hp ≈ 745.7 Watts (W). Metric horsepower (PS): 1 PS = 735.499 Watts (W). Most car specifications use metric horsepower. For example, 100 PS ≈ 73.55 kW.

What power units does this converter support?

It supports Watts (W), Kilowatts (kW), Megawatts (MW), Mechanical Horsepower (hp), Metric Horsepower (PS), BTU/h, and kcal/h. Covers everything from appliance wattage to engine output and HVAC capacity.

How do I convert BTU/h to kilowatts?

1 BTU/h ≈ 0.000293 kW. Conversely, 1 kW ≈ 3,412.14 BTU/h. BTU/h is commonly used for air conditioner capacity ratings — e.g., 12,000 BTU/h ≈ 3.517 kW.

Is my data sent to a server?

No. All conversions happen locally in your browser. The tool works offline once loaded and your data stays private on your device.

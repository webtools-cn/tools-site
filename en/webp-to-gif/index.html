❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to install any software?

No. Webp To Gif is a web-based tool — just open it in your browser and start using it. No downloads, plugins, or extensions required.

Is my data safe when using Webp To Gif?

Absolutely. All processing happens locally in your browser. No data is sent to any server, so there's zero risk of data leaks. Your information stays completely under your control.

Can I use Webp To Gif on my phone?

Yes. Webp To Gif is fully responsive and works on phones, tablets, and desktops. The interface adapts automatically to your screen size.

Does Webp To Gif upload my data to a server?

No. Webp To Gif uses client-side processing — everything runs in your browser. Your data never leaves your device, and the tool works even without an internet connection.

Are there any usage limits for Webp To Gif?

Webp To Gif has no usage limits — it's free and unlimited. Since processing is done client-side, speed depends on your device. For very large files, we recommend processing in batches for the best experience.

What image formats are supported?

Common formats including PNG, JPEG, WebP, GIF, BMP, and SVG are supported. The exact formats depend on the specific tool.

Will image quality decrease after processing?

For lossless formats (like PNG), quality is preserved. For lossy formats (like JPEG), you can choose the output quality.

Are converted files compatible with other software?

Yes. Converted files follow standard format specifications and are compatible with mainstream software and platforms.

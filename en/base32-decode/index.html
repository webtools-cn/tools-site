How to Use

Free online Base32 decoding tool. Decode Base32 encoded strings back to original text, supporting RFC 4648 standard.

All data is processed locally in your browser and never uploaded to any server, ensuring your data security.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely free. No registration, no download required. All functions run locally in your browser, data never leaves your device.

Does data go through a server?

No. This is a pure client-side application. All operations happen locally in your browser. Your data never leaves your device.

📋 Instructions

  2. Quiet environment: Find a quiet place and minimize background noise.
  3. Set volume: Use the slider above to set a comfortable volume level (70% recommended).

  5. Both ears: Left ear first, then right ear. Total test time: approximately 3-5 minutes.

❓ FAQ

How accurate is this hearing test?▼
This test is based on standard pure-tone audiometry principles, but limited by browser audio API precision, headphone quality, and ambient noise. Results are for reference only. For accurate diagnosis, visit an ENT specialist.
How long does the test take?▼
A complete test for both ears takes approximately 3-5 minutes. We test 7 frequency points (125/250/500/1000/2000/4000/8000Hz), playing tones at multiple volume levels to determine your threshold.
What is the normal hearing range?▼
Normal hearing thresholds are 0-25 dB HL. 25-40dB indicates mild hearing loss, 40-55dB moderate, 55-70dB moderately severe, 70-90dB severe, and above 90dB profound hearing loss.
Why can't I hear certain frequencies?▼
Different frequencies correspond to different hair cells in the cochlea. High-frequency hearing (4000-8000Hz) typically declines first with age. If you cannot hear certain frequencies at all, consult a doctor.

📖 About Quick Ratio

Quick Ratio = (Current Assets - Inventory) ÷ Current Liabilities. It excludes slower-to-liquidate inventory for a more conservative liquidity assessment.

Benchmarks: >1.0 Healthy | 0.5-1.0 Caution | <0.5 Crisis. ⚠️ Standards vary by industry; retail typically lower.

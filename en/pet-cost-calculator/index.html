📖 How to Use

This pet cost calculator helps you estimate the full cost of pet ownership:

Step 1: Select Pet Type

Dogs, cats, rabbits, and hamsters have very different cost profiles.

Step 2: Set Size and Lifespan

Large dogs typically cost 50%+ more for food and vet care than small dogs.

Step 3: Enter Cost Details

Include food, vet care, grooming, other expenses (toys/treats/insurance) and one-time adoption/purchase fee.

Step 4: View Total Costs

See monthly, annual and lifetime totals to evaluate if you're financially ready for a pet.

💡 Money-Saving Tips

  • Adopt, don't shop: Shelter fees are much lower and include basic medical care.
  • Buy food in bulk: Large bags of pet food cost less per pound and can save 20-30%.
  • Pet insurance: $30-$50/month can prevent massive unexpected vet bills.
  • DIY grooming: Learning basic grooming skills saves hundreds per year.

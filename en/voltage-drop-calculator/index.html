❓ FAQ

What is voltage drop and how is it calculated?

Voltage drop is voltage loss due to wire resistance. DC formula: Vd = 2×I×R×L. R = ρ/A, copper ρ=0.0172. Example: 2.5mm² copper, 30m, 10A: Vd=2×10×(0.0172/2.5)×30=4.13V.

What is acceptable voltage drop per NEC?

NEC: feeder ≤2%, branch ≤3%, total ≤5%. This tool: green (<3%), yellow (3-5%), red (>5% - upgrade wire size).

Copper vs aluminum wire?

Copper resistivity: 0.0172, aluminum: 0.0282. Aluminum needs ~1.6× cross-section. Copper conducts better; aluminum is lighter and cheaper but needs special anti-oxidation connections.

FAQ

What's the difference between one-way and mutual NDA?

One-way NDA only binds the receiving party, suitable for unilateral information disclosure. Mutual NDA binds both parties, suitable for cooperation where both share sensitive information.

How to set the confidentiality term?

Typically set to 2-5 years, can also be customized based on business needs. The receiving party's confidentiality obligation ends after the term expires.

Is the generated NDA legally binding?

The NDA generated is for reference only and does not constitute legal advice. It is recommended to have it reviewed by a professional lawyer before formal use.

FAQ

What can I customize?

You can customize couple names, wedding date, time, venue, invitation message, font styles, color themes, and background patterns. Preview and export for printing after generation.

Can I export the invitation?

Supports browser printing and PDF export. Click the print button in the preview area to save as PDF.

Is data uploaded to servers?

No. All operations are done locally in your browser. Couple information is never uploaded to any server.

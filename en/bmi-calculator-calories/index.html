What is BMI and TDEE?

BMI = weight(kg)/height(m)², used to assess weight status. TDEE (Total Daily Energy Expenditure) is the calories needed to maintain current weight, calculated from BMR and activity level. Weight loss: reduce 500 kcal/day (≈0.5kg/week). Weight gain: add 500 kcal/day.

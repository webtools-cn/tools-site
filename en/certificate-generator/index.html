Frequently Asked Questions

How do I use the certificate generator?

Select a template → fill in the certificate details (title, recipient name, description, date) → customize colors and fonts → preview the result → click Download PNG or PDF. Everything happens in your browser — no registration required.

Does the generated certificate have a watermark?

Absolutely not. Our certificate generator is 100% free and watermark-free. All processing is done locally in your browser, and no data is ever uploaded to any server.

What download formats are available?

PNG format is available for digital sharing, social media, and standard printing. PDF format is ideal for high-quality printing and formal record-keeping. Both maintain sharp clarity.

How to Use

Green (correct position): Enter letters that Wordle shows as green in the matching position (1-5).

Yellow (wrong position): Enter the letter and which position(s) it is NOT in (comma-separated). e.g., if E is yellow and NOT in position 3, enter "E" and "3".

Gray (not in word): Enter all gray letters in one line, e.g. "abcd".

Word list: 2500+ common 5-letter English words included, covering most of the standard Wordle dictionary.

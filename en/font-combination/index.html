The Art of Font Pairing

Font pairing is one of the most critical elements in design. A good font combination conveys brand personality and enhances reading experience. Headings should be eye-catching while body text needs to be legible.

Typography is the art and technique of arranging type to make written language legible, readable, and appealing when displayed.

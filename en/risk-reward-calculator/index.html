About Risk/Reward Calculator

The Risk/Reward Ratio (R/R) measures how much potential profit you stand to gain for every dollar risked. Professional traders typically require a minimum of 1:2 or even 1:3 before entering a trade.

Combined with win rate, you can calculate Expected Value (EV = Win% × Profit - Loss% × Loss). Positive EV means the strategy is profitable in the long run.

About Retirement Corpus Calculator

How to set retirement goals?

Target 70-80% of pre-retirement income. Consider medical costs and travel. Inflation 4-6%, post-retirement returns 5-7%.

Is this calculation accurate?

This is an estimation tool. Results vary with inflation, returns, and longevity. Reassess every 3-5 years.

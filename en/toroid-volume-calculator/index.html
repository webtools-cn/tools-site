How is toroid volume calculated?

The toroid volume formula is V = 2π²Rr², where R is the major radius and r is the minor radius.

What is the difference between major and minor radius?

The major radius R is the distance from the center to the tube center, and the minor radius r is the radius of the tube itself. R must be greater than r.

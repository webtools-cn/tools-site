FAQ

What is import duty?

Import duty is a tax levied by customs on imported goods, calculated as a percentage of the dutiable (CIF) value. Rates vary by country and HS code classification.

What is an HS code?

HS (Harmonized System) codes are international product classification codes. The first 6 digits are globally standardized. Check your country's customs website or WTO tariff database for accurate rates.

How is duty calculated?

Duty = CIF Value × Duty Rate. VAT = (CIF Value + Duty) × VAT Rate. Total = Duty + VAT. Some countries may also charge excise or additional fees.

What is CIF?

CIF stands for Cost, Insurance, and Freight — the total value including the item price, shipping insurance, and freight charges. Customs typically uses CIF as the basis for duty calculation.

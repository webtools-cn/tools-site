About Child Growth Percentiles

This tool uses WHO child growth standards to evaluate height and weight development for ages 0-18. The percentile indicates how your child compares to peers of the same age and gender. The 50th percentile is average; the 3rd-97th percentile range is considered normal. Below 3rd or above 97th percentile warrants consultation with a healthcare provider. This tool is for reference only and cannot replace professional medical advice.

What is Fall Risk Assessment?

Fall Risk Assessment is a free online tool that runs entirely in your browser. No download or signup needed — enter Fall Risk Assessment and other values to get results instantly. All data is processed locally.

Use Cases

  • Self-screen fall risk for older adults (65+) before a doctor visit
  • Caregivers and family members can help track fall risk over time
  • Identify common risk factors like balance, medication, and vision issues
  • Trigger a conversation about home safety and fall prevention

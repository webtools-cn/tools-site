📖 About SWR

What is the 4% Rule?

The 4% rule was proposed by retirement planner William Bengen in 1994. The core idea: withdraw 4% of your total portfolio in the first year of retirement, then adjust for inflation each year. Based on US market data, this strategy had a 95%+ success rate over most 30-year retirement periods.

How to use this calculator?

Enter your retirement portfolio, desired withdrawal rate, inflation rate, retirement duration, and expected investment return. The calculator simulates year-by-year portfolio changes, showing withdrawal amounts, investment gains, and remaining balance.

Beyond the 4% Rule

More conservative investors may choose 3% (highly safe), while aggressive investors might use 5-6%. This calculator supports custom rates so you can explore different strategies. Note that higher rates increase the risk of depleting savings early.

Key Factors

SWR is affected by: asset allocation (stock/bond ratio), market volatility, retirement duration, inflation, and other income sources (Social Security, pensions). This calculator provides estimates — consult a financial advisor for comprehensive planning.

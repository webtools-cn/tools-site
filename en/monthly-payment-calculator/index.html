📖 About Monthly Payments

Amortized vs Equal Principal

Amortized: fixed monthly payment, higher interest initially. Equal Principal: fixed principal payment, decreasing total. Equal principal saves more interest overall.

How to Reduce Interest?

1) Shorter loan term; 2) Larger down payment; 3) Choose equal principal; 4) Extra or bi-weekly payments.

📖 About Online Stamp Maker

A free online stamp and seal creation tool — no software download required. Design professional electronic stamps directly in your browser. Supports circle, square, and oval stamp shapes with customizable text, colors, fonts, and sizes.

🎯 Use Cases

Company chops, personal signature stamps, contract seals, approval stamps, electronic document signing, and online document authorization.

❓ FAQ

Can I use generated stamps commercially?

Yes. Stamps generated with this tool have no copyright restrictions. Use downloaded images for commercial or personal purposes freely.

What stamp shapes are supported?

Circle (round), square, and oval (elliptical) stamps are supported. Switch between them using the tabs above.

Can I customize stamp colors?

Yes. Choose from preset red, blue, and black, or use the color picker for any custom color.

Can I add a logo in the center?

Yes. Circle and oval stamps support center text (e.g., "OFFICIAL", "APPROVED") or a star icon. Square stamps support a subtitle line.

Is my data uploaded to a server?

No. All stamp generation runs entirely in your browser using the HTML5 Canvas API. No data ever leaves your device.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data safe when using DNS Records Lookup?

Absolutely. Everything runs locally: the domain you enter and other values are processed on your device, never sent to a server. No data is sent to any server, so there's zero risk of data leaks. Your information stays completely under your control.

Do I need to install any software?

No. DNS Records Lookup is a web-based tool — just open it in your browser and start using it. No downloads, plugins, or extensions required.

Are there any usage limits for DNS Records Lookup?

DNS Records Lookup has no usage limits — it's free and unlimited. Since processing is done client-side, speed depends on your device. For very large files, we recommend processing in batches for the best experience.

Does DNS Records Lookup upload my data to a server?

No. DNS Records Lookup uses client-side processing — everything runs in your browser. Your data never leaves your device, and the tool works even without an internet connection.

Is DNS Records Lookup free to use?

Yes, DNS Records Lookup is completely free to use. No registration or login required — all features are available immediately.

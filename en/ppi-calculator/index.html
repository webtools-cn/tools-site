About PPI Calculator

PPI (Pixels Per Inch) measures screen sharpness. Same resolution on a smaller screen = higher PPI = sharper image. Compare display quality across devices instantly.

How to Use

  1. Enter horizontal pixels (e.g. 1920)
  2. Enter vertical pixels (e.g. 1080)
  3. Enter diagonal size (inches)
  4. Click Calculate for PPI

FAQ

What PPI is sharp?

Phone >400 PPI(Retina), monitor >100 PPI, TV >50 PPI. Human eye limit ~300 PPI at 25cm.

PPI vs DPI?

PPI = screen pixel density. DPI = dots per inch for print. Similar numbers, different concepts.

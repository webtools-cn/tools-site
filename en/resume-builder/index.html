❓ FAQ

What is ATS compatibility?

ATS (Applicant Tracking System) is software companies use to screen resumes. Our templates use standard text layout and export selectable-text PDFs, so they are parsed correctly by ATS and improve your pass rate.

How good is the exported PDF?

We use the browser's native print to produce a vector PDF — text stays sharp and selectable, the layout matches the preview exactly, and it works for both printing and ATS.

Is my data secure?

Absolutely. All data stays in your browser's local storage (localStorage) and is never uploaded to any server. It is cleared when you close the page.

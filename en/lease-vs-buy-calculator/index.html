About Lease vs Buy Calculator

Leasing offers lower monthly payments but you don't own the car. Buying costs more monthly but builds equity. This calculator compares total costs including down payment, monthly payments, insurance, and maintenance. Leasing suits those who switch cars every 3 years; buying is better for 5+ year ownership.

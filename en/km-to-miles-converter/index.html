How many miles is 1 kilometer?

1 kilometer equals approximately 0.621371 miles. To convert km to miles, multiply the kilometer value by 0.621371.

How do I convert kilometers to miles?

To convert kilometers to miles, multiply the number of kilometers by 0.621371. For example, 10 km × 0.621371 = 6.21 miles.

Is this converter accurate?

Yes, this converter uses the standard conversion factor of 1 km = 0.621371 miles, which is the internationally recognized value.

Do I need to install anything?

No, this is a free web-based tool. Simply open the page and start converting. All calculations run in your browser with no data sent to any server.

FAQ

What is RSVP speed reading?

RSVP (Rapid Serial Visual Presentation) flashes words one at a time at the same screen position, eliminating eye movement time. Research shows RSVP can reach 400-1000 WPM.

What is the best training speed?

Start at 300-400 WPM and increase gradually. Your optimal speed is when you can understand 80% of the content. Increase by 50 WPM each time you adapt. Average reading speed is 200-300 WPM.

Does speed reading really work?

Speed reading training can improve reading efficiency, but comprehension decreases as speed increases. Use high speeds for skimming and normal speeds for detailed reading. The key is finding the balance between speed and comprehension.

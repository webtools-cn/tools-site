About Paint Needed Calculator

Paint Needed Calculator is a free online tool that calculates the exact amount of paint required based on wall area, paint coverage per liter, and number of coats. It works on both mobile and desktop, with all calculations done locally in your browser — your data never leaves your device.

How to Use

  1. Enter the wall area in square meters
  2. Enter the paint coverage per liter
  3. Enter the number of coats needed

Is this tool accurate?

The calculation is based on standard mathematical formulas, providing precise and reliable results.

Do I need to download anything?

No download required — simply open the webpage and start calculating. Everything runs in your browser.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

    

      
Do I need to install any software?

      
No. Neon Text Generator is a web-based tool — just open it in your browser and start using it. No downloads, plugins, or extensions required.

    

    

      
Can I use Neon Text Generator on my phone?

      
Yes. Neon Text Generator is fully responsive and works on phones, tablets, and desktops. The interface adapts automatically to your screen size.

    

    

      
Is Neon Text Generator free to use?

      
Yes, Neon Text Generator is completely free to use. No registration or login required — all features are available immediately.

    

    

      
Is my data safe when using Neon Text Generator?

      
Absolutely. Everything runs locally: Text and Glow Color are processed on your device, never sent to a server. No data is sent to any server, so there's zero risk of data leaks. Your information stays completely under your control.

    

    

      
What text encodings are supported?

      
UTF-8 is the primary encoding. Most tools also support ASCII, UTF-16, and other common encodings.

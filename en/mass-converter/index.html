❓ FAQ

How many pounds in 1 kilogram?

1 kilogram (kg) = 2.20462 pounds (lb). Quick memory: 1 kg ≈ 2.2 lb.

How many grams in 1 ounce?

1 ounce (oz) = 28.3495 grams (g). A standard slice of bread weighs about 1 oz.

What's the difference between metric ton and short ton?

A metric ton (tonne) = 1000 kg. A US short ton = 2000 lb ≈ 907.18 kg. A UK long ton = 2240 lb ≈ 1016 kg.

What is a stone (st) in weight?

Stone (st) is a UK/Ireland body weight unit. 1 st = 14 lb = 6.35 kg. "I weigh 11 stone" means about 70 kg.

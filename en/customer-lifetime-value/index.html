❓ FAQ

What is Customer Lifetime Value (CLV/LTV)?

CLV is the total profit a customer contributes over the entire business relationship. Simple formula: CLV = ARPU × Gross Margin / Churn Rate. Example: ARPU $100/mo, 80% margin, 5% monthly churn → CLV = $100 × 80% / 5% = $1,600.

What is a healthy LTV:CAC ratio?

SaaS industry standard is LTV:CAC ≥ 3:1 for a healthy business. Below 3 suggests CAC is too high or retention too poor. Above 5 may mean under-investing in growth. The sweet spot is 3-5x.

What is a good churn rate?

For SaaS, monthly churn below 2-3% is considered healthy (24-36% annually). Top companies stay under 1%. B2B SaaS typically has lower churn than B2C. Consumer subscription products at 5-8% monthly churn are still normal.

What is the difference between CLV and CAC?

CLV is total profit per customer; CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost) is cost to acquire one. CLV is a revenue-side metric; CAC is cost-side. Together they measure business sustainability: CLV must exceed CAC, typically targeting LTV:CAC ≥ 3.

How to improve CLV?

① Reduce churn (better product/customer success); ② Boost ARPU (price increases/upsells/cross-sells); ③ Improve margins (optimize cost structure). Reducing churn has the biggest impact since it sits in the denominator.

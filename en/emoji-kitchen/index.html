📖 What can the Emoji Mixer do?

The Emoji Mixer is a creative online tool that combines two different emojis into a brand new one. With real-time Canvas rendering and multiple blend modes, you can create unique and fun emojis for chats, social media, and creative design.

Key Features

  • Two-emoji mixing: pick any two emojis to blend together
  • 6 blend modes: Normal, Multiply, Screen, Overlay, Difference, Color Dodge
  • Adjustable parameters: fine-tune opacity and zoom in real time
  • Random mix: one-click random fun combinations
  • Export: download as PNG or copy to clipboard

How to Use

  1. Click the left or right emoji display to select the side you want to edit
  2. Pick an emoji from the panel below
  3. Adjust the blend mode, opacity, and zoom
  4. Check the combined result on the right
  5. Click "Download PNG" or "Copy" to save the result

Use Cases

1. Chat stickers

Create unique emoji combos that stand out in chats, more fun than ordinary ones.

2. Social media

Design distinctive emojis for posts, comments, and profiles to boost engagement.

3. Creative design

Explore the artistic possibilities of emojis and discover unexpected visual effects.

Extras

Emoji Kitchen is a Gboard feature Google launched in 2020 that lets users combine two emojis into a new one. The official version uses pre-made images with thousands of hand-crafted combinations. This tool uses the Canvas API's globalCompositeOperation to blend in real time, supporting any combination, though results may differ from the official version. Blend modes come from image compositing theory — different modes produce different visual effects.

📖 FAQ

What is the Emoji Mixer?

The Emoji Mixer is an online tool that combines two different emojis into a brand new creative one. Inspired by Google's Emoji Kitchen, it overlaps and blends the visual elements of two emojis to create unique symbols.

Is the Emoji Mixer Google Emoji Kitchen?

This tool is inspired by Google Emoji Kitchen but works differently. Google Emoji Kitchen uses pre-made composite images, while this tool blends two emojis in real time using Canvas and supports any combination. Results may differ from Google's official version.

Can I download the combined emoji?

Yes. The combined emoji can be downloaded as a PNG image or copied to the clipboard. The output is 128x128 pixels, suitable for chats and social media.

Which emoji combinations are supported?

Any two emojis can be combined. Pairing a face emoji (such as 😀😍🥺) with an item emoji (such as 🔥❤️🌈) usually gives the best results. You can freely choose the left and right emoji.

Are the combined emojis copyright protected?

Emojis follow the Unicode standard, and each platform's emoji designs are copyright protected. This tool uses your system's built-in emoji rendering; combined results are for personal entertainment and non-commercial use. Please seek legal advice for commercial use.

FAQ

How is ceiling fan size determined?

Fan size is based on room area: ≤75 sq ft → 29-36", 76-144 sq ft → 36-42", 144-225 sq ft → 44-50", 225-400 sq ft → 50-54", 400+ sq ft → 56" or multiple fans.

Are more blades better?

Not necessarily. 3-4 blades work well for smaller rooms with higher RPM. 5-blade fans deliver a softer breeze for larger spaces. Blade count affects aesthetics and noise more than airflow.

What is the ideal ceiling fan mounting height?

Blades should be 7-9 feet above the floor. For high ceilings, use an extended downrod to bring the fan into the optimal range.

DC vs AC motor — which to choose?

DC motors use 50%+ less energy, run quieter, and offer finer speed control. They cost more upfront. Ideal for bedrooms. AC motors are fine for living rooms and outdoor areas.

📖 About Safe Withdrawal Rates

The 4% Rule

Originating from Bill Bengen (1994) and the Trinity Study (1998): withdrawing 4% of portfolio in year 1, adjusted for inflation thereafter, has ~95% success rate over 30 years.

Modern Adjustments

Some experts recommend 3-3.5% for extra safety given longer life expectancies. This calculator lets you test any withdrawal rate.

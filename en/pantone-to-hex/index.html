❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use Pantone To Hex on my phone?

Yes. Pantone To Hex is fully responsive and works on phones, tablets, and desktops. The interface adapts automatically to your screen size.

Is Pantone To Hex free to use?

Yes, Pantone To Hex is completely free to use. No registration or login required — all features are available immediately.

Is my data safe when using Pantone To Hex?

Absolutely. Everything runs locally: Pantone Code and other values are processed on your device, never sent to a server. No data is sent to any server, so there's zero risk of data leaks. Your information stays completely under your control.

Does Pantone To Hex upload my data to a server?

No. Pantone To Hex uses client-side processing — everything runs in your browser. Your data never leaves your device, and the tool works even without an internet connection.

Are the color values accurate?

Color values are based on standard color space conversion algorithms with high precision. Display may vary slightly depending on monitor calibration.

Are converted files compatible with other software?

Yes. Converted files follow standard format specifications and are compatible with mainstream software and platforms.

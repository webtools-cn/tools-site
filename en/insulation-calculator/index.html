📖 Insulation Calculator Guide

How much insulation do I need?

Follow these steps to estimate your insulation requirements:

  • 1. Find the area: Area = length × width (or enter it directly)
  • 2. Add waste: Total coverage = area × (1 + waste%)
  • 3. Count bags: Bags = total coverage ÷ coverage per bag (round up)
  • 4. Estimate cost: Cost = bags × price per bag

Common Insulation Materials

  • 🟦 Fiberglass batts: Most popular and cost-effective. R-value ≈ 2.2–2.7 per inch. Keep dry during installation.
  • 🟧 Mineral wool: Excellent fire resistance. R-value ≈ 3.0–3.3 per inch. Denser than fiberglass.
  • 🟩 Cellulose: Eco-friendly, made from recycled paper. R-value ≈ 3.2–3.8 per inch. Used as loose-fill in attics.
  • 🟪 Spray foam: Best air sealing. Open-cell ≈ R-3.5/in, closed-cell ≈ R-6.0/in. Professional installation recommended.
  • ⬜ Rigid foam board: Great moisture resistance. R-value ≈ 4.0–6.5 per inch. Ideal for exterior wall sheathing.

Waste Factor Explained

Insulation must be cut to fit around pipes, wires, and irregular spaces, so some material is lost. Use 10% for standard rectangular walls. Use 15% for areas with many penetrations or unusual shapes. Spray foam has minimal waste — 5% is usually enough.

Multi-Area Projects

If your project spans multiple areas with different materials or R-values (e.g., exterior walls, attic, and crawlspace), use the "Add Area to Total" button after each calculation. The summary section will combine all areas for a grand total.

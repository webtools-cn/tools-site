How Hourly to Salary Conversion Works

The formula is straightforward: Annual Salary = Hourly Rate x Hours per Week x Weeks per Year. For example, at $25/hour, working 40 hours/week for 52 weeks/year: Annual = 25 x 40 x 52 = $52,000.

Key considerations:

  • Monthly Pay: Monthly ≈ Weekly x 4.33 (52 weeks / 12 months), not simply x4 which underestimates by ~8%
  • Overtime: Many hourly workers earn 1.5x overtime rates. This calculator uses standard hours only.
  • Benefits Gap: Salaried positions typically include paid time off, health insurance, and retirement contributions worth 30-40% of base pay. Hourly workers may not get these.

Typical Scenarios

  • Full-Time 40hx52w: $25/h -> $52,000/year
  • Part-Time 25hx50w: $20/h -> $25,000/year
  • 996 Mode 60hx52w: $30/h -> $93,600/year

FAQ

How much is $25 per hour annually?

At standard full-time (40h/week × 52 weeks/year), $25/h = $25 × 40 × 52 = $52,000/year. Monthly is about $4,333. After-tax depends on your location and tax bracket.

How do I convert annual salary to hourly rate?

Formula: Hourly = Annual ÷ (Hours per Week × Weeks per Year). Example: $100,000/year ÷ (40h × 52w) ≈ $48.08/h.

What's the difference between part-time and full-time pay?

Part-time (25h/week × 50 weeks) at $20/h = $25,000/year. At the same hourly rate, full-time earns about 60% more. But part-time offers flexibility that hourly rates don't capture.

Is monthly pay weekly × 4 or × 4.33?

Use × 4.33 (52 weeks ÷ 12 months). Using × 4 underestimates by ~8%. When negotiating job offers, an offer of $4,000/month = $48,000/year, not some confused weekly × 4 × 52 math.

Does salary include benefits? Hourly vs salary total compensation?

Salary positions typically include paid time off, health insurance, and retirement contributions worth 30-40% of base pay. Hourly workers may not get these, making the gap larger than the raw numbers suggest.

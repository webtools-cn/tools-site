✨ Fancy Text Generator

Convert plain text into bold, italic, script, double-struck, circled, and other Unicode styles.

📖 Supported Platforms

These styles use Unicode Mathematical Alphanumeric Symbols and work on most social platforms (Twitter/X, Instagram, Facebook, Discord, WhatsApp, etc.). Note: Some styles lack mappings for non-Latin characters — those will appear as-is.

What is Fancy Text Generator?

Fancy Text Generator is a free online tool that runs entirely in your browser. No installation, no registration, just open and use. All data is processed locally.

Use Cases

  • Quick calculations during daily work
  • Verifying results in study or teaching
  • Mobile use without installing apps
  • Comparing results across parameters

Key Features

  • Completely Free: No registration, no limits
  • Privacy Safe: Data never leaves your browser
  • Cross-Platform: Any modern browser
  • Zero Dependencies: Open and use

💡 How It Works

LEDs need a current-limiting resistor to prevent burnout. Ohm's Law: R = (Vsupply - VLED × N) / I

Typical LED Forward Voltages

  • 🔴 Red LED: 1.8-2.2V
  • 🟢 Green LED: 2.0-3.0V
  • 🟡 Yellow LED: 1.8-2.2V
  • 🔵 Blue LED: 3.0-3.4V
  • ⚪ White LED: 3.0-3.4V
  • 🟣 Infrared LED: 1.2-1.6V

Important Notes

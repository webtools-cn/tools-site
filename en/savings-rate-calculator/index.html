📖 About Savings Rate

Savings rate is one of the most important metrics in personal finance — it directly determines how quickly you can achieve financial independence. The formula is: Savings Rate = (Income - Expenses) / Income × 100%. FIRE movement advocates typically aim for 50%+ savings rates to achieve financial independence within 10-15 years.

Generally: 20% is the bare minimum, 30% is good, and 50%+ is excellent. Every 10 percentage point increase in savings rate can shave years off your time to financial independence. This tool not only calculates your savings rate but also provides FIRE timeline estimates and expense breakdown analysis to help you optimize. All calculations run locally — your financial data stays private.

About Free ToolBase

Free ToolBase is a collection of 3400+ free online tools covering 61 categories — from JSON formatters and PDF converters to financial calculators and health trackers. Every tool runs entirely in your browser. No installation, no sign-up, no server uploads. Just open and use.

We're a small team of developers who believe great tools should be free and private. We hand-code every tool's calculation logic — no AI-generated filler content. Your data stays on your device; we don't even have a server to send it to.

All code is open source at github.com/webtools-cn/tools-site. Found a bug? Want a tool? Open an issue or email us: dexshuang@google.com.

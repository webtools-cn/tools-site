50/30/20 Rule Guide

50% Needs: Rent, utilities, groceries, insurance, minimum debt payments — essentials you can't avoid.

30% Wants: Dining out, entertainment, hobbies, subscriptions — things you enjoy but don't need.

20% Savings: Emergency fund, retirement, investments, extra debt payments — building your future.

What is a Monthly Budget Calculator?

A monthly budget calculator helps you track income against expenses, visualize spending categories, and plan savings. Using the 50/30/20 rule as a guideline, it shows whether you're overspending on wants or undersaving for the future.

How to Use

Enter your monthly take-home pay, then add expenses by category. The tool calculates totals, shows a visual bar chart breakdown, and highlights your savings rate and adherence to the 50/30/20 rule.

FAQ

Is my data safe? All calculations run locally in your browser. Data never uploads to any server.

What's included in "needs" vs "wants"? Needs are essential living expenses; wants are discretionary. The tool auto-categorizes based on your selections.

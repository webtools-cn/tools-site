    ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

    What is a chronotype?
    A chronotype is your body's natural preference for sleep and wake times. It's determined by your circadian rhythm and influenced by genetics. Understanding your chronotype can help you optimize your work, study, and rest schedule.
    What do the four animal types mean?
    🐻 Bear (~55% of people): Follow the sun, most active during daylight hours.
    🦁 Lion (~15% of people): Early risers, peak energy in the morning.
    🐺 Wolf (~15% of people): Night owls, most creative in the evening.
    🐬 Dolphin (~10% of people): Light sleepers, highly intelligent but prone to insomnia.
    Is this quiz scientifically based?
    This quiz is based on Dr. Michael Breus's chronotype classification theory and incorporates elements from validated sleep research questionnaires. While not a clinical diagnostic tool, it provides a reliable indicator of your biological clock preference.
    Can I change my chronotype?
    Chronotypes are largely genetic, but you can make adjustments through light exposure timing, consistent sleep schedules, and reducing blue light before bed. However, completely changing your chronotype is very difficult — it's better to work with your natural rhythm rather than against it.

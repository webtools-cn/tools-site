About Device Info Viewer

Free online device information viewer. View browser, OS, CPU, memory, network, battery details with one click. No signup required.

FAQ

What information can I view?

Browser name/version, OS, CPU cores, RAM, network type, battery status, language, timezone, Cookie/LocalStorage status, and more.

Is my privacy safe?

Completely safe. All information is displayed only in your browser. Some info (like battery) requires your permission.

Why do some items show 'Unavailable'?

Certain APIs (battery, network info) require browser support. If unsupported or permission denied, they show as unavailable.

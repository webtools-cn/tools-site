FAQ

What is Title Case?

Title Case is a capitalization style where the first letter of each major word is capitalized, while articles, conjunctions, and short prepositions remain lowercase. Different style guides have different rules about which words stay lowercase.

What is the difference between AP and Chicago style?

AP style: prepositions of 3 letters or fewer are lowercase, all conjunctions are lowercase. Chicago style: all prepositions regardless of length are lowercase. For example, "Through" is capitalized in AP but lowercase in Chicago.

When should I use Title Case?

Book/article titles, paper titles, news headlines, button text, navigation menus. Academic papers typically require MLA or APA style.

How many grams in a kilogram?

1 kg = 1000 g = 1,000,000 mg = 0.001 metric tons ≈ 2.2046 lb. This is defined by the International System of Units (SI).

Should I use grams or kilograms for cooking?

Home cooking typically uses grams (g) since most ingredients fall in the 1-500g range. Kilograms are better for bulk baking or commercial kitchens.

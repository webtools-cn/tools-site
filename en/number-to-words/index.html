How It Works

English Words

Converts numbers to their English word representation, e.g. 12345 → twelve thousand three hundred forty-five. Ideal for check writing and legal documents.

Chinese Financial Uppercase

Uses anti-forgery Chinese characters (壹贰叁肆伍陆柒捌玖拾佰仟万亿) with yuan/jiao/fen units. Standard format for invoices, contracts, and financial documents in China.

Chinese Uppercase / Lowercase

Chinese uppercase uses 一二三四五六七八九十百千万亿 units. Lowercase uses Arabic numerals with Chinese unit labels.

FAQ

How large a number can I convert?

Supports numbers from 0 up to 9999 trillion (10^12) for integers, with up to 2 decimal places for financial amounts.

What is Chinese financial uppercase?

It uses special anti-forgery characters (壹贰叁肆伍陆柒捌玖) with yuan/jiao/fen units, standard for invoices and contracts in China.

Are negative numbers supported?

Yes. Negative numbers are prefixed with "minus" in English and "负" in Chinese.

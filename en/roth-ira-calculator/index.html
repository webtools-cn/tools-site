A Roth IRA is one of the most popular retirement accounts in the U.S. — contribute after-tax dollars and enjoy completely tax-free withdrawals in retirement

A Roth IRA (Individual Retirement Arrangement) allows individuals to contribute after-tax income annually, with tax-free growth inside the account and tax-free withdrawals after age 59½. The 2025 contribution limit is $7,000 ($8,000 if age 50+). This tool helps you calculate the long-term compound growth of your Roth IRA.

🏦 About 401k Retirement Savings

What is a 401k plan? The 401k is the most popular retirement savings plan in the US, offered by employers, allowing employees to contribute directly from their pre-tax salary. The individual contribution limit for 2024 is $23,000 (with an additional $7,500 catch-up for those 50 and older).

Employer Match: Most employers will match employee contributions proportionally. A common plan is a 50% match on the first 6% of salary. This is "free money," and it is highly recommended to contribute at least enough to max out the match.

Traditional vs. Roth: Traditional 401k uses pre-tax income for contributions, and taxes are paid upon withdrawal; Roth 401k uses after-tax income for contributions, and withdrawals are tax-free. Which one to choose depends on a comparison of your current tax rate and your expected retirement tax rate.

The Power of Compound Interest: Starting early is key. If you start saving $500 per month at age 30 with a 7% annual return, you could have over $610,000 by age 60. Starting 10 years later could cut this amount nearly in half.

Suggested Strategies: ① Contribute at least enough to get the full employer match; ② Increase your contribution rate by 1-2% each year; ③ Use annual raises to increase contributions; ④ Avoid early withdrawals to prevent penalties.

Split Tip Calculator — Split the Check Effortlessly

The Split Tip Calculator helps you quickly figure out how much each person owes when splitting a restaurant bill. Whether dining with friends, on a business lunch, or traveling with a group, just enter the bill amount, choose a tip percentage, and specify the number of people to get an instant, accurate split.

Supports multiple currencies (USD, EUR, GBP, CNY, JPY, KRW) with common tip presets (10%/15%/18%/20%) and custom rates. Large, easy-to-read results make it simple to show your dining companions. All calculations run locally in your browser — your bill data is never sent to any server.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I use the split tip calculator?

Enter the bill amount, select a tip percentage (10%/15%/18%/20% or custom), enter the number of people, and click Calculate to see the tip amount, total, and each person's share.

How much tip should I give?

In the US, 15-20% is standard; for excellent service, 20%+ is common. In Europe, 10-15% is typical. In Japan, tipping is not customary. Always consider local customs and service quality.

Which currencies are supported?

The calculator supports US Dollar (USD), Euro (EUR), British Pound (GBP), Chinese Yuan (CNY), Japanese Yen (JPY), and Korean Won (KRW). Select your currency and results will display with the appropriate symbol.

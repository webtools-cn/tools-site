📖 How to Use

  • Formula: C₁ × V₁ = C₂ × V₂ (stock concentration × stock volume = final concentration × final volume)
  • Enter any three values, leave one blank, and click Calculate
  • Molarity mode: units in mol/L and mmol/L, ideal for chemistry lab prep
  • Percent mode: units in %, for weight/volume (w/v%) dilutions
  • Custom units mode: flexible units for ppm, mg/mL, or any concentration
  • Results include the dilution factor and solvent volume to add
  • All calculations run in your browser — no data sent to any server

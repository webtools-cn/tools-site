❓ FAQ

How do I track my total subscription spending?

Add each subscription with its name, price, and billing cycle. The calculator automatically totals your monthly, annual, and daily spending. Popular services are pre-loaded for one-click adding.

Does this tool save my data?

No. All calculations run in your browser. Your subscription data is never uploaded and is cleared when you refresh the page.

How can I reduce my subscription costs?

Review subscriptions regularly and cancel unused ones. Annual plans typically save 15-20% versus monthly. Share family plans with household members. Look for free alternatives.

About Food Calorie Lookup

Search and compare the calorie content and macronutrients (protein, fat, carbs) of more than 200 common foods, shown per 100g. Filter by category — fruit, vegetable, meat, seafood, grain, dairy, snack, drink, and fast food — or type a keyword such as "chicken", "rice", or "apple". A built-in bar chart highlights the top 12 matches for quick comparison, and results can be copied with one click. All lookups run locally in your browser: nothing is uploaded or stored.

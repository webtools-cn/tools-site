❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data safe when using Tiff To Jpg?

Absolutely. Everything runs locally: Output Quality and other values are processed on your device, never sent to a server. No data is sent to any server, so there's zero risk of data leaks. Your information stays completely under your control.

Are there any usage limits for Tiff To Jpg?

Tiff To Jpg has no usage limits — it's free and unlimited. Since processing is done client-side, speed depends on your device. For very large files, we recommend processing in batches for the best experience.

Is Tiff To Jpg free to use?

Yes, Tiff To Jpg is completely free to use. No registration or login required — all features are available immediately.

Can I use Tiff To Jpg on my phone?

Yes. Tiff To Jpg is fully responsive and works on phones, tablets, and desktops. The interface adapts automatically to your screen size.

Does Tiff To Jpg upload my data to a server?

No. Tiff To Jpg uses client-side processing — everything runs in your browser. Your data never leaves your device, and the tool works even without an internet connection.

Do I need to install any software?

No. Tiff To Jpg is a web-based tool — just open it in your browser and start using it. No downloads, plugins, or extensions required.

What image formats are supported?

Common formats including PNG, JPEG, WebP, GIF, BMP, and SVG are supported. The exact formats depend on the specific tool.

Will image quality decrease after processing?

For lossless formats (like PNG), quality is preserved. For lossy formats (like JPEG), you can choose the output quality.

📖 Use Cases

Image watermarks are commonly used for copyright protection:

  • 🛡️ Copyright Protection — Add copyright marks to your work
  • 🏢 Brand Promotion — Add logo watermarks to boost brand visibility
  • 📸 Photo Signing — Add signatures to photography work
  • 🔒 Anti-Theft — Watermark product images to prevent competitor theft
  • 📱 Social Media — Add personal info to shared images

❓ FAQ

Will my images be uploaded to a server?

No. All image processing is done locally in your browser using the Canvas API. Your images never leave your device.

Can I add an image watermark?

Yes. Switch to the "Image Watermark" tab and upload a logo or other image as your watermark, with adjustable size and position.

What image formats are supported?

JPG/JPEG, PNG, WebP, and BMP formats are supported. PNG is recommended for best quality downloads.

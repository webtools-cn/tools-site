❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Yes, Readability Score Calculator is completely free to use. No registration or login required — all features are available immediately.

Does Readability Score Calculator upload my data to a server?

No. Readability Score Calculator uses client-side processing — everything runs in your browser. Your data never leaves your device, and the tool works even without an internet connection.

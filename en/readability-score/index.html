FAQ

Q1: What does Flesch Reading Ease score mean?

Flesch Reading Ease scores range from 0-100, higher means easier to read. 90-100 = very easy (5th grade level), 60-70 = standard (8-9th grade level), 30-50 = college level, 0-30 = professional. Recommended target for general content is 60-70.

Q2: What are the differences between readability algorithms?

Flesch-Kincaid Grade Level is based on sentence length and syllable count, outputting a grade level. Gunning Fog also uses sentence length and complex words (3+ syllables), suitable for professional writing. SMOG Index is more conservative, suited for medical text. Coleman-Liau is based on character count, no syllable counting needed. Using multiple algorithms together gives a more complete picture.

Q3: How can I improve text readability?

Use shorter sentences (average 15-20 words), choose common words, reduce passive voice, one idea per paragraph, use lists and headings to structure text. Target Flesch score of 60+, Flesch-Kincaid grade level below 8.

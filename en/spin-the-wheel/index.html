Edit Options (one per line)

How to Use

Edit Options: Enter your options in the text box on the right, one per line. Supports 2-20 options.

Spin: Click the "Spin" button and the wheel will randomly rotate and stop on an option.

Presets: Click preset buttons to quickly load common option sets (Yes/No, Dinner, Raffle, Colors, Dice).

How it works: Uses Canvas to draw the wheel, with random angles and easing animation to simulate a realistic spinning effect. Results are completely random.

Use Cases

Daily decisions: "What's for dinner?" Put options on the wheel and let fate decide.

Classroom interaction: Teachers can randomly pick students to answer questions — fair and fun.

Event raffles: Annual party raffles, promotional events — adds excitement and ceremony.

Games: Board games, truth or dare random selection.

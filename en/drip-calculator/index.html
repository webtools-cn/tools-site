What is DRIP?

DRIP (Dividend Reinvestment Plan) automatically uses dividend payouts to purchase additional shares. Through compounding, dividend reinvestment can dramatically increase long-term total returns. Price growth + dividend reinvestment + regular contributions = triple compounding growth. This is the wealth-building strategy used by Warren Buffett and other master investors.

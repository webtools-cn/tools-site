Formula

Break-Even Qty = Fixed Costs ÷ (Price - Variable Cost per Unit)

Contribution Margin = Price - Variable Cost per Unit

CM Ratio = Contribution Margin ÷ Price × 100%

Break-Even Revenue = Break-Even Qty × Price

FAQ

What is the break-even point?

The break-even point is the sales volume where total revenue equals total cost — the business neither makes a profit nor incurs a loss.

Fixed costs vs variable costs?

Fixed costs stay constant regardless of output (rent, equipment). Variable costs change with production (raw materials, packaging).

What is break-even analysis used for?

It helps determine the minimum sales needed to cover costs, evaluate pricing strategies, and assess new project feasibility.

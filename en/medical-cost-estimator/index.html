How to Use

1. Select the type of medical service (Outpatient/Inpatient/Surgery/Dental/Vision)

2. Enter the expected number of visits or hospital days

3. Fill in the cost per visit, medication costs, and insurance coverage percentage

4. Click "Calculate" to see the cost breakdown before and after insurance

Use Cases

Scenario 1: Pre-Visit Budget Planning

Before visiting a doctor, use this tool to estimate potential costs and prepare funds in advance to avoid financial stress.

Scenario 2: Insurance Plan Comparison

Compare out-of-pocket amounts under different insurance coverage rates to choose the optimal plan.

Scenario 3: Annual Healthcare Budget

Estimate annual medical expenses based on family members' visit frequency and plan your household budget wisely.

Additional Information

Medical costs typically include registration fees, examination fees, medication, treatment, and hospitalization costs. Insurance reimbursement usually has deductible and cap limits, and rates vary by region and insurance type. This tool provides basic estimates; please refer to actual hospital charges and local insurance policies for precise figures.

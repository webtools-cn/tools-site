Are my images uploaded to any server?
No. All processing happens entirely in your browser. Your images never leave your device and are never uploaded to any server.
What image formats are supported?
PNG, JPG, JPEG, and WebP formats are supported. Clear subject images work best for background removal.
How good is the background removal quality?
Uses Canvas color analysis for background detection. Works best on images with solid color backgrounds. Complex backgrounds may need tolerance adjustments.
Does it preserve transparency?
Output is PNG format with full alpha channel support. Semi-transparent areas like hair edges are preserved.
Does it work on mobile devices?
Yes. Fully responsive design works on smartphones, tablets, and desktops. No app installation needed.

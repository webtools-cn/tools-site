❓ FAQ
What are the 12 Chinese zodiac animals?
Rat, Ox, Tiger, Rabbit, Dragon, Snake, Horse, Goat, Monkey, Rooster, Dog, Pig. They cycle every 12 years.
What about the Five Elements?
Each zodiac year is also associated with one of the Five Elements (Metal, Wood, Water, Fire, Earth), determined by the Heavenly Stem in a 10-year cycle.
When does the zodiac year start?
The Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, starting at Chinese New Year (Spring Festival), not January 1st. This tool uses the Gregorian year for quick reference.

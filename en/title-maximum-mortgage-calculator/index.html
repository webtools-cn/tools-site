About Maximum Mortgage Calculator

Want to buy a home but unsure how much you can borrow? Banks use DTI as the key metric. Enter income and debts for maximum loan estimates.

How to Use

  1. Enter monthly income
  2. Enter existing monthly debts
  3. Click for maximum loan amount

FAQ

What DTI is required?

Mortgage DTI <43%, preferred <36%. Payments + debts <43% of income.

Does down payment affect loan?

Yes! Higher down=lower loan but better rates. 20% min to avoid PMI.

What is Maximum Mortgage Calculator?

Maximum Mortgage Calculator is a free online tool that runs entirely in your browser. No download or signup needed — enter Monthly Income and Monthly Income 2 to get results instantly. All data is processed locally.

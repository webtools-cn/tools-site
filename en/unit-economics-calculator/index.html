📖 Unit Economics Guide

  • Contribution Margin = Price - Variable Cost (exl. CAC)
  • Contribution Margin % = Contribution Margin ÷ Price × 100%
  • LTV = Contribution Margin × Orders/Month × Lifetime
  • LTV:CAC Healthy > 3x; each $1 spent acquires $3+ in value
  • Break-Even Orders = (CAC + Fixed Cost ÷ Orders/Month) ÷ Contribution Margin

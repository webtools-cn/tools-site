📖 KPI Formula Reference

Completion Rate = Actual ÷ Target × 100%

Gap = Target - Actual

Achievement Rate = Completion Rate（SameCompletion Rate）

YoY Growth Rate = (Current Period - Last Year Same Period) ÷ Last Year Same Period × 100%

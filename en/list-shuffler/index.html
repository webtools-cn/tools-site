List Shuffler

Free online List Shuffler - randomly shuffle list items, one per line, with instant copy support.

What is List Shuffler?

List Shuffler is a free online tool that runs entirely in your browser. No download or signup needed — enter Input List and Shuffled Result to get results instantly. All data is processed locally.

Use Cases

  • Randomize team assignments or group orders
  • Shuffle quiz questions for varied practice
  • Randomize playlist or reading list order
  • Quick random selection for decision making

Key Features

  • Completely Free: No registration, no limits
  • Privacy Safe: Data never leaves your browser
  • Cross-Platform: Any modern browser
  • Zero Dependencies: Open and use

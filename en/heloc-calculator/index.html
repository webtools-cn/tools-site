FAQ

What is a HELOC?

A HELOC (Home Equity Line of Credit) is a revolving credit line secured by your home equity. You can borrow during the draw period (interest-only payments), then repay principal + interest during the repayment period.

What does LTV ratio mean?

LTV (Loan-to-Value) = total loan amount / home value. Most lenders allow a maximum LTV of 80-85%, meaning you can borrow up to 80-85% of your home equity.

How are draw period payments calculated?

During the draw period, you typically pay interest only: monthly payment = balance × APR ÷ 12. You can pay down principal anytime to reduce payments.

How are repayment period payments calculated?

Repayment uses standard amortization: monthly = principal × monthly rate × (1+r)^n ÷ ((1+r)^n - 1), fully paying off the balance over the repayment term.

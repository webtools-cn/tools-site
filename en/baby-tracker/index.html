What is Baby Tracker?

Baby Tracker is a free online tool that runs entirely in your browser. No download or signup needed — record feedings, naps, diaper changes, and growth data to get instant daily summaries. All data is processed locally.

Use Cases

  • Track daily feeding amounts and formulas for newborns
  • Log nap and sleep durations to spot patterns
  • Record height and weight to follow growth trends
  • Share a clean daily summary with caregivers

Key Features

  • Completely Free: No registration, no limits
  • Privacy Safe: Data never leaves your browser
  • Growth Charts: Visual height and weight trends
  • Zero Dependencies: Open and use

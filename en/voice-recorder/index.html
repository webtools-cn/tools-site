What is Voice Recorder?

Voice Recorder is a free online tool that runs entirely in your browser. No download or signup needed — record audio from your microphone, view real-time waveform, and download as WAV file. All data is processed locally.

Use Cases

  • Record voice memos and interviews on the go
  • Capture audio for podcasts and content creation
  • Record meeting notes and lectures for later review
  • Create voiceovers for videos and presentations

Key Features

  • Completely Free: No registration, no limits
  • Privacy Safe: Data never leaves your browser
  • Real-time Waveform: Visualize your audio while recording
  • Multiple Quality Settings: Choose from 44100Hz, 22050Hz, or 11025Hz

❓ FAQ
What is a leap year?
Gregorian rule: A year divisible by 4 but not by 100 is a leap year, OR divisible by 400 is also a leap year.
Why do we need leap years?
Earth's orbit takes ~365.2422 days. Leap years compensate for the extra ~0.2422 days per year.
When is the next leap year?

About Tons to Pounds Converter

Tons to Pounds Converter is a free online tool. Convert between metric tons, short tons (US), long tons (UK), and pounds. Essential for international trade, shipping, and freight.

How to Use

  1. Enter the metric tons value (1 metric ton = 1000 kg)
  2. Click "Calculate" to see conversions to pounds, short tons, and long tons

What is the difference between metric, short, and long tons?

Metric ton = 1000kg = 2204.62 lb. Short ton (US) = 2000 lb ≈ 907 kg. Long ton (UK) = 2240 lb ≈ 1016 kg. International trade uses metric tons.

Which ton does my country use?

The metric ton (tonne) is the international standard. The US uses short tons domestically; the UK has mostly switched to metric but long tons appear in some contexts.

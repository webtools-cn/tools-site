📖 About Burn Rate

  • Gross Burn: Total monthly cash expenses
  • Net Burn: Monthly Expenses - Monthly Revenue, i.e. net cash consumed per month
  • Runway: Number of months until cash runs out at current burn rate
  • Healthy runway is typically >18 months; under 6 months is a red flag
  • Including revenue growth rate provides a more accurate runway estimate

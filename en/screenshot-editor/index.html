How to Use

This online screenshot editor is simple yet powerful. Here's how to get started:

Upload a Screenshot

Click the upload area to select an image file, or drag and drop an image. Even easier: press Ctrl+V (Mac: Cmd+V) to paste a screenshot directly from your clipboard. Supports JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP, and more.

Choose an Annotation Tool

  • Rectangle: Drag to draw a rectangle — perfect for highlighting or boxing important content
  • Arrow: Drag from start to end point to draw an arrow pointing to key areas
  • Text: Click anywhere on the screenshot to add a text label
  • Blur: Drag to select an area and apply a mosaic/pixelation effect — ideal for hiding phone numbers, addresses, or personal details
  • Draw: Freehand drawing for custom annotations or circling content

Customize Appearance

Before drawing, use the color picker, stroke width slider, and font size dropdown in the toolbar. Stroke width ranges from 1-20px, font sizes from 14-48px. Common colors: red for emphasis, yellow for highlights, blue and green for notes.

Undo & Clear

Click Undo (or press Ctrl+Z) to revert your last annotation. Click Clear to remove all annotations while keeping the original screenshot intact.

Use Cases

Bug Reports & Tech Support: Annotate screenshots with arrows pointing to issues and text describing the problem. Send clear, professional bug reports to your development team or support staff.

Tutorials & Documentation: Create step-by-step guides by highlighting buttons with rectangles, indicating flow with arrows, and adding explanatory text. Export high-quality annotated screenshots for documentation, blog posts, or presentations.

Privacy Protection: Before sharing screenshots, use the Blur tool to obscure phone numbers, ID numbers, bank details, home addresses, and other sensitive personal information. The mosaic effect ensures information cannot be recovered.

Design Reviews & Feedback: Designers and product managers can mark up screenshots with specific feedback — arrows pointing to elements, text describing changes needed. Much more efficient than text-only communication.

FAQ

Will editing reduce image quality?

No. This tool renders annotations as an overlay on the original image at full resolution. Export as PNG (lossless) or JPEG (adjustable quality).

What image formats are supported?

All browser-supported formats: JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP, and more. You can also paste screenshots directly from your clipboard (Ctrl+V).

Are my images uploaded to a server?

Never. This tool is 100% client-side. All processing happens in your browser via Canvas — your image data never leaves your device.

How do I blur sensitive information?

Select the Blur tool and drag a rectangle over the area you want to hide. The tool applies a pixelated mosaic effect — perfect for obscuring phone numbers, addresses, or personal details.

Can I undo my last action?

Yes. Click the Undo button or press Ctrl+Z to revert your most recent annotation. Multi-step undo is supported.

Can I paste a screenshot from clipboard?

Yes! Simply press Ctrl+V (Mac: Cmd+V) to paste a screenshot directly into the editor. You can also drag-and-drop or click to upload.

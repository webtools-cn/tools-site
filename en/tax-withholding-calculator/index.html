About Tax Withholding Calculator

The Tax Withholding Calculator is a free online tool that computes your net salary after deducting income tax and social insurance contributions. Useful for employees checking take-home pay, job seekers evaluating offers, and HR professionals planning compensation.

How to Use

  1. Enter your gross salary
  2. Set deduction rates (social insurance, housing fund)
  3. Click "Calculate" to see net pay

Is this tool accurate?

The calculations are based on standard mathematical formulas with reliable results.

Do I need to download anything?

No download needed — open the page and use it instantly. All calculations run locally in your browser.

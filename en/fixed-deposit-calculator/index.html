💡 Deposit Tips

Compound effect: monthly compounding yields ~0.1-0.2% more effective annual return than annual. More frequent = more gains.
Ladder strategy: split large deposits into multiple tenures for liquidity + high rates. E.g., split 100k into 1/2/3/4/5-year deposits of 20k each.

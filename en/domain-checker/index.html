FAQ

How to choose a good domain?
Good domains should be short, memorable, and easy to spell. Prefer .com and avoid numbers/hyphens. Using brand names or keywords helps with SEO.

What if the domain is taken?
Try different extensions (like .net/.io), add prefixes/suffixes to the name, or contact the domain owner to negotiate a purchase.

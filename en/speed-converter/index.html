FAQ

How fast is Mach 1?

Mach 1 ≈ 1225 km/h at sea level. At high altitude, sound travels slower, Mach speed decreases.

Knots to km/h?

1 knot = 1 nautical mile/hour = 1.852 km/h. Used in marine and aviation.

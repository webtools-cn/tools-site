📋 Fence Types Explained

🔒 Privacy Fence: Uses wide boards (typically 150mm) placed tightly together with no gaps, standing 1.8m or taller. Completely blocks the view — ideal for backyards and pool enclosures.

🏡 Picket Fence: Uses narrower pickets (typically 90mm) with 50-70mm gaps between them, usually around 1.2m tall. Offers both aesthetics and airflow — great for front yards.

⛓️ Chain-link Fence: Uses metal mesh sold by the roll (15-25m per roll). The most economical option, requires tension bars and ties. Best for large areas and industrial sites.

💡 Installation Tips

  • Hole depth: Should be 1/3 to 1/2 of the post's above-ground height, and must extend below the local frost line
  • Post spacing: Keep it under 2.4m to prevent rails from sagging over time
  • Concrete: Each post needs roughly 1-2 bags of 40kg concrete, depending on hole dimensions
  • Buy 10% extra: Always order 10% more material for waste and cutting errors
  • Underground utilities: Always call your local utility company before digging
  • Corners & gates: Each corner and gate opening needs additional posts

📝 How to Use

  • Select your fence type, then enter the total fence length and post spacing — materials are calculated automatically
  • Privacy and picket fences require board width and gap inputs; chain-link fences do not
  • Enter material unit prices to get an automatic cost estimate
  • We recommend ordering 10% extra material beyond the calculated amounts
  • All calculations run locally in your browser — no data is uploaded

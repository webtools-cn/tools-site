💡 Conversion Notes

Formulas:
• Hourly → Monthly: Hourly Rate × Hours/Week × 4.33 (avg weeks/month)
• Monthly → Yearly: Monthly × 12
• Yearly → Monthly: Yearly ÷ 12
• Daily → Monthly: Daily × 21.67 (avg working days/month)
Note: All amounts shown are gross (pre-tax). Net income = Gross × (1 - Tax Rate%).

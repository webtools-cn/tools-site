About Retirement Withdrawal Strategies

Plan your retirement spending with confidence. The key question: how long will my savings last?

Common Strategies

  • •4% Rule: Withdraw 4% of total assets in year one, adjust for inflation
  • •Variable Withdrawal: Withdraw more in good years, less in bad
  • •Bucket Strategy: Short/medium/long-term pools

How much do I need to retire?

Using the 4% rule in reverse: annual expenses × 25 = target savings. E.g., $40,000/year needs $1,000,000. This is the FIRE movement's "25x rule."

    📖 About Metabolic Syndrome

    Why It Matters

    Metabolic syndrome is a precursor to cardiovascular disease and type 2 diabetes. It increases CVD risk 2x and diabetes risk 5x. Affects ~25% of adults globally.

    5 Diagnostic Criteria (IDF)

    Central obesity REQUIRED + at least 2 of:
    1️⃣ Waist ≥94cm men/≥80cm women
    2️⃣ Triglycerides ≥150mg/dL (1.7mmol/L)
    3️⃣ HDL <40 men/<50 women mg/dL
    4️⃣ BP ≥130/85mmHg
    5️⃣ Fasting glucose ≥100mg/dL (5.6mmol/L)

    Good News

    Metabolic syndrome is reversible! With lifestyle changes (weight loss, exercise, diet), most people improve within 3-6 months.

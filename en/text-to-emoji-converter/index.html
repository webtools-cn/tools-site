FAQ

How does this tool work?
It uses a built-in mapping of 200+ common emoji with their English names. Text is matched against keywords and replaced with corresponding emoji.
What words can be converted?
Common emotions (happy, sad), weather (sun, rain), animals (cat, dog), food (coffee, cake), activities (run, music) and more.
Can I do reverse conversion?
Yes. Use the search box to find emoji by name. You can also look up what an emoji means.
Is my data uploaded?
No. All processing is done locally in your browser. Text never leaves your device.
Does it support batch conversion?
Yes. Each line or space-separated word is independently matched and converted.

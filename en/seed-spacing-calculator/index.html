What is Seed Spacing Calculator?

Seed Spacing Calculator is a free online tool that runs entirely in your browser. No download or signup needed — enter your bed dimensions, row spacing, and plant spacing to calculate how many plants you need. All data is processed locally.

Use Cases

  • Plan vegetable garden layout and planting density
  • Calculate seed requirements for raised beds
  • Optimize row spacing for commercial farming
  • Estimate plant counts for container gardening

Key Features

  • Completely Free: No registration, no limits
  • Privacy Safe: Data never leaves your browser
  • Cross-Platform: Any modern browser
  • Zero Dependencies: Open and use

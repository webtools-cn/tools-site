📖 How to Use

This holiday savings calculator helps you plan and spread out holiday spending:

Step 1: Set Total Budget

Add up all expected holiday expenses (gifts, meals, travel, decorations, etc.).

Step 2: Set Time Remaining

Enter how many months until the holiday. The earlier you start, the less per month.

Step 3: Enter Current Savings

If you've already saved some money, enter the current balance to calculate the remaining gap.

Step 4: View Your Plan

The calculator shows how much to save monthly, weekly, and daily to reach your goal.

💡 Saving Tips

  • Auto-transfer: Set up monthly automatic transfers to a dedicated savings account.
  • 52-week challenge: Save $1 week 1, $2 week 2, etc. - you'll have $1,378 by year-end!
  • Shop sales: Make a gift list early and buy during sales to save 30-50%.
  • DIY gifts: Handmade gifts are more personal and cost less.

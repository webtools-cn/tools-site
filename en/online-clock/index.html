📖 About This Clock

This online clock uses your device's system time to display the current time in real-time. Switch between analog and digital modes, and view time across major world time zones.

The analog clock displays precise hour, minute, and second hands. The digital clock shows time in a large, clear format.

Accuracy

This tool updates every animation frame (~60fps) using JavaScript's Date object. If your device has NTP (Network Time Protocol) enabled, time accuracy matches atomic clock precision.

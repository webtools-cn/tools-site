❓ FAQ

How many m/s² in 1g?

1 standard gravity (g) = 9.80665 m/s². This is the average gravitational acceleration at Earth's surface, used in aerospace and physics calculations.

What is a Gal unit?

Gal (Galileo) is an acceleration unit used in gravimetry. 1 Gal = 1 cm/s² = 0.01 m/s². Commonly used in geophysics and gravity surveys.

How to convert km/h·s to m/s²?

1 km/h·s = 1/3.6 ≈ 0.2778 m/s². It means the speed increases by 1 km/h per second, commonly used in car acceleration specs.

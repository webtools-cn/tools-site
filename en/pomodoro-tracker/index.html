FAQ

What is the Pomodoro Technique?

The Pomodoro Technique was invented by Francesco Cirillo: 25 minutes of focused work (one "Pomodoro"), then a 5-minute break. After 4 Pomodoros, take a 15-30 minute long break. The core idea is short bursts of intense focus plus regular rest.

Why is 25 minutes optimal?

Research shows attention drops significantly after 25-30 minutes. 25 minutes is enough to complete a small task without causing fatigue. You can adjust based on personal preference.

What should I do during breaks?

Stand up, walk, drink water, stretch, look out the window — stay away from screens. Don't scroll phone or continue working. True mental rest restores focus.

How Text Reversal Works

Full Reverse: Every character in the text is reversed, including spaces and punctuation. For example, "Hello World" becomes "dlroW olleH". The entire character sequence is flipped end-to-end.

Reverse Words: The order of words is reversed, but each word itself remains readable. For example, "Hello World" becomes "World Hello". Only word positions change, not the characters within each word.

Both Mode: Combines both approaches, showing both the fully reversed text and the word-reversed text for comparison. This is useful for understanding the difference between character-level and word-level reversal.

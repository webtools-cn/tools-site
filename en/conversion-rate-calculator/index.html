About Conversion Rate Calculator

The Conversion Rate Calculator is a free online tool that calculates your conversion rate from total visits and conversion counts. Perfect for marketers, e-commerce managers, and growth analysts. Works on mobile and desktop, all calculations run locally in your browser.

How to Use

  1. Enter the total number of visits
  2. Enter the number of conversions
  3. Click "Calculate" to see the conversion rate percentage

Is this tool accurate?

The calculation is based on standard math formulas, results are precise and reliable.

No download needed — just open the page and start calculating. Everything runs in your browser.

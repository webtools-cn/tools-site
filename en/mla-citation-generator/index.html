About MLA 9th Edition Format

The MLA (Modern Language Association) 9th Edition citation format is the most widely used academic citation standard in the humanities. The Works Cited list is alphabetized by author's last name and uses a hanging indent. In-text citations follow the (Author Page) format, allowing readers to locate the full source in the Works Cited list.

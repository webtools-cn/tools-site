Roof Pitch Calculator: calculation logic

Roof area multiplier = √(1 + (Rise/Run)²)
Actual roof surface area = Footprint area × Roof area multiplier

📖 Formulas

Core Formulas

Pitch (X:12) = (Rise ÷ Run) × 12

Angle = arctan(Rise ÷ Run)

Slope % = (Rise ÷ Run) × 100

Rafter Length = √(Rise² + Run²)

FAQ

How many steps per day is healthy?

For most adults, 8,000-10,000 steps is an ideal health target. Recent studies show adults over 60 can gain significant health benefits from just 6,000-8,000 steps. Consistency matters more than the number.

Can walking help me lose weight?

Yes! Walking 10,000 steps burns approximately 300-500 calories (depending on weight and pace). Combined with a healthy diet, you can lose 3-5 kg (7-11 lbs) in 3 months. Choose "Lose Weight" for a higher step recommendation.

How far is 10,000 steps?

The average stride length is about 0.7 meters, so 10,000 steps equals roughly 7 km (4.3 miles). This takes about 60-90 minutes of walking. Spread it throughout the day — you don't need to do it all at once.

Is this calculator accurate?

This tool provides science-based recommendations based on age, weight, and goals, but individual fitness levels vary. Use a fitness tracker or phone pedometer for actual step counts. This tool is for goal-setting reference only.

FAQ

What is the Airbnb platform fee?

Airbnb typically charges hosts 15% of the booking subtotal. Superhosts or long-term stays may qualify for 3-5% rates. Cleaning fees paid by guests are not included in the fee calculation. Adjust as needed.

How can I improve my occupancy rate?

High-quality photos, accurate listings, competitive pricing, and fast response rates are key. Raise prices during peak season and discount during off-peak. 65%+ occupancy is considered strong performance.

How is short-term rental ROI calculated?

Annual ROI = (Annual Net Profit ÷ Total Investment) × 100%. Total Investment = Down Payment + Renovation Costs. Net Profit = Annual Revenue - Annual Operating Costs - Annual Mortgage Interest. A good short-term rental typically yields 6-12%.

Do I need to pay taxes on Airbnb income?

Yes, Airbnb income is taxable. Rates vary by country and city, and some jurisdictions impose additional tourist taxes. Consult a local tax professional for guidance.

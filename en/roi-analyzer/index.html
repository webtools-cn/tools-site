What is ROI?

ROI (Return on Investment) measures investment efficiency: ROI = (Final Value - Invested) / Invested × 100%. CAGR (Compound Annual Growth Rate) accounts for compounding: CAGR = (Final/Initial)^(1/years) - 1. The higher the CAGR, the better the compound growth.

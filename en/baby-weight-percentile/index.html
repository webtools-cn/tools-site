FAQ

What does weight percentile mean?

The percentile shows where your baby's weight ranks among peers of the same age and gender. For example, P75 means your baby is heavier than 75% of babies. P3-P97 is considered the normal range.

What if my baby is outside the normal range?

Below P3 may indicate underweight, above P97 may indicate overweight. However, growth trends matter more than single measurements. Consult a pediatrician if concerned. This tool is for reference only, not medical advice.

What data source is used?

This calculator uses WHO 2006 Child Growth Standards, covering 0-24 months weight-for-age percentiles. It's the globally recognized gold standard.

What is a normal birth weight?

Full-term newborns typically weigh 2.5-4.0 kg (5.5-8.8 lbs), with a median of about 3.3 kg (7.3 lbs). Below 2.5 kg is considered low birth weight.

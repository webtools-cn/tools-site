FAQ

What is the HELOC draw period?

The HELOC draw period, typically 5-10 years, is when you can draw funds anytime and only need to pay interest.

Do I repay principal during the draw period?

Most HELOCs only require interest payments during the draw period, not principal.

What happens after the draw period ends?

The repayment period begins, typically 10-20 years, where you must repay principal + interest and can no longer draw funds.

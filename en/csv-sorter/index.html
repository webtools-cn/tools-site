❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use Csv Sorter on my phone?

Yes. Csv Sorter is fully responsive and works on phones, tablets, and desktops. The interface adapts automatically to your screen size.

Are there any usage limits for Csv Sorter?

Csv Sorter has no usage limits — it's free and unlimited. Since processing is done client-side, speed depends on your device. For very large files, we recommend processing in batches for the best experience.

Is Csv Sorter free to use?

Yes, Csv Sorter is completely free to use. No registration or login required — all features are available immediately.

Do I need to install any software?

No. Csv Sorter is a web-based tool — just open it in your browser and start using it. No downloads, plugins, or extensions required.

Does Csv Sorter upload my data to a server?

No. Csv Sorter uses client-side processing — everything runs in your browser. Your data never leaves your device, and the tool works even without an internet connection.

Is my data safe when using Csv Sorter?

Absolutely. All processing happens locally in your browser. No data is sent to any server, so there's zero risk of data leaks. Your information stays completely under your control.

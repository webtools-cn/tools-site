Free online workout generator that creates personalized exercise plans based on your goals, level, and venue. Perfect for muscle building, fat loss, or toning.

  • 🎯 3 goal modes: Muscle Building (strength focus), Fat Loss (HIIT + cardio), Toning (moderate weight, high reps)
  • 📊 3 difficulty levels with appropriate exercise selection
  • 🏠 Home (bodyweight) and Gym (free weights + machines) modes
  • ⏱️ Adjustable duration from 15-90 minutes
  • 📋 Complete plans: warm-up → main workout → cardio/HIIT → stretch
  • 🖨️ One-click print for a physical copy

About Social Security Benefits

Social Security is a federal retirement benefits program. Your benefit amount is based on your average lifetime earnings, birth year, and claiming age.

Key Ages

  • •62: Earliest claiming age, benefit permanently reduced (up to 30%)
  • •67: Full Retirement Age for those born 1960+
  • •70: Maximum benefit age, 8% per year delayed credits

How to maximize Social Security?

Delay to 70 for maximum monthly benefit (~76% more than age 62). Also ensure at least 35 years of earnings, as benefits are based on your highest 35 years.

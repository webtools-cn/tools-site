HAS-BLED Bleeding Risk Score

Assess 1-year major bleeding risk in anticoagulated atrial fibrillation patients. Clinical decision support for safe anticoagulation.

HAS-BLED = Hypertension · Abnormal renal/liver function · Stroke · Bleeding history/predisposition · Labile INRs · Elderly (>65) · Drugs/alcohol

❓ FAQ

Will my images be uploaded to a server?

No. All computation happens on your device — ↔️ Side by Side Image Comparison, other values and other inputs stay in your browser. Your images never leave your device.

What if the images have different dimensions?

The tool automatically scales images to the same height (horizontal) or same width (vertical) for optimal comparison.

What formats are supported?

JPG/JPEG, PNG, WebP, and BMP. Downloads are in PNG format.

❓ FAQ

How many calories in 1 Joule?

1 Joule (J) ≈ 0.239 calories (cal). One calorie is defined as the energy needed to raise 1 gram of water by 1°C, equal to 4.184 Joules.

How many Joules in 1 kWh?

1 kWh = 3,600,000 Joules (3.6 MJ). Kilowatt-hour is the common unit for electricity billing — the energy used by a 1 kW appliance running for 1 hour.

What's the difference between calories and kilocalories?

1 kilocalorie (kcal) = 1,000 calories (cal). Food labels typically show kilocalories, often labeled as 'Calories' with a capital C. One apple contains about 50-100 kcal.

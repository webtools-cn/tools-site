💡 How to Use

  • Filter by Category: Click category tags to filter (multi-select supported). Leave all unselected to pick from everything.
  • One-Click Pick: Click "Random Pick" for an instant meal suggestion. Don't like it? Click "Try Another".
  • Custom Menu: Switch to Custom mode to enter your own food list. The tool picks randomly from your personal menu.
  • History: All picks are saved automatically so you can review past suggestions.
  • 100% Free: Runs entirely in your browser. No registration, no data upload.

❓ FAQ

  • What categories are available? 8 categories: Chinese Classics, Western Cuisine, Fast Food, Healthy Light Meals, Noodles & Rice Noodles, Japanese & Korean, Southeast Asian Flavors, and Snacks — 100+ dishes total.
  • Can I exclude dishes I don't like? Yes! Switch to Custom mode and edit the list to keep only your favorites.
  • Does it work with food delivery apps? This tool helps you decide what to eat. Once decided, order from your favorite delivery app (Uber Eats, DoorDash, etc.).
  • Mobile friendly? Fully responsive — works perfectly on phones. Solve your "what to eat" dilemma anywhere!

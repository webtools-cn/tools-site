📖 About Lifetime Earnings

How much will I earn in a lifetime?

With a $50K starting salary, 3% annual raises, and 40-year career, gross lifetime earnings reach ~$3.8M. With promotions every 5 years at 15%, this can grow to ~$4.5M+. Career choices and development paths significantly impact total earnings.

Why calculate lifetime earnings?

1) Understand earning potential to motivate saving; 2) Plan major expenses (home, education); 3) Evaluate career change economics; 4) Set retirement savings goals proportionally.

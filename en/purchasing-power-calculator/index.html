What is Purchasing Power?

Purchasing power is how much goods and services your money can buy. Inflation erodes it — $100 today buys less than $100 did 10 years ago.

FAQ

How to beat inflation?

Investing is key: 1) Stocks average 7-10% long-term, well above inflation; 2) Real estate appreciates with inflation; 3) TIPS bonds are directly CPI-linked; 4) Gold is a traditional inflation hedge. Keeping cash in a low-interest savings account means losing purchasing power every year.

Why does inflation feel higher than CPI?

CPI is a weighted average. Your personal spending mix may differ — if you spend more on rent and food, your experienced inflation may be higher. Asset prices (housing, stocks) are not included in CPI.

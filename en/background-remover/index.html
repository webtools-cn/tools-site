❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Does Image Background Remover upload my data to a server?

No. Image Background Remover uses client-side processing — everything runs in your browser. Your data never leaves your device, and the tool works even without an internet connection.

Can I use Image Background Remover on my phone?

Yes. Image Background Remover is fully responsive and works on phones, tablets, and desktops. The interface adapts automatically to your screen size.

Is my data safe when using Image Background Remover?

Absolutely. Everything runs locally: your image is processed on your device, never sent to a server. No data is sent to any server, so there's zero risk of data leaks. Your information stays completely under your control.

Are there any usage limits for Image Background Remover?

Image Background Remover has no usage limits — it's free and unlimited. Since processing is done client-side, speed depends on your device. For very large files, we recommend processing in batches for the best experience.

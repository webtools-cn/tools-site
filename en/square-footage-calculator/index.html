FAQ

How to calculate sq ft?

Area = Length × Width (in feet). If in meters, convert: 1m ≈ 3.281ft.

Sq m to sq ft conversion?

1 sq m ≈ 10.764 sq ft. Multiply sq meters by 10.764.

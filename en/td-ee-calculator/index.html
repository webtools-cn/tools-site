📖 What is TDEE?

TDEE = BMR × Activity Factor. BMR is the calories your body burns at complete rest. TDEE adds daily activity and exercise.

🎯 Weight Loss & Gain

  • Weight Loss: TDEE - 20%
  • Maintenance: = TDEE
  • Muscle Gain: TDEE + 20%

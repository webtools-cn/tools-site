About Extra Payment Calculator

Extra payments are voluntary additional principal payments on top of your regular monthly amount. Even small extra payments can significantly reduce total interest and shorten your loan term due to compounding effects. Common approaches include fixed monthly extras, annual bonus lump sums, or one-time large payments.

📖 How It Works

Pick a start date, enter the amount (positive to add, negative to subtract), and choose the unit. Supports days, weeks, months, and years. Month addition handles end-of-month boundaries automatically (e.g., Jan 31 + 1 month = Feb 28/29). Use the preset buttons for quick selections.

What is Date Add Calculator?

Date Add Calculator is a free online tool that runs entirely in your browser. No download or signup needed — enter Start Date and Amount to get results instantly. All data is processed locally.

Use Cases

  • Calculate time intervals when scheduling events
  • Convert time zones for international communication
  • Track important dates like age or countdowns
  • Quick time checks on mobile without calendar apps

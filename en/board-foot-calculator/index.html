About Board Feet

A board foot (BF) is the standard unit of lumber volume used in North America. One board foot equals 12 inches × 12 inches × 1 inch, or 144 cubic inches of wood. It's the go-to measurement for buying and selling hardwood lumber.

Calculation Formula

  • Imperial: Board Feet = (Thickness[in] × Width[in] × Length[ft]) / 12 × Quantity
  • Alternative: Board Feet = (Thickness[in] × Width[in] × Length[in]) / 144 × Quantity
  • Metric conversion: 1 BF = 0.002359737 m³ = 2,359.74 cm³

Tips

  • Lumber sizes are nominal — actual dimensions are smaller. A 2×4 measures 1.5" × 3.5". This calculator uses actual dimensions.
  • Recommended waste factor: 10% for standard cuts, 15–20% for complex projects.
  • Weight estimates are based on average density and may vary ±15% depending on moisture content.
  • Cost includes waste compensation: Total = Price/BF × Total BF × (1 + Waste%) × (1 + Tax%).

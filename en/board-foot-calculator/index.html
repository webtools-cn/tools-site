Board Foot Calculation Guide

A board foot (BF) is the standard unit of measurement for lumber in North America. Whether you're a professional woodworker, DIY enthusiast, or construction contractor, accurately calculating board feet is essential for estimating material needs and costs.

Board Foot Formula

When thickness and width are in inches and length is in feet:

Board Feet = (Thickness[in] × Width[in] × Length[ft]) / 12

When all dimensions are in inches:

Board Feet = (Thickness[in] × Width[in] × Length[in]) / 144

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a board foot?

A board foot (BF) is the standard unit of measurement for lumber in North America. One board foot equals 12 inches × 12 inches × 1 inch of wood volume. The formula is: Board Feet = (Thickness[in] × Width[in] × Length[ft]) / 12. Lumber is typically priced and sold by the board foot.

What is the difference between nominal and actual lumber sizes?

Nominal size is the size of lumber when cut at the sawmill (e.g., 2×4), while actual size is the real dimension after drying and planing (a 2×4 is actually 1.5" × 3.5"). Lumber is sold by nominal size names, but board foot calculations should use actual dimensions. This calculator includes a built-in nominal-to-actual size lookup table.

How do I calculate board feet for multiple boards?

For boards of the same size, calculate the board feet of one piece and multiply by the quantity. For multiple batches of different sizes, use the Batch Calculation mode — add each specification as a row, and the tool automatically sums up total board feet and total cost.

How do board feet relate to cubic feet?

One board foot equals 1/12 of a cubic foot (approximately 0.0833 cubic feet). Therefore, 1 cubic foot = 12 board feet. In metric units, 1 board foot ≈ 0.00236 cubic meters ≈ 2,359.7 cubic centimeters. Board feet are primarily used in North America; Europe and Asia typically use cubic meters.

How do I estimate lumber cost?

Lumber is typically sold by the board foot. Enter the price per board foot ($/BF) in the calculator, and it will compute total cost = board feet × unit price. Prices vary widely by species: pine runs about $2-5/BF, oak $4-8/BF, and walnut $8-15/BF. Check with your local supplier for current pricing.

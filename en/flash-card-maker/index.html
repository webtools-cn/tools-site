📖 About Flash Card Learning

Flash cards are a scientifically proven study tool that leverages Active Recall and Spaced Repetition to help transfer knowledge from short-term to long-term memory.

Use Cases

  • 📝 Language Learning: Word-Definition, Phrase-Translation
  • 📚 Exam Prep: Concept-Definition, Formula-Explanation
  • 🧠 Knowledge Memorization: Question-Answer, Date-Event
  • 💼 Professional Skills: Term-Explanation, Command-Function

Tips

  • Keep each card focused on one concept
  • Write a question/prompt on front, full answer on back
  • Review regularly; mark mastered cards to skip them
  • Reviewing 10-20 cards daily yields the best results

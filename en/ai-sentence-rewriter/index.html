❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data safe when using Ai Sentence Rewriter?

Absolutely. Everything runs locally: Ai Sentence Rewriter and 相关参数 are processed on your device, never sent to a server. No data is sent to any server, so there's zero risk of data leaks. Your information stays completely under your control.

Are there any usage limits for Ai Sentence Rewriter?

Ai Sentence Rewriter has no usage limits — it's free and unlimited. Since processing is done client-side, speed depends on your device. For very large files, we recommend processing in batches for the best experience.

Do I need to install any software?

No. Ai Sentence Rewriter is a web-based tool — just open it in your browser and start using it. No downloads, plugins, or extensions required.

Does Ai Sentence Rewriter upload my data to a server?

No. Ai Sentence Rewriter uses client-side processing — everything runs in your browser. Your data never leaves your device, and the tool works even without an internet connection.

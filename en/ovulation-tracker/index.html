FAQ

How is ovulation day calculated?

Ovulation typically occurs 14 days before your next period. So: Ovulation Day = Last Period + Cycle Length - 14. For a 28-day cycle, ovulation is on day 14.

What is the fertile window?

Sperm can survive 3-5 days in the body, and the egg lives about 24 hours. The fertile window spans 5 days before to 1 day after ovulation — about 6-7 days total. This is the best time for conception.

How accurate is this calculator?

This tool is based on a standard cycle model and works best for women with regular cycles. Actual ovulation can shift due to stress, illness, etc. For irregular cycles, use ovulation test kits or consult a doctor.

Is the rhythm method reliable for birth control?

The rhythm method has a high failure rate (~24%) and is not recommended as a sole contraceptive. This tool is for reference only — do not rely on it as your only birth control method.

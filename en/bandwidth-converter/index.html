❓ FAQ

What's the download speed of 100Mbps?

100 Mbps = 12.5 MB/s. Since 1 Byte = 8 bits, divide by 8: 100 ÷ 8 = 12.5 MB/s. This is the theoretical maximum; actual speeds vary with network conditions.

What's the difference between Mbps and MB/s?

Mbps (Megabits per second) measures network bandwidth. MB/s (Megabytes per second) measures file transfer speed. 1 MB/s = 8 Mbps. ISPs advertise speeds in Mbps.

How to convert Kbps to KB/s?

1 KB/s = 8 Kbps. The lowercase 'b' means bit, uppercase 'B' means Byte. Remember the 8:1 ratio.

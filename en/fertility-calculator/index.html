💡 Ovulation Facts

Ovulation typically occurs 14 days before next period. Sperm survive 3-5 days, egg survives 12-24 hours. Best conception time: 1-2 days before ovulation.
Signs: clear stretchy mucus, BBT rise 0.3-0.5°C, mild cramping, breast tenderness.

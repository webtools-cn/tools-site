📖 About the FIRE Movement

What is FIRE?

Financial Independence, Retire Early. Core principle: save 50%+ of income, invest in low-cost index funds, accumulate 25x annual expenses for retirement.

FIRE Variants

Lean FIRE: minimalist retirement. Fat FIRE: affluent retirement. Barista FIRE: semi-retired with part-time work. Coast FIRE: enough saved, no need to contribute more.

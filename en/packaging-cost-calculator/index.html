About the Packaging Cost Calculator

The Packaging Cost Calculator is a free online tool that calculates the per-unit packaging cost from your total packaging material cost and the number of products. It also shows the cost per 100 units for bulk planning. All calculations run locally in your browser — your data is never uploaded.

How to Use

  1. Enter the total packaging material cost
  2. Enter the number of products to package
  3. Click "Calculate" to see the per-unit and per-100-unit cost

Is this tool accurate?

The calculation uses standard arithmetic: per-unit cost = total material cost / quantity, per-100 cost = per-unit × 100. Results are precise.

Do I need to download anything?

No download needed. Everything runs in your browser — your data never leaves your device.

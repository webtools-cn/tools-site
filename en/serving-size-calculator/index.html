About Recipe Serving Size Converter

Recipe Serving Size Converter is a free online tool. Enter original and desired serving counts to auto-scale all ingredient amounts. Perfect for meal prep, batch cooking, and dinner parties.

How to Use

  1. Enter the original recipe servings (e.g., 4 servings)
  2. Enter your desired new servings (e.g., 6 servings)
  3. Click "Calculate" to see the scaling ratio and ingredient examples

Does doubling servings double cooking time?

Not necessarily. Cooking time increases but not always proportionally. Use a thermometer to check doneness.

Do all ingredients scale linearly?

Most do, but spices and seasonings often only need 1.5x not 2x — taste and adjust.

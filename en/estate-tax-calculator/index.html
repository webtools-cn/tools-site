📖 Understanding US Estate Tax

The US federal estate tax applies to estates whose total assets exceed the exemption threshold. In 2025, the federal exemption is approximately $13,990,000 (individual) or $27,980,000 (married couple). Amounts above the exemption are taxed at progressive rates from 18% to 40%.

Federal Estate Tax Rates (2025)

Taxable amounts above exemption: first $10,000 at 18%, $10,001-$20,000 at 20%, $20,001-$40,000 at 22%, gradually rising to 40% for amounts over $1,000,000.

State Estate Tax Notes

  • 12 states + DC levy independent estate taxes with lower exemptions than federal
  • State estate tax rates typically range from 10% to 20%
  • Some states (e.g. NY) have a "cliff rule": estates exceeding the exemption by 5%+ may have the entire estate taxed
  • This calculator provides estimates only — consult a professional tax advisor for actual planning

❓ FAQ

What's the difference between estate tax and inheritance tax?

Estate tax is paid by the estate itself before distributions to heirs. Inheritance tax is paid by individual heirs based on their share. The federal government only has estate tax. Some states (KY, NJ, etc.) levy inheritance tax.

What assets count toward Estate Value?

Nearly all assets count: real estate, bank accounts, stocks, bonds, retirement accounts, life insurance (if estate is beneficiary), business interests, art, jewelry. Only assets already gifted or in irrevocable trusts are excluded.

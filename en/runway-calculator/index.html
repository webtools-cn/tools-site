📖 Runway Calculation Guide

  • Optimistic: High revenue growth, longest runway
  • Base: Revenue grows as expected
  • Pessimistic: Slow or no revenue growth
  • Runway = months until cash runs out (considering revenue/expense growth)
  • Healthy runway > 18 months; < 6 months is a red flag

What is Withholding Tax?

Withholding tax is the federal income tax your employer deducts from each paycheck. This tool uses 2024 IRS tax brackets: standard deduction $14,600 (Single), $29,200 (MFJ), $21,900 (HOH). Brackets: 10%/12%/22%/24%/32%/35%/37%.

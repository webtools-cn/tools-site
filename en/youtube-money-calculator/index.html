FAQ

What is YouTube CPM?

CPM (Cost Per Mille) is what advertisers pay per 1,000 ad impressions. Creator ad revenue = views ÷ 1,000 × CPM × 55% (YouTube keeps 45%). CPM varies widely by niche and audience location.

How much for 1 million views?

At CPM=$5: ~$2,750 (creator's 55% share); at CPM=$10: ~$5,500. Actual earnings depend on ad types, viewer demographics, and content niche.

What is YouTube's revenue split?

YouTube takes 45% of ad revenue, creators keep 55%. This calculator defaults to 55% and is adjustable.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. All calculations run locally in your browser. Your data never leaves your device.

💡 EPF Facts

EPF is a mandatory retirement savings scheme. Both employee and employer contribute monthly with guaranteed interest. Malaysia EPF rate ~5.5% for 2024.
Compound effect: early contributions are far more valuable. Starting at 30 vs 40 can more than double your final balance.

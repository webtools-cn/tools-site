❓ FAQ

Is Bcrypt Password Checker free?

Yes, completely free to use. No signup or login required.

Is my data secure?

Yes. This is a local tool built with pure JavaScript that runs in your browser. Passwords and hashes are calculated locally and never uploaded to any server.

What bcrypt formats are supported?

Supports standard $2a$, $2b$, and $2y$ bcrypt hash formats with rounds 4-31.

FAQ

What is DCA?

DCA (Dollar Cost Averaging) means investing a fixed amount regularly regardless of market conditions. It smooths out purchase prices and reduces timing risk over the long term.

DCA vs lump sum: which is better?

Lump sum tends to outperform in bull markets; DCA reduces risk in volatile/bear markets. DCA is generally more robust for most investors.

What DCA frequency should I choose?

Monthly is most common. More frequent DCA (weekly) provides slightly better averaging but the difference is usually marginal. Consistency matters most.

What expected return should I use?

Stock funds: ~8-12% long-term; bonds: ~4-6%; money market: ~2-3%. Be conservative in your estimates.

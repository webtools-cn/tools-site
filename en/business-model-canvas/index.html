The Business Model Canvas is a classic strategic tool created by Alexander Osterwalder, helping entrepreneurs and business leaders present a complete business model on a single page. This tool is completely free, no registration required, no software installation. All data is stored locally in your browser and never uploaded to any server. Supports multiple canvas saves, export, and print functionality. Through the 9 building blocks (Customer Segments, Value Propositions, Channels, Customer Relationships, Revenue Streams, Key Resources, Key Activities, Key Partners, Cost Structure), you can systematically analyze and optimize your business model. Whether you're an entrepreneur, product manager, or student, you'll find it valuable.

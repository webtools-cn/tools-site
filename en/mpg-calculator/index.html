About the MPG Calculator

MPG (Miles Per Gallon) is the standard measure of fuel efficiency in the United States and United Kingdom. Our MPG Calculator helps you quickly determine your vehicle's fuel economy by simply entering the distance you've driven and the amount of fuel consumed.

This tool also supports bidirectional conversion between MPG and L/100km (liters per 100 kilometers). The conversion formula is: L/100km = 235.215 ÷ MPG. For example, 30 MPG equals approximately 7.84 L/100km, and 25 MPG equals approximately 9.41 L/100km.

If you're car shopping, comparing MPG ratings is a great way to evaluate fuel economy. Generally, a MPG of 25-30 is average for sedans, 30-40 is fuel-efficient, and 40+ is excellent (typical of hybrid vehicles). Trucks and large SUVs typically get 15-22 MPG.

For drivers outside the US who are more familiar with L/100km, this converter makes it easy to understand American fuel economy ratings. All calculations run entirely in your browser — no data is sent to any server, and no registration is required. Works seamlessly on both mobile and desktop.

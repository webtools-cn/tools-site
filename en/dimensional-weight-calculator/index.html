FAQ

What is dimensional weight?

Dimensional weight (volumetric weight) is calculated from package dimensions. Carriers charge the greater of actual weight or dimensional weight to prevent bulky, lightweight packages from taking up too much space.

What standards are used?

Express carriers (DHL/UPS/FedEx) typically use ÷5000 (cm³/kg); air freight uses ÷6000; US domestic uses ÷139 (in³/lb). Always check your carrier's specific divisor.

How is chargeable weight determined?

Carriers take the greater of actual weight and dimensional weight. If your package is bulky but light, you'll pay the dimensional rate.

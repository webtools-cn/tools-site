💡 ESOP Tips

Strike = price you pay. FMV = current fair value. Exercise profit = (FMV - strike) × options.
Tax note: Exercise spread usually taxed as ordinary income. Sale gains taxed as capital gains. Rules vary — consult a tax advisor.

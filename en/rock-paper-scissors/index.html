❓ FAQ
How to play?
Rock beats Scissors, Scissors beats Paper, Paper beats Rock. Choose your move, AI picks randomly. Each win = 1 point. First to target wins.
Is the AI really random?
Yes, using JavaScript random for each round. Completely independent and fair.
Can I change the target score?
Click the target score to cycle through 3, 5, 7, and 10 points.

About Online Poll Maker

Create and share polls with a shareable link. Supports single choice and multiple choice modes, with real-time result statistics. All data is stored in your browser's localStorage — nothing is uploaded to any server. Your browser data stays on your device.

What is Online Poll Maker?

Online Poll Maker is a free online tool that runs entirely in your browser. No download or signup needed — create polls with a title and options, share the link, and collect votes instantly. All data is processed locally.

Use Cases

  • Quick team decision making and voting
  • Event planning and preference gathering
  • Classroom or workshop interactive surveys
  • Community feedback collection

Key Features

  • Completely Free: No registration, no limits
  • Privacy Safe: Data never leaves your browser
  • Cross-Platform: Any modern browser
  • Zero Dependencies: Open and use

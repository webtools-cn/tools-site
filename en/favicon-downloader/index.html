❓ FAQ

What is a favicon?

A favicon is the small icon displayed in browser tabs and bookmarks, typically 16×16 pixels. Almost every website has one, referenced via a link tag in the HTML head.

How does this tool work?

It tries multiple favicon sources: Google Favicon API, DuckDuckGo icon service, and the website's own /favicon.ico path. All requests go directly from your browser to the source — no data is sent to our server.

Can I use downloaded favicons commercially?

Favicons are typically part of a website's brand/trademark. Use for personal reference only. Contact the site owner for commercial use authorization.

Is my data uploaded?

No. All processing happens in your browser. Your data never leaves your device.

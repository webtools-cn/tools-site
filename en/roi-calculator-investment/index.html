What is Investment ROI Calculator?

Investment ROI Calculator is a free online tool that helps you evaluate the performance of your investments. It calculates three key metrics: net profit, total ROI percentage, and annualized return (CAGR). Simply enter your initial investment, current value, and investment period to get instant results.

All calculations happen locally in your browser — no data is ever sent to a server. Your financial information stays completely private.

Use Cases

  • Compare the performance of different investment portfolios
  • Evaluate stock, bond, or mutual fund returns over time
  • Assess whether your investments are beating inflation
  • Plan for retirement by tracking growth of your savings
  • Make informed decisions about rebalancing your portfolio

Key Features

  • Instant Results: See your ROI, profit, and CAGR in milliseconds
  • Completely Free: No registration, no limits, no hidden fees
  • Privacy Safe: All data stays in your browser, never uploaded
  • Accurate Formulas: Uses standard CAGR and ROI calculations
  • Mobile Friendly: Works perfectly on phones and tablets

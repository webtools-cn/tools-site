📖 Valuation Method Guide

  • Berkus Method: For seed/pre-revenue startups. Values 5 key dimensions, up to $2.5M total.
  • Scorecard Method: Compares against industry peers, adjusts for team, market, tech, and other factors.
  • VC Method: Works backwards from exit value. Best for VC rounds with clear exit expectations.
  • DCF Method: Projects future cash flows and discounts them. Best for mature startups with stable revenue.
  • These valuations are estimates only. Actual valuation depends on market conditions and negotiation.

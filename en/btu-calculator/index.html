    💡 Buying Tips

    • Choose an AC with BTU close to the calculated value — too high causes short cycling, too low won't cool adequately
    • Inverter ACs auto-adjust capacity, so slightly oversizing is less wasteful than with non-inverter units
    • Heating typically needs 20-30% more BTU than cooling (varies by climate zone)
    • For multiple rooms, install separate units rather than one oversized AC for the whole house
    • West-facing rooms may need an additional 10% BTU capacity

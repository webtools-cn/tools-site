📝 How to Calculate Salary from Hourly Wage

The formula for converting hourly wage to annual salary is: Annual Salary = Hourly Wage × Hours Per Week × Weeks Per Year

For a standard full-time job (40 hours/week, 52 weeks/year), you work 2,080 hours per year, so:

  • Annual Salary = Hourly Wage × 2,080
  • Monthly Salary = Annual Salary ÷ 12 = Hourly Wage × Weekly Hours × 52 ÷ 12
  • Weekly Pay = Hourly Wage × Hours Per Week
  • Daily Pay = Weekly Pay ÷ Workdays Per Week

This calculator lets you customize your weekly hours and annual weeks, making it suitable for full-time, part-time, and contract work. Switch to "Salary → Hourly" mode to convert an annual salary back to an hourly rate.

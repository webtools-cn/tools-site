❓ What is Pig Latin?

What are the rules?

Words starting with vowels (a/e/i/o/u) get "way" appended. Words starting with consonants move the consonant cluster to the end + "ay". E.g. "hello"→"ellohay", "apple"→"appleway".

Pig Latin is an English word game that originated as a children game in late 19th century America

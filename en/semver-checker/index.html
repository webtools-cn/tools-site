📦 Semver Checker

Free online tool to instantly check and validate semantic version numbers. Supports full Semver 2.0 specification — MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH, prerelease tags, and build metadata. Type a version and get instant feedback.

🔍 Check Your Version

Try: 1.0.0 2.1.3-beta.1 1.0.0-alpha+001 0.1.0 1.2.3+build.456 v1.2.3 01.2.3 1.2.3.4
💡 Semver 2.0 format: MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH — e.g., 1.2.3. Optional: -prerelease and +build. See semver.org for the full spec.

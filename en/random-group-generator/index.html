How to Use

Enter Items: Type or paste items in the text area on the left, one per line. Supports Chinese, English, and numbers.

Set Group Count: Choose how many groups you want (2-50 groups).

Random Grouping: Click the "Random Group" button, and the system shuffles all items and distributes them evenly across groups.

Re-shuffle: Not happy with the result? Click "Re-shuffle" for a new random grouping.

Use Cases

Team Building: Quickly divide participants into random teams for activities and competitions.

Classroom Activities: Create random groups for class projects and collaborative work.

Event Planning: Assign guests to tables or groups for parties and events.

Online Games: Randomly distribute players into teams for multiplayer games.

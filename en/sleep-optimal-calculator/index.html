📖 Sleep Science

90-Minute Sleep Cycles

Each cycle lasts ~90 minutes: light sleep → deep sleep → REM. Waking at cycle end (light sleep) = refreshed. Waking during deep sleep = groggy (sleep inertia).

Recommended Sleep by Age

School-age: 9-11h | Teens: 8-10h | Adults: 7-9h | Seniors: 7-8h. 5-6 cycles (7.5-9h) is ideal for most adults.

Sleep Hygiene Tips

Consistent schedule, no screens before bed, cool dark bedroom, no evening caffeine/alcohol, daytime sunlight exposure.

FAQ

How does vocal removal work?
Uses phase cancellation. In stereo audio, vocals are typically in the center channel. Inverting one channel and mixing cancels center-panned vocals.
What formats are supported?
WAV and MP3. Stereo audio works best; mono audio cannot be effectively separated.
Are files uploaded?
No. All processing is done locally in your browser. Files never leave your device.
How good is the separation?
Depends on the original mix. Studio-produced stereo music works best. Live recordings may have limited results.
Can I download separated tracks?
Yes. After processing, download instrumental and vocal tracks separately.

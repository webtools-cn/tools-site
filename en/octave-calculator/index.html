About Octave Note Calculator

Octave Note Calculator is a free online tool. Calculate note frequency relationships across octaves with semitone steps Works on mobile and desktop, all calculations run locally in your browser for privacy.

Octave Note Calculator in 3 steps

  1. Enter base frequency (Hz), default 440Hz
  2. Enter octave offset (positive=higher, negative=lower)
  3. Click "Calculate" to see the frequency result

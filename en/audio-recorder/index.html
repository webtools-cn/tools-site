What is the Free Online Audio Recorder?

Free Online Audio Recorder is a free online tool that runs entirely in your browser. No download or signup needed — hit record to capture your microphone, pause and resume, then play back or download as WAV or WebM. All processing happens locally on your device.

Use Cases

  • Record voice memos and quick notes without installing apps
  • Capture lectures, interviews, or meetings with pause/resume
  • Record audio for videos, podcasts, or voiceovers
  • Save recordings as WAV (lossless) or WebM directly from the browser

Key Features

  • Completely Free: No registration, no limits
  • Privacy Safe: Data never leaves your browser
  • Cross-Platform: Any modern browser with a microphone
  • Real-time waveform visualization

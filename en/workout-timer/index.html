💡 Mode Descriptions

HIIT: High-intensity interval training. Recommended: 40s Work + 20s Rest, 8-12 rounds.
Tabata: 20s all-out effort + 10s Rest, 8 rounds for a 4-minute session.
EMOM: Every Minute On the Minute — complete your reps each minute, rest for the remainder. Recommended 10-20 minutes.
Custom: Fully customize Work/Rest times and number of rounds.

What is Workout Timer?

Workout Timer is a free online tool that runs entirely in your browser. No download or signup needed — enter Prep Time and Work Time to get results instantly. All data is processed locally.

Use Cases

  • Calculate time intervals when scheduling events
  • Convert time zones for international communication
  • Track important dates like age or countdowns
  • Quick time checks on mobile without calendar apps

Key Features

  • Completely Free: No registration, no limits

What is Word Counter Online?

Word Counter Online is a free online tool that runs entirely in your browser. No download or signup needed — enter inthisInputorPasteTe and other values to get results instantly. All data is processed locally.

Use Cases

  • Transform text formatting quickly while editing documents
  • Count words or change case during content creation
  • Batch process text during data cleaning tasks
  • Handle text on mobile without opening a desktop editor

Key Features

  • Completely Free: No registration, no limits
  • Privacy Safe: Data never leaves your browser
  • Cross-Platform: Any modern browser
  • Zero Dependencies: Open and use

FAQ

What is the difference between wet bulb and regular temperature?

Regular temperature (dry bulb) is what a thermometer reads. Wet bulb temperature accounts for evaporative cooling and reflects the body's ability to cool through sweating — a key metric for heat stress.

What wet bulb temperature is dangerous?

Below 26°C (79°F): Safe. 26-30°C: Caution, reduce strenuous activity. 30-35°C: Dangerous, stop outdoor exercise. Above 35°C (95°F): Extreme danger, potentially fatal.

How accurate is this formula?

This tool uses the Stull formula, accurate to within ±0.5°C for typical temperature and humidity ranges. For professional meteorological use, consult specialized instruments.

Is my data safe?

Completely safe. This is a pure client-side tool — all calculations happen in your browser and never leave your device.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is Audio Volume Adjuster free to use?

Yes, Audio Volume Adjuster is completely free to use. No registration or login required — all features are available immediately.

Does Audio Volume Adjuster upload my data to a server?

No. Audio Volume Adjuster uses client-side processing — everything runs in your browser. Your data never leaves your device, and the tool works even without an internet connection.

Are there any usage limits for Audio Volume Adjuster?

Audio Volume Adjuster has no usage limits — it's free and unlimited. Since processing is done client-side, speed depends on your device. For very large files, we recommend processing in batches for the best experience.

Is my data safe when using Audio Volume Adjuster?

Absolutely. All processing happens locally in your browser. No data is sent to any server, so there's zero risk of data leaks. Your information stays completely under your control.

Can I use Audio Volume Adjuster on my phone?

Yes. Audio Volume Adjuster is fully responsive and works on phones, tablets, and desktops. The interface adapts automatically to your screen size.

Do I need to install any software?

No. Audio Volume Adjuster is a web-based tool — just open it in your browser and start using it. No downloads, plugins, or extensions required.

What audio formats are supported?

Common formats including MP3, WAV, OGG, AAC, and FLAC are supported.

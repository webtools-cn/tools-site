📖 FAQ

What is a calorie deficit?

A calorie deficit is the difference between calories burned and calories consumed. A 500 kcal/day deficit = ~0.5 kg/week weight loss; a 1000 kcal/day deficit = ~1 kg/week weight loss.

What is BMR and TDEE?

BMR (Basal Metabolic Rate) is the energy your body burns at complete rest. TDEE (Total Daily Energy Expenditure) is BMR plus calories burned through daily activities and exercise.

💡 About the Sobriety Tracker

The Sobriety Tracker helps you track days free from any habit — alcohol, smoking, sugar, or anything you want to change. It shows real-time progress and milestone achievements to keep you motivated.

🏆 Milestones

  • 1 day - First step!
  • 7 days - One week
  • 30 days - One month
  • 90 days - Three months
  • 180 days - Six months
  • 365 days - One year!
  • 500 days - 500 milestone
  • 1000 days - Four digits!

What is Sobriety Tracker - Track Days Sober / Smoke-/ Habit-?

Sobriety Tracker - Track Days Sober / Smoke-/ Habit- is a free online tool that runs entirely in your browser. No download or signup needed — enter What are you trackin and Start Date to get results instantly. All data is processed locally.

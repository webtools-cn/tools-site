📖 About PSA Density

PSA Density = serum PSA (ng/mL) ÷ prostate volume (mL). Normal: <0.15 ng/mL² = low risk; 0.15-0.25 = intermediate risk; >0.25 = high risk. PSAD is more specific than PSA alone.

⚠️ Important: This tool is for informational purposes only. Elevated PSA can occur with prostatitis and BPH. Consult your physician for diagnosis.

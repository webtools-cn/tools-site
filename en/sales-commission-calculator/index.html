📖 Commission Mode Guide

📐 Flat Rate

Simplest method: Sales × Rate = Commission. Suitable for most sales roles.

🪜 Tiered

Different rates apply based on total sales bracket. For example: up to 50k at 3%, 50k-100k at 5%, above 100k at 8%. The rate for the bracket that your total sales falls into applies to the entire amount.

📊 Progressive

Like progressive tax rates. Each bracket's rate applies only to the amount within that bracket. Example: first 50k at 3% ($1,500), next 50k at 5% ($2,500), excess at 8%. This is more favorable to salespeople.

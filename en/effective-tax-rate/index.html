The Effective Tax Rate Calculator is a free online tool that helps you quickly calculate your actual tax burden. Unlike the nominal tax rate, the effective tax rate reflects the actual percentage of your income that goes to taxes. The tool runs entirely in your browser — your data never leaves your device. Works on all major browsers and mobile devices. The effective tax rate is the most intuitive indicator for evaluating your personal tax burden. Unlike marginal tax rates, it reflects your overall tax level. Suitable for salaried employees, freelancers, and business owners for annual tax planning and analysis.

About FICO Score Simulator

The FICO score is the most widely used credit scoring model in the United States, ranging from 300 to 850. This simulator helps you understand how the five key factors contribute to your credit score: Payment History (35%), Credit Utilization (30%), Credit Age (15%), Credit Mix (10%), and New Inquiries (10%). By adjusting the sliders for each factor, you can simulate how different credit behaviors may impact your FICO score. This is a free educational tool for estimation purposes only; actual FICO scores may vary.

FAQ

What is a FICO Score?

The FICO (Fair Isaac Corporation) score is the most commonly used credit scoring system in the US, used by over 90% of lenders. Scores range from 300-850, with higher scores indicating lower credit risk.

What is the biggest factor in FICO scores?

Payment history accounts for 35%, making it the most important factor. Paying bills on time is crucial for maintaining a high score. Credit utilization (30%) is the second most important — it's recommended to keep it below 30%.

How accurate is this simulator?

This tool provides estimates based on the FICO scoring model for educational purposes only. Actual FICO scores are calculated using FICO's proprietary algorithm and may differ from estimates.

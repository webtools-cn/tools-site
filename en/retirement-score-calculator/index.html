About Retirement Readiness Score

Retirement Readiness Score is a free online tool. Assess retirement readiness with savings gap analysis and score Works on mobile and desktop, all calculations run locally in your browser for privacy.

How to Use

  1. Enter current age and planned retirement age
  2. Enter savings, monthly expenses and expected return rate
  3. Click "Calculate" to see your retirement readiness score

Is this tool accurate?

Results are computed with standard, industry-accepted formulas for Current Age and Target Retirement Ag calculations.

No, it runs entirely in your browser. No downloads needed.

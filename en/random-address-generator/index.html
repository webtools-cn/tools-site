Frequently Asked Questions

Are the generated addresses real?

Address formats are realistic but data is randomly generated. Cities and street names come from a preset database, but house numbers are random combinations — not real addresses.

Which countries are supported?

China, US, UK, Canada, Australia, Japan, South Korea, and more common country address formats.

What information is included?

Full details: name, street address, city, state/province, postal code, country, phone number, and more.

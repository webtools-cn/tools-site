About Biweekly Payment Calculator

Biweekly payments split your monthly payment in half and pay every 2 weeks. Since a year has 52 weeks = 26 biweekly periods = 13 monthly equivalents, you make one extra payment per year toward principal, significantly reducing interest and loan term. Ideal for salaried workers with biweekly paychecks.

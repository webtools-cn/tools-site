💡 Tipping Guide

Tipping culture by country:
• USA/Canada: 15%-20% is standard, check if service charge is included
• UK: 10%-15%, check bill for included service charge
• Japan/Korea: Tipping is generally not practiced and may offend
• Europe: Service charge often included; extra 5%-10% appreciated

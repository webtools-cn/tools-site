FAQ

Do I need to install anything?

No. The DSCR calculator is a pure front-end tool that runs directly in your browser.

What is DSCR?

DSCR (Debt Service Coverage Ratio) measures a property's ability to cover its debt. DSCR = NOI / Annual Debt Service. Banks typically require at least 1.20-1.25.

Is my data secure?

Completely secure. All data processing happens in your browser's local memory.

Can I use it on mobile?

Yes. This tool is optimized for mobile with responsive design.

How to Use the DSCR Calculator

Using the online DSCR calculator is simple:

  1. Enter NOI
    Enter Net Operating Income (annual revenue minus operating expenses).
  2. Enter debt service
    Enter annual debt service (total principal + interest payments per year).
  3. View DSCR
    See your DSCR ratio and risk assessment instantly.

FAQ

What is MELD score?

MELD (Model for End-Stage Liver Disease) is a scoring system used to assess chronic liver disease severity and prioritize liver transplantation. Scores range from 6 to 40; higher scores indicate worse prognosis.

How is MELD 3.0 different from MELD-Na?

MELD 3.0 (updated 2021) adds albumin and female sex adjustment, making it fairer for women and better at predicting waitlist mortality.

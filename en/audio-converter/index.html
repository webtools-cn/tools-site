❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data safe when using Audio Converter?

Absolutely. Everything runs locally: Audio Converter and other values are processed on your device, never sent to a server. No data is sent to any server, so there's zero risk of data leaks. Your information stays completely under your control.

Does Audio Converter upload my data to a server?

No. Audio Converter uses client-side processing — everything runs in your browser. Your data never leaves your device, and the tool works even without an internet connection.

Can I use Audio Converter on my phone?

Yes. Audio Converter is fully responsive and works on phones, tablets, and desktops. The interface adapts automatically to your screen size.

Are there any usage limits for Audio Converter?

Audio Converter has no usage limits — it's free and unlimited. Since processing is done client-side, speed depends on your device. For very large files, we recommend processing in batches for the best experience.

Is Audio Converter free to use?

Yes, Audio Converter is completely free to use. No registration or login required — all features are available immediately.

Are converted files compatible with other software?

Yes. Converted files follow standard format specifications and are compatible with mainstream software and platforms.

What audio formats are supported?

Common formats including MP3, WAV, OGG, AAC, and FLAC are supported.

What is Workdays Calculator?

Workdays Calculator is a free online tool that counts business days between two dates, automatically excluding weekends and optionally excluding custom holidays. All processing runs locally in your browser.

Use Cases

  • Calculate business days between two project milestones
  • Plan work schedules and track deadline availability
  • Exclude weekends and holidays for accurate time tracking
  • Verify payroll periods and employee attendance

Key Features

  • Automatically excludes weekends (Saturday & Sunday)
  • Custom holiday exclusion support
  • Instant calculation with no registration required
  • Privacy Safe: all data stays in your browser

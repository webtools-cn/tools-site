❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data safe when using Bitwise Calculator?

Absolutely. Everything runs locally: Operation and other values are processed on your device, never sent to a server. No data is sent to any server, so there's zero risk of data leaks. Your information stays completely under your control.

Is Bitwise Calculator free to use?

Yes, Bitwise Calculator is completely free to use. No registration or login required — all features are available immediately.

Do I need to install any software?

No. Bitwise Calculator is a web-based tool — just open it in your browser and start using it. No downloads, plugins, or extensions required.

Can I use Bitwise Calculator on my phone?

Yes. Bitwise Calculator is fully responsive and works on phones, tablets, and desktops. The interface adapts automatically to your screen size.

How accurate are the calculations?

Calculations use JavaScript's floating-point arithmetic with up to 15 significant digits of precision. For most practical purposes, this is more than sufficient.

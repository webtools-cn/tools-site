What is Countdown Clock?

Countdown Clock is a free online tool that runs entirely in your browser. Set a target date and time and the countdown updates in real time, perfect for events, deadlines, exams or reminders. No download or signup needed — all data is processed locally.

Use Cases

  • Count down to a big event, holiday or trip
  • Track time remaining for an exam or presentation
  • Set a reminder for a product launch or release date
  • Count down to a deadline while working or studying

Key Features

  • Completely Free: No registration, no limits
  • Privacy Safe: Data never leaves your browser
  • Cross-Platform: Any modern browser
  • Zero Dependencies: Open and use

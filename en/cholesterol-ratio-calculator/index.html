Why CholesterolRatio Matters More Than Individual Numbers?

Cholesterol ratio considers both HDL ("good" cholesterol) and LDL ("bad" cholesterol) together, providing a more comprehensive cardiovascular risk assessment than individual cholesterol values alone.

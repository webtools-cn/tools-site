FAQ

What's a good pace?

Recreational: 9-11 min/mile. Good: 8 min/mile. Excellent: under 6:30. Sub-3 marathon = 6:52 min/mile.

Treadmill vs road pace?

Treadmill: no wind + cushion, ~10-15 sec/mile faster. Road is harder but better training.

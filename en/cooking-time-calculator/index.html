The Cooking Time Calculator is a free online kitchen tool that helps you determine the perfect cooking time for any meat or ingredient. Whether you're a beginner cook or an experienced chef, precise timing is the key to delicious results. This calculator coves 8 common food types — beef, pork, chicken, lamb, fish, eggs, turkey, and duck — with 6 cooking methods: roast, boil, steam, pan-fry, deep-fry, and braise. Each combination provides exact cooking time based on weight, along with target internal temperature and professional tips. All calculations run locally in your browser — no app download needed. Pair with a food thermometer for consistently perfect results every time.

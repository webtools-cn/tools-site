💡 Exercise Tips

  • Beginners should start at 50%-60% intensity and gradually increase duration and effort
  • For fat loss, aim for the 60%-70% zone and sustain for 30-60 minutes per session
  • To improve cardiovascular fitness, train in the 70%-80% zone, 3-5 times per week
  • High-intensity training (80%+) should not exceed 20-30 minutes per session; allow adequate recovery
  • Stop immediately if you feel dizzy, chest pain, or other discomfort during exercise
  • Consult a doctor before starting an exercise program if you have heart disease, high blood pressure, or other chronic conditions

📏 How to Measure Exercise Heart Rate

  • Heart rate monitor / fitness watch: Most convenient for real-time tracking during exercise
  • Manual measurement: Stop exercising, count pulse for 15 seconds and multiply by 4
  • Resting heart rate: Measure first thing in the morning before getting up; average over 3 days
  • Exercise heart rate has a 5-10 second delay, so measure immediately after stopping

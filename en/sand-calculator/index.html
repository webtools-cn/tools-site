FAQ

What is the density of different sand types?

Fine sand has a density of about 1.44 t/m³, coarse sand about 1.60 t/m³, and builder's sand about 1.52 t/m³. Selecting the correct type improves accuracy.

How deep should sand be for paving?

For paver base, 2-4 inches (5-10cm) of sand is typical. For sandboxes, 12-20 inches. For mortar mix, 1-2 inches.

Should I account for waste?

This tool calculates theoretical amounts. For actual projects, add 5-10% extra for compaction, uneven surfaces, and spillage.

Is my data safe?

Completely safe. This is a pure client-side tool — all calculations happen in your browser and never leave your device.

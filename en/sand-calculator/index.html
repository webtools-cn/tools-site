FAQ

Sand weight per m³?

Dry: ~1.4-1.6 tons, wet: ~1.8-2.0. Construction: estimate 1.5 tons/m³.

How deep should sand be?

Paver base: 3-5cm, lawn: 5-10cm, sandbox: 30-50cm. Depends on use.

What is Sand Calculator?

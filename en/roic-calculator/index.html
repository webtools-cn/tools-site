📖 About ROIC

ROIC = NOPAT ÷ Invested Capital. Measures how efficiently a company uses all capital (equity + debt) to generate profits — a key moat indicator.

Benchmarks: >15% Excellent | 10-15% Good | <10% Needs Attention. ROIC > WACC means creating value.

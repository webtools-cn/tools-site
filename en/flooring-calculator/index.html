📋 Installation Tips

Laying Direction
  • Run planks parallel to the main light source (usually parallel to windows)
  • In hallways, lay planks along the walking direction for visual length
  • Keep the same direction across connected rooms for consistency
Purchasing Advice
  • Buy 1-2 extra boxes as spares — future batches may have color variations
  • Let flooring acclimate in the room for 48 hours before installation
  • Leave space for baseboards and transition strips

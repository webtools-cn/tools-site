What is Viewport Checker?

Viewport Checker is a free online tool that runs entirely in your browser. No download or signup needed — enter Preset Width and other values to get results instantly. All data is processed locally.

Use Cases

  • Verify mathematical calculations during study or teaching
  • Solve geometry problems quickly in engineering work
  • Calculate ratios and percentages in daily life
  • Mobile math calculations without a calculator app

Key Features

  • Completely Free: No registration, no limits
  • Privacy Safe: Data never leaves your browser
  • Cross-Platform: Any modern browser
  • Zero Dependencies: Open and use

Is My Data Safe?

All computation happens on your device — Screen Resolution Test, other values and other inputs stay in your browser. No data is uploaded to any server.

Why might results differ from actual resolution?

Browsers are affected by system scaling. DPR > 1 means scaling is enabled (e.g., Retina = 2).

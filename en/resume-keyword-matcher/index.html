The Resume Keyword Matcher is a free online job application tool that helps you quickly compare your resume against a job description (JD). Simply paste both texts, and the system automatically extracts skill keywords, industry terms, and core requirements, then calculates a match percentage and identifies missing keywords. By tailoring your resume based on the results, you can significantly improve your chances of passing ATS (Applicant Tracking System) screening and landing more interviews. All analysis is performed locally in your browser — your resume data is never uploaded to any server, ensuring complete privacy. Supports both English and Chinese resumes. No registration required — start using it immediately.

FAQ

What is EBITDA?

EBITDA (Earnings Before Interest, Tax, Depreciation and Amortization) is a core metric for measuring business profitability. It excludes interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization to reveal true operating earnings.

What is the difference between EBITDA and Net Income?

Net Income is final profit after all expenses. EBITDA = Net Income + Interest + Tax + Depreciation + Amortization. EBITDA removes capital structure and tax policy effects for better cross-company comparison.

What is EV/EBITDA?

EV/EBITDA is Enterprise Value divided by EBITDA, a common valuation multiple. 10-15x is typical; high-growth companies can be 20-30x; traditional industries 6-10x.

    What is Asset Allocation?

    Asset allocation is the strategy of dividing your investments across different asset classes — stocks, bonds, real estate, commodities, and cash — to balance risk and reward. This calculator helps visualize your portfolio composition, compute diversification metrics, and assess your risk profile.

    How to Use

    Click "Add Asset" to enter your holdings with dollar amounts and asset categories. Use the preset buttons for common allocations. The pie chart updates in real-time, and the risk profile shows how aggressive or conservative your portfolio is.

    FAQ

    Is my data safe? All calculations run locally in your browser. Data never uploads to any server.

    What asset categories are supported? Stocks, bonds, real estate, commodities, crypto, cash, and custom categories.

    How is risk calculated? Risk is estimated based on the proportion of high-risk assets (stocks, crypto) versus low-risk assets (bonds, cash).

How to Play

Left-click: Reveal a cell. If it's a mine, game over.

Right-click: Flag/unflag a suspected mine (🚩).

Numbers: Each number shows how many mines are adjacent to that cell (out of 8 surrounding cells).

Win condition: Reveal all non-mine cells to win.

Difficulty: Beginner 9×9 (10 mines), Intermediate 16×16 (40 mines), Expert 30×16 (99 mines), or Custom.

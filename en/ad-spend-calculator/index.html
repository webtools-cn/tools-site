❓ FAQ

What is CPM?

CPM (Cost Per Mille) is the cost per thousand impressions. Formula: CPM = (Total Ad Spend / Impressions) × 1000. CPM is the most common pricing model for brand awareness campaigns.

What's the difference between CPC and CPA?

CPC (Cost Per Click) = Total Ad Spend / Clicks. CPA (Cost Per Acquisition) = Total Ad Spend / Conversions. CPC measures click efficiency, while CPA measures final conversion effectiveness.

How can I reduce advertising costs?

Optimize ad creative to improve CTR, use precise audience targeting, A/B test different copy, improve landing page conversion rates, use retargeting, and bid during less competitive times or regions.

What is a good CPM/CPC benchmark?

Benchmarks vary: Facebook ads average CPM ~$7-10, CPC $0.5-2. Google Search CPC $1-3. B2B CPA may reach $50-200, while e-commerce CPA typically $10-50. Always compare against ROAS.

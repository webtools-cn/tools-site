Frequently Asked Questions

What formulas does this cylinder calculator use?

Volume = πr²h. Lateral Surface Area = 2πrh. Total Surface Area = 2πr(r+h). Base Area = πr². All calculations use the standard value of π for precise results.

What is lateral area vs total surface area?

Lateral surface area is the curved side of the cylinder (2πrh). Total surface area includes both circular bases plus the lateral area (2πr² + 2πrh).

Can I calculate tank capacity with this?

Yes! Enter the radius and height in your desired unit — the volume output can be directly interpreted as the tank's liquid capacity in cubic units.

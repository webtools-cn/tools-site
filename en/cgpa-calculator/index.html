Frequently Asked Questions

What is CGPA and how is it different from GPA?

CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average) is the weighted average of your grade points across all semesters. While GPA typically refers to a single semester's grade point average, CGPA combines all semesters using credit-weighted averaging. The formula is: CGPA = sum(grade points × credits for each course) ÷ sum(all credits).

What grading scales does this CGPA calculator support?

This tool supports three grading scales: Standard 4.0 (A=4.0, used in most US universities), Standard 5.0 (A=5.0, used in some Asian universities), and Percentage to 4.0 (automatically converts 0-100 scores to 4.0-scale grade points). Choose the one your institution uses.

How does the target CGPA planner work?

Switch to the Target Planner tab, enter your current CGPA, completed credits, target CGPA, remaining semesters, and credits per semester. The tool calculates the average GPA you need each remaining semester to reach your target — helping you set realistic academic goals.

Is my data uploaded to a server?

No. This tool runs entirely in your browser. All calculations happen on your device. Click "Save" to store data in browser localStorage for later use, but nothing is ever uploaded to any server.

How to Use the CGPA Calculator

CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average) is the core metric for evaluating academic performance across your entire degree program. Universities worldwide use it for degree classification, scholarship decisions, and graduate school admissions. Unlike a single-semester GPA, CGPA weights all your courses by credit hours, giving a comprehensive picture of your academic standing.

CGPA Calculation Formula

CGPA = sum(grade point × credits for each course) ÷ sum(all credits). For example: Semester 1 has three courses (Calculus 4 credits / GP 3.7, English 3 credits / GP 4.0, Physics 4 credits / GP 3.3), Semester 2 has three courses (Data Structures 3 credits / GP 3.7, Operating Systems 3 credits / GP 4.0, Probability 3 credits / GP 3.0). CGPA = (3.7×4 + 4.0×3 + 3.3×4 + 3.7×3 + 4.0×3 + 3.0×3) ÷ (4+3+4+3+3+3) = 62.7 ÷ 20 = 3.14.

Degree Classification

Different countries have different standards for CGPA-based degree classification. This tool uses common benchmarks: CGPA 3.5+ = First Class Honours, 3.0-3.49 = Second Class Upper, 2.5-2.99 = Second Class Lower, 2.0-2.49 = Third Class, below 2.0 = Pass degree. These align with UK, Malaysian, and Indian university standards.

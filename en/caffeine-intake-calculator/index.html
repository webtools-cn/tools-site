About Caffeine Intake Calculator

Caffeine Intake Calculator is a free online tool. Free caffeine intake calculator. Calculate your daily caffeine total based on drink type and cups, compare against 400mg daily safety limit for healthy adults. Works on mobile and desktop, all calculations run locally in your browser for privacy.

How to Use

  1. Select your drink type (e.g. Espresso, Brewed Coffee, Green Tea)
  2. Enter the number of cups consumed, optionally enter body weight for per-kg calculation
  3. Click "Calculate" to see total caffeine intake and safety assessment

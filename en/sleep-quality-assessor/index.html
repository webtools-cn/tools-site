FAQ

What is the PSQI sleep quality index?

The Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index (PSQI) is a globally recognized sleep assessment using 7 dimensions. Total score 0-21; ≤5 is good, >5 suggests improvement needed.

How to improve sleep quality?

Maintain regular schedule, avoid screens 1h before bed, keep bedroom cool and dark, avoid caffeine/alcohol, exercise moderately. Consult a doctor for chronic insomnia.

How much sleep do adults need?

Most adults need 7-9 hours. Individual variation is large — the key is waking up refreshed. Consistently <6h or >10h may increase health risks.

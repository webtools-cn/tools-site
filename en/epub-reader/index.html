❓ FAQ

What ebook formats can I read?

This tool is designed for EPUB format (.epub), the most common ebook format. EPUB files are ZIP archives containing HTML, CSS and images. We extract and render them directly in your browser.

Does it support DRM-protected EPUBs?

No. This reader can only open DRM-free EPUB files. If you purchased ebooks from Amazon Kindle or Apple Books with copyright protection, you'll need to remove DRM through official channels first.

Is my reading progress saved?

Yes! Progress is saved to your browser's localStorage. Reopening the same book will take you to where you left off. Dark/light theme preference is also remembered.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Does EPUB Online Reader upload my data to a server?

No. EPUB Online Reader uses client-side processing — everything runs in your browser. Your data never leaves your device, and the tool works even without an internet connection.

Are there any usage limits for EPUB Online Reader?

EPUB Online Reader has no usage limits — it's free and unlimited. Since processing is done client-side, speed depends on your device. For very large files, we recommend processing in batches for the best experience.

Is my data safe when using EPUB Online Reader?

Absolutely. All processing happens locally in your browser. No data is sent to any server, so there's zero risk of data leaks. Your information stays completely under your control.

Do I need to install any software?

No. EPUB Online Reader is a web-based tool — just open it in your browser and start using it. No downloads, plugins, or extensions required.

Is EPUB Online Reader free to use?

Yes, EPUB Online Reader is completely free to use. No registration or login required — all features are available immediately.

📖 How to Use

  • Enter a chemical formula like H2O or C6H12O6
  • Supports parentheses: Ca(OH)2, Fe2(SO4)3
  • Supports hydrates: CuSO4·5H2O (use · or * as separator)
  • Element symbols: first letter uppercase, second lowercase (e.g. Na not NA)
  • Result unit: g/mol
  • 100% client-side — data never leaves your browser

What is Molar Mass Calculator?

Molar Mass Calculator is a free online tool that runs entirely in your browser. No download or signup needed — enter e.g. H2O, Ca2, CuSO4 and other values to get results instantly. All data is processed locally.

Use Cases

  • Solve geometry problems quickly in engineering work
  • Calculate ratios and percentages in daily life
  • Mobile math calculations without a calculator app

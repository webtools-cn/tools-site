❓ FAQ
What is Planning Poker?
Planning Poker is a collaborative estimation technique used in agile development to estimate user story complexity. Team members each select a Fibonacci sequence card to represent their estimate, then reveal simultaneously, discuss differences, and reach consensus.
What card sequence does Planning Poker use?
Typically a modified Fibonacci sequence: 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 20, 40, 100, plus ? (uncertain) and ☕ (need a break).
How to use this online tool?
Enter participant names, each person takes turns selecting their estimate from the cards. After everyone has voted, results are revealed with average, median, range, and consensus analysis.
Is data uploaded to a server?
No. All data is processed locally in your browser only. Nothing is uploaded to any server, keeping your estimation data safe and private.

FAQ

How do you calculate a tip?

Tip = Bill Amount × Tip Percentage. For example, a $100 bill with 15% tip: tip = $100 × 0.15 = $15, total = $115.

What is the tipping culture in the US?

Tipping is deeply ingrained in US culture. Restaurants typically expect 15-20%, bartenders $1-2 per drink, taxis 15%, hotel bellhops $1-2 per bag. Not tipping is considered rude.

How do you split a tip among multiple people?

Calculate the total (bill + tip), then divide by the number of people. For example, $200 bill with 15% tip ($30) = $230 total, split among 3 people = $76.67 each.

What are tipping standards in different countries?

US 15-20%, Canada 15-18%, UK 10-15%, Japan no tipping (considered rude), China no mandatory tipping, Continental Europe 5-10%, Australia no mandatory tipping.

When should you not leave a tip?

You may skip tipping when: service is extremely poor, at buffets (service charge included), fast food counter service, in countries where tipping is not customary, or when a service charge is already included on the bill.

About Antenna Wavelength Calculator

Antenna design requires wavelength from frequency. Enter operating frequency for full, half, and quarter wavelengths to plan physical dimensions.

How to Use

  1. Enter frequency (MHz or GHz)
  2. Select frequency unit
  3. Click Calculate for all wavelengths

FAQ

Antenna length vs wavelength?

Dipole=½λ, ground plane=¼λ. Higher freq=shorter wavelength=smaller antenna.

5GHz WiFi antenna size?

5GHz λ≈6cm, ½λ≈3cm — very compact! 2.4GHz ½λ≈6.25cm.

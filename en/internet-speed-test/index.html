📖 About Internet Speed Test

What These Metrics Mean

Ping Latency: Round-trip time from your device to a server and back. Lower is better. Gaming and video calls need <50ms. <20ms Excellent, 20-50ms Good, 50-100ms Average, >100ms Poor.

Download Speed: How fast you receive data from the internet. Affects web browsing, video streaming, and file downloads. 100Mbps+ recommended for 4K streaming and large downloads.

Upload Speed: How fast you send data to the internet. Affects video conferencing, cloud backups, and live streaming. 20Mbps+ recommended for HD video calls.

Tips to Improve Speed

  • Use a wired Ethernet connection instead of Wi-Fi
  • Place your router in a central location, away from walls and metal objects
  • Regularly restart your router and modem
  • Check for background apps using bandwidth (auto-updates, cloud sync)
  • Use 5GHz band (faster but shorter range) instead of 2.4GHz
  • Upgrade to a Wi-Fi 6 or Wi-Fi 7 router

Common Broadband Plans Reference

100Mbps: 1-2 users, HD streaming + browsing | 300Mbps: Family, multiple devices | 500Mbps: Gaming/4K streaming | 1000Mbps (Gigabit): Heavy downloads, multi-user 4K, streaming

What is Business Valuation Calculator - Multi-Method Valuation?

Business Valuation Calculator - Multi-Method Valuation is a free online tool that runs entirely in your browser. No download or signup needed — enter Annual Net Profit and Annual Revenue to get results instantly. All data is processed locally.

Use Cases

  • Estimate a company's value for sale, purchase, or fundraising discussions
  • Compare your business against industry P/E, P/S, and EBITDA multiples
  • Quickly sanity-check valuations before consulting a professional advisor
  • Teach or study the fundamentals of multi-method business valuation

Key Features

  • Completely Free: No registration, no limits
  • Privacy Safe: Data never leaves your browser
  • Cross-Platform: Any modern browser
  • Zero Dependencies: Open and use

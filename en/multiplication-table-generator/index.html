FAQ

What is a multiplication table?

A multiplication table shows the products of numbers in a grid format. The row and column headers are the factors, and each cell contains their product.

How to memorize multiplication tables efficiently?

1) Master 1, 2, 5, and 10 first (easiest); 2) Use the commutative property (3×7 = 7×3); 3) Focus on commonly confused ones (6×7=42, 7×8=56); 4) Practice 5 minutes daily for 2 weeks.

What does the diagonal highlight mean?

Diagonal cells contain square numbers (1×1=1, 2×2=4, 3×3=9...). Highlighting them helps identify these special values.

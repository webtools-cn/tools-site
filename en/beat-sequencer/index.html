Online Beat Sequencer - Free Drum Machine Tool

The Online Beat Sequencer is a free, browser-based drum machine. It uses the Web Audio API to mix 8 drum sounds across a 16-step sequence with adjustable BPM, per-track volume control and ready-made presets. No installation is required — just open the page and start making beats. You can also export your sequence as a WAV audio file.

Key Features

  • 8 drum sounds: kick, snare, closed hi-hat, open hi-hat, clap, tom, rim, crash
  • 16-step sequencer with standard 4/4 time
  • Adjustable BPM from 60 to 200
  • Individual volume control for each track
  • 5 built-in presets for a quick start
  • Export your beat as a WAV file

Use Cases

  • Music production: sketch out drum patterns quickly
  • Education: learn rhythm and sequencing fundamentals
  • Demo recording: create backing beats for vocals or instruments
  • Content creation: add simple drum tracks to videos and podcasts

How to Use the Free Online Beat Sequencer?

Tap any step cell to toggle a drum sound on or off, choose a preset to get started, adjust the BPM slider, then press Play. Every track has its own volume control. All processing happens in your browser — nothing is uploaded.

Can I Export My Sequence as Audio?

Yes. Click the Export WAV button to render the current 16-step sequence into a standard WAV audio file and download it directly to your device.

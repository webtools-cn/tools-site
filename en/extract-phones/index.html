FAQ

Q1: What phone formats are supported?

Supports: US/Canada numbers ((555) 123-4567, 555-123-4567), international numbers (+country code), toll-free (1-800, 1-888, etc.), and mobile numbers. Various separators supported: spaces, hyphens, parentheses, dots.

Q2: How accurate is the extraction?

The tool uses regex patterns to match common phone formats with high accuracy, but not 100%. Some unusual formats may be missed, and similar digit sequences (like IDs) may be falsely matched. Manual review is recommended for important data.

Q3: Is my data safe?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser — your data is never sent to any server. No text or phone numbers are uploaded, and all data disappears when you close the page.

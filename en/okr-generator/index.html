📖 OKR Best Practices

  • Objectives should be ambitious: A good Objective excites and slightly scares you. Hitting 70% is success.
  • Key Results must be measurable: Every KR needs numbers – start value, target value, and unit.
  • 3-5 KRs per Objective: Too many dilutes focus; too few won't measure comprehensively.
  • Review regularly: Update KR progress weekly; evaluate Objective progress monthly.
  • Committed vs. Aspirational: Committed OKRs must hit 100%; aspirational OKRs succeed at 70%.

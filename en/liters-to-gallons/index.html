About Liters to Gallons

Liters to Gallons is a free online tool that converts between liters and US gallons in both directions. Commonly used for cooking, fuel calculations, and industrial fluid measurements, results are precise to 4 decimal places. Works on mobile and desktop, with all calculations performed locally in your browser for data privacy.

Liters to Gallons in 3 steps

  1. Enter a value in the "Liters (L)" field or the "US Gallons" field
  2. Click the "Calculate" button
  3. The other field will automatically display the converted result, with a notification at the bottom

Is this tool accurate?

The calculation uses the standard conversion formula (1 US gallon = 3.78541 liters), ensuring precise and reliable results.

Do I need to download anything?

No download needed. Simply open the page and start converting. All calculations run in your browser.

📖 Churn Rate Guide

  • Customer Churn = Churned Customers ÷ Starting Customers × 100%
  • Annual Churn = 1 - (1 - Monthly Churn)^12
  • Revenue Churn = (Start MRR + New MRR - End MRR) ÷ Start MRR
  • NRR = (Start MRR + Expansion MRR - Churned MRR) ÷ Start MRR × 100%
  • Avg Lifetime = 1 ÷ Monthly Churn Rate (months)
  • Monthly churn < 2% is good, > 5% is a red flag

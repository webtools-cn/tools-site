📖 About Child BMI

Child BMI uses percentiles instead of fixed thresholds. Based on WHO growth standards: 5th-85th percentile is healthy, 85th-95th is overweight, >95th is obesity.

⚠️ BMI cannot distinguish muscle from fat. Not for children under 2. Results are for reference only — consult a pediatrician.

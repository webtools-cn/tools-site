How to Use the 🥧 Pie Chart Maker

Using the 🥧 Pie Chart Maker is quick and easy. Here's how it works:

  1. Enter Your Data
    Start by entering labels and values in the table. The tool processes everything locally in your browser — no data is uploaded to any server.
  2. Customize Settings
    Adjust labels, values, and colors, then click "Update Chart" to render your pie chart instantly.
  3. Export as PNG
    Click "Export PNG" to download the chart for reports and presentations. Completely free, with no limits.

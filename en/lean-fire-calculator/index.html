📘 What is Lean FIRE?

Lean FIRE is the most minimalist FIRE strategy, targeting annual expenses under $40,000. It's for those who want financial independence as quickly as possible by minimizing lifestyle costs.

FIRE Comparison

  • Lean FIRE: Minimalist living, fastest to achieve
  • Fat FIRE: Maintains higher lifestyle
  • Coast FIRE: Stop saving at tipping point, let compound interest work

How It's Calculated

Target Assets = Annual Expenses / Safe Withdrawal Rate. E.g., $30,000 expenses at 4% SWR = $750,000. At a more conservative 3.5%, you'd need ~$857,000.

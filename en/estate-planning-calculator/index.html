About Estate Planning

Estate planning ensures your wealth passes according to your wishes. It includes wills, trusts, healthcare directives, and tax strategies.

2024 Federal Estate Tax Key Numbers

  • •Individual exemption: $13,610,000
  • •Married couple exemption: $27,220,000
  • •Top federal rate: 40%
  • •⚠️ Exemption may drop to ~$7M in 2026

Do I need a trust?

Consider a trust if assets exceed the exemption or you have special distribution needs. Even with smaller estates, a revocable living trust can avoid probate.

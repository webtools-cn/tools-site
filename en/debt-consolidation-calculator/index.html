FAQ

What is debt consolidation?

Debt consolidation combines multiple high-interest debts (credit cards, personal loans) into a single lower-interest loan, reducing your monthly payment and total interest paid.

Does consolidation always save money?

It saves money if the new rate is significantly lower than your current weighted average APR. However, extending the term may increase total interest. This calculator helps you compare.

What fees are involved?

Possible fees include origination fees, appraisal fees, and prepayment penalties. This calculator supports adding a fee for accurate cost comparison.

How many debts can I enter?

Up to 6 debts at once, covering most real-world scenarios.

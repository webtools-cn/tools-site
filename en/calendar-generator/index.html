📖 How to Use

1. Select Date: Choose the year and month from the dropdowns.

2. Generate: Click "Generate Calendar" to create the monthly view.

3. Export/Print: Download as PNG image or print directly from your browser.

❓ FAQ

How do I generate a calendar?

Select the year and month, then click "Generate Calendar". The system automatically calculates every day and its weekday, producing a clean monthly calendar view with weekends highlighted.

Can I export as an image?

Yes! Click "Export PNG" to render the calendar as a high-quality PNG image using Canvas. Generate the calendar first, then export.

Does it support lunar calendar?

Currently shows Gregorian calendar with weekend highlighting. Lunar calendar and holidays will be added in future updates.

Can I print it?

Yes! Click the "Print" button to use your browser's print function. You can also export as PNG first, then print the image.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How good is the conversion quality?

Our tool excels with logos, flat icons, and simple illustrations. For photographic images, try reducing the color count to 16 or fewer for cleaner results. The output is true vector — it scales to any size without losing sharpness.

How do I choose the right number of colors?

Fewer colors produce smaller, cleaner SVGs. Logos and icons: 8-32 colors. Simple shapes: 4-8 colors. Complex illustrations: try 32-64 colors. We recommend starting with 16 and adjusting from there.

Are my images safe and private?

100% private. Everything runs locally in your browser using JavaScript. Your images are never uploaded to any server — we never see your files.

Why does my photo look bad after conversion?

Photos contain thousands of colors and gradients. Vectorizing them creates massive files with poor results. This tool is designed for graphics — logos, icons, and flat illustrations. For photos, try reducing colors to 16 or fewer, but expect tradeoffs.

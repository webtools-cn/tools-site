📘 About Reverse Mortgages

A reverse mortgage allows homeowners 62+ to convert home equity into cash without monthly payments. The loan is repaid when you sell, move out, or pass away.

Key Factors

  • Age: Older borrowers can access a higher percentage of equity
  • Home Value: Higher value = more available equity
  • Interest Rate: Affects loan balance growth and borrowing limit

Important Considerations

  • You must still pay property taxes and insurance
  • Loan balance grows with compound interest
  • May reduce inheritance for your heirs

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is an ATS system?▼
ATS (Applicant Tracking System) is software used by over 90% of large companies to automatically screen resumes. It scans for keywords, format, and structure to decide whether to forward your resume to a hiring manager.
Why would my resume get rejected by ATS?▼
Common reasons include: missing keywords matching the job description, using tables or images (ATS can't read them), complex multi-column layouts, missing standard headings (like "Work Experience"), or incompatible file formats.
How can I improve my ATS score?▼
1) Use standard headings (Work Experience, Education, Skills); 2) Include keywords from the job description; 3) Use a simple single-column layout; 4) Avoid tables, images, and charts; 5) Use standard fonts; 6) Submit in PDF or Word format.
Can this tool replace human resume review?▼
No. This tool simulates common ATS system checks to help you identify issues that could cause automatic rejection. Final review is still done by recruiters, so continue optimizing your content after passing ATS checks.
Is my data secure?▼
Absolutely. All analysis runs locally in your browser — your resume content is never uploaded to any server. Data is automatically cleared when you close the page.

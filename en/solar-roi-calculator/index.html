📖 Understanding Solar Investment

Residential solar systems typically25years+ year lifespan，Payback Periodapprox.6-10years。federalITCtax credit covers30% of system cost，significantly shortens payback。

Key ROI Factors

  • Installation Cost: US average $2.5-$3.5/W. Lower cost = faster payback.
  • Local Electricity Rate: Higher rates (e.g., CA $0.30+/kWh) make solar more attractive.
  • Sunlight Conditions: Southwest gets 300+ sunny days/year for maximum efficiency.
  • Net Metering: Whether you can sell excess power back to the grid.
  • Federal/State Incentives: ITC + state rebates can cover 40-50% of costs.

State Solar Friendliness

  • Best: CA, AZ, NV - high rates + strong sun
  • Good: TX, FL, NM - strong sun + moderate rates
  • Average: NY, MA - less sun but high rates + good incentives
  • Poor: WA, OR - low sun + low rates

❓ FAQ

How long do solar systems last?

Most panels have 25-30 year performance warranties, typically retaining 80-85% efficiency after 25 years. Inverters last 10-15 years and need mid-life replacement.

Can renters install solar?

Generally no. But consider Community Solar programs — buy or lease shares of nearby solar farms for similar savings.

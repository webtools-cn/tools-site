❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use URL Unshortener on my phone?

Yes. URL Unshortener is fully responsive and works on phones, tablets, and desktops. The interface adapts automatically to your screen size.

Is URL Unshortener free to use?

Yes, URL Unshortener is completely free to use. No registration or login required — all features are available immediately.

Does URL Unshortener upload my data to a server?

No. URL Unshortener uses client-side processing — everything runs in your browser. Your data never leaves your device, and the tool works even without an internet connection.

Is my data safe when using URL Unshortener?

Absolutely. Everything runs locally: URL Unshortener and other values are processed on your device, never sent to a server. No data is sent to any server, so there's zero risk of data leaks. Your information stays completely under your control.

About Roman Numerals

Basic Rules

  • Roman numerals use 7 basic symbols: I(1), V(5), X(10), L(50), C(100), D(500), M(1000)
  • The same symbol can appear at most three times (e.g., III=3, but 4 is IV not IIII)
  • A smaller number before a larger means subtraction (IV=4, IX=9, XL=40, XC=90, CD=400, CM=900)
  • A smaller number after a larger means addition (VI=6, LX=60, DC=600)
  • Arranged left to right, largest to smallest

Common Examples

  • 2024 = MMXXIV (M+M+X+X+IV = 1000+1000+10+10+4)
  • 1987 = MCMLXXXVII (M+CM+L+X+X+X+V+I+I)
  • 3999 = MMMCMXCIX (maximum standard Roman numeral)

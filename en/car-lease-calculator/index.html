❓ FAQ

Q: How to convert money factor to APR?

A: APR ≈ Money Factor × 2400. For example, 0.00125 × 2400 = 3%.

Q: Can I buy the car at the end of the lease?

A: Most lease contracts allow you to purchase the vehicle at the residual value at lease end. Check your specific lease terms.

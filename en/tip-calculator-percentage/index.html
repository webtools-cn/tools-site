🌍 Tipping Culture by Country

  • United States 🇺🇸: Restaurants 15%-20%, bars $1-2/drink, taxis 10%-15%
  • Canada 🇨🇦: Similar to the US, 15%-20% is standard
  • United Kingdom 🇬🇧: Bills often include 12.5% service charge; extra tip is optional
  • Japan 🇯🇵: No tipping; it may be considered rude
  • China 🇨🇳: Generally no tipping; high-end restaurants may charge 10%-15% service fee
  • France 🇫🇷: Bills include 15% service charge; leaving change is common

FAQ

What percentage should I tip?

In the United States, restaurant tips are typically 15%-20% of the bill. Tip 20%+ for excellent service, 15% for average, and 10% for below average. Fast food and buffets generally don't require tipping.

Can this calculator split the bill?

Yes. Enter the number of diners and the calculator automatically computes each person's share including tip and tax.

Is tip calculated before or after tax?

By convention, tip should be calculated on the pre-tax amount. However, post-tax tipping is also widely accepted. This tool supports both methods by letting you input the tax rate.

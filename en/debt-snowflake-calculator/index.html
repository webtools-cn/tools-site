📖 About Debt Repayment Strategies

Snowball vs. Avalanche vs. Snowflake

  • Snowball: Pay off the smallest balance first. Gives you psychological wins and momentum — great if you need motivation.
  • Avalanche: Pay off the highest interest rate first. Mathematically saves the most money on interest.
  • Snowflake: Apply occasional small extra payments (from side income, refunds, bonuses, etc.) on top of regular payments to accelerate debt freedom.

Why Use the Snowflake Method?

The core idea of the snowflake method is "use every available dollar to pay off debt faster." Each seemingly tiny extra payment ($5–$50) adds up significantly over time. For example: an extra $50/month means $600/year directly reducing principal and interest.

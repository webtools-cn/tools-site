🎮 How to Play Bingo

Each player gets a bingo card. The host randomly draws items (numbers or words), and players mark matching squares on their cards. The first player to complete a horizontal, vertical, or diagonal line shouts "Bingo!" and wins. In 5×5 grids, the center square is usually "FREE" (pre-marked).

💡 Use Cases

• Classroom Teaching: Use word bingo to review vocabulary. Students hear a word and mark it on their cards — learning through play.

• Team Building: "Coworker Bingo" — fill squares with traits like "wears glasses" or "has a pet" for a great icebreaker.

• Party Games: Number bingo or themed bingo adds interactive fun to any party.

• Holiday Events: Christmas, Halloween themed bingo with holiday-related words and emojis.

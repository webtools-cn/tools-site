📖 Formulas Used

This calculator uses the following formulas:

  • Epley: 1RM = Weight × (1 + Reps/30)
  • Brzycki: 1RM = Weight × 36 / (37 - Reps)
  • Lander: 1RM = 100 × Weight / (101.3 - 2.67123 × Reps)
  • Average: Average of the three formulas above

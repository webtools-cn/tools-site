About ARM vs Fixed Rate Calculator

An ARM (Adjustable-Rate Mortgage) offers a lower fixed rate for the first N years, then adjusts to market rates. This calculator simulates the payment jump and total cost change when the fixed period ends and rates rise, helping you decide if an ARM is worth it.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is Audio Normalize free to use?

Yes, Audio Normalize is completely free to use. No registration or login required — all features are available immediately.

Is my data safe when using Audio Normalize?

Absolutely. Everything runs locally: Target Peak and other values are processed on your device, never sent to a server. No data is sent to any server, so there's zero risk of data leaks. Your information stays completely under your control.

Can I use Audio Normalize on my phone?

Yes. Audio Normalize is fully responsive and works on phones, tablets, and desktops. The interface adapts automatically to your screen size.

Are there any usage limits for Audio Normalize?

Audio Normalize has no usage limits — it's free and unlimited. Since processing is done client-side, speed depends on your device. For very large files, we recommend processing in batches for the best experience.

Does Audio Normalize upload my data to a server?

No. Audio Normalize uses client-side processing — everything runs in your browser. Your data never leaves your device, and the tool works even without an internet connection.

What audio formats are supported?

Common formats including MP3, WAV, OGG, AAC, and FLAC are supported.

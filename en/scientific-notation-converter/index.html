📖 About Scientific Notation

Scientific Notation Format

Standard Format: a × 10^n, where 1 ≤ |a| < 10
E Notation: aEn or aen
Programming Format: exponential notation (e.g. 1.5e6, 2.7e-9)

Scientific Notation in Programming

JavaScript, Python, C, Java, and other major languages all support E notation: 1.5e6 === 1500000, 2.5e-3 === 0.0025. Note float precision limits; big number calculations may produce different results.

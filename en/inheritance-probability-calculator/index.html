What is Inheritance Probability Calculator?

Inheritance Probability Calculator is a free online tool that runs entirely in your browser. No download or signup needed — enter Your Age and Parents' Average Age to get results instantly. All data is processed locally.

  • Compare conservative and optimistic inheritance scenarios

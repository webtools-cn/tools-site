📖 SaaS Metrics Guide

  • MRR: Monthly Recurring Revenue | ARR: MRR × 12
  • ARPU: MRR ÷ Paying Customers
  • CAC: Monthly S&M Cost ÷ New Customers/Month
  • LTV: ARPU × Gross Margin ÷ Monthly Churn Rate
  • LTV:CAC: Healthy > 3x; < 1x is a red flag
  • NRR: (MRR + Expansion MRR - Churned MRR) ÷ MRR × 100%
  • CAC Payback: CAC ÷ (ARPU × Gross Margin), healthy < 12 months

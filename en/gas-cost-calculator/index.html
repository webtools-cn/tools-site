FAQ

How is cost per km calculated?

Cost per km = Fuel Economy (L/100km) ÷ 100 × Fuel Price. Example: 8L/100km × $1.2/L = $0.096/km.

What affects fuel consumption?

1) Driving habits (aggressive driving increases consumption 20-30%); 2) Traffic conditions; 3) Vehicle load; 4) Tire pressure; 5) AC usage; 6) Maintenance condition.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Are there any usage limits for Gif To Webp?

Gif To Webp has no usage limits — it's free and unlimited. Since processing is done client-side, speed depends on your device. For very large files, we recommend processing in batches for the best experience.

Is Gif To Webp free to use?

Yes, Gif To Webp is completely free to use. No registration or login required — all features are available immediately.

Can I use Gif To Webp on my phone?

Yes. Gif To Webp is fully responsive and works on phones, tablets, and desktops. The interface adapts automatically to your screen size.

Is my data safe when using Gif To Webp?

Absolutely. Everything runs locally: Gif To Webp and other values are processed on your device, never sent to a server. No data is sent to any server, so there's zero risk of data leaks. Your information stays completely under your control.

Does Gif To Webp upload my data to a server?

No. Gif To Webp uses client-side processing — everything runs in your browser. Your data never leaves your device, and the tool works even without an internet connection.

Do I need to install any software?

No. Gif To Webp is a web-based tool — just open it in your browser and start using it. No downloads, plugins, or extensions required.

What image formats are supported?

Common formats including PNG, JPEG, WebP, GIF, BMP, and SVG are supported. The exact formats depend on the specific tool.

Will image quality decrease after processing?

For lossless formats (like PNG), quality is preserved. For lossy formats (like JPEG), you can choose the output quality.

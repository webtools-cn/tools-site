Plan Tier Guide

Bronze: Lowest premiums, highest deductibles — best for young, healthy individuals. Silver: Moderate premiums — good for average families. Gold: Higher premiums, lower deductibles — good for regular medical needs. Platinum: Highest premiums, lowest deductibles — best for high medical usage.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Does Yaml To Xml upload my data to a server?

No. Yaml To Xml uses client-side processing — everything runs in your browser. Your data never leaves your device, and the tool works even without an internet connection.

Do I need to install any software?

No. Yaml To Xml is a web-based tool — just open it in your browser and start using it. No downloads, plugins, or extensions required.

Can I use Yaml To Xml on my phone?

Yes. Yaml To Xml is fully responsive and works on phones, tablets, and desktops. The interface adapts automatically to your screen size.

Is my data safe when using Yaml To Xml?

Absolutely. Everything runs locally: Root element and Indent are processed on your device, never sent to a server. No data is sent to any server, so there's zero risk of data leaks. Your information stays completely under your control.

Are converted files compatible with other software?

Yes. Converted files follow standard format specifications and are compatible with mainstream software and platforms.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data safe when using Banner Generator?

Absolutely. Everything runs locally: Preset Sizes and Width are processed on your device, never sent to a server. No data is sent to any server, so there's zero risk of data leaks. Your information stays completely under your control.

Do I need to install any software?

No. Banner Generator is a web-based tool — just open it in your browser and start using it. No downloads, plugins, or extensions required.

Is Banner Generator free to use?

Yes, Banner Generator is completely free to use. No registration or login required — all features are available immediately.

Are there any usage limits for Banner Generator?

Banner Generator has no usage limits — it's free and unlimited. Since processing is done client-side, speed depends on your device. For very large files, we recommend processing in batches for the best experience.

Does Banner Generator upload my data to a server?

No. Banner Generator uses client-side processing — everything runs in your browser. Your data never leaves your device, and the tool works even without an internet connection.

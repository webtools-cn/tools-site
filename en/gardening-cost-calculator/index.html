📖 How to Use

This gardening cost calculator analyzes the ROI of growing your own food:

Step 1: Enter Garden Size

In square feet. Balcony gardens are typically 30-50 sq ft, yard gardens 100-300 sq ft.

Step 2: Set Growing Season

Typically 4-8 months for spring/summer. Adjust based on your climate.

Step 3: Enter All Costs

Seeds/seedlings, soil & fertilizer, tools (one-time), and monthly water cost increase.

Step 4: Set Expected Harvest Value

Estimate the retail value of vegetables/flowers you'll harvest this season.

Step 5: View ROI Analysis

See total cost, net return, and ROI percentage to decide if it's worth the investment.

💡 High-ROI Vegetables

  • Cherry Tomatoes: 3 plants yield 20-30 lbs, grocery price $3-5/lb, ROI can exceed 300%
  • Salad Greens: Fast 30-day harvest, cut-and-come-again, virtually pest-free
  • Herbs: Basil, mint, cilantro cost $3-5 per tiny pack at stores - grow for pennies
  • Beans: High yield, easy maintenance, among the most productive per square foot

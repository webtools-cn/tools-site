📖 About Peak Flow

What is PEF?

Peak Expiratory Flow measures the maximum speed of exhalation (L/min). It's vital for daily asthma monitoring and reflects large airway function.

Finding personal best

Measure 2-3 times daily for 2-3 weeks when well-controlled. Use the highest value as your personal best. This calculator provides a predicted normal based on demographics.

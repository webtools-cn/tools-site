Frequently Asked Questions

Is PPT to PDF free to use?▼
Yes, PPT to PDF is completely free to use. No registration or login required — all features are available immediately.
Will the PDF layout match the PPT?▼
This tool extracts text content from .pptx files and arranges it by slide order. Images, charts, and complex animations are not preserved. Each slide occupies one page in the PDF.
What PPT formats are supported?▼
.pptx format only (PowerPoint 2007 and later). Older .ppt format is not supported.
Is there a file size limit?▼
Files up to 50MB are recommended for smooth client-side processing. Larger files may cause browser slowdown.
Does PPT to PDF upload my data to a server?▼
No. PPT to PDF uses client-side processing — everything runs in your browser. Your data never leaves your device.

💡 Paycheck Calculation Notes

How it works:
• Regular pay = Hourly Rate × Hours/Week × 4.33
• Overtime pay = Hourly Rate × Overtime Rate × Overtime Hours × 4.33
• Monthly Gross = Regular Pay + Overtime Pay
• Annual Pay = Monthly × 12
• Net Pay = Gross × (1 - Tax Rate%)

How accurate is this?
This is standard sun sign astrology based on Gregorian calendar dates. For more precise analysis, birth time and location are needed for rising and moon signs.
What's the difference between zodiac and Chinese zodiac?
Western zodiac is based on the Sun's position through constellations. Chinese zodiac follows the lunar year cycle. They are different cultural systems.

❓ FAQ

How many L/min in 1 GPM?

1 GPM (US gallon per minute) = 3.78541 L/min. A typical US shower head flows at 2.5 GPM ≈ 9.46 L/min.

How to convert CFM to m³/h?

1 CFM = 1.699 m³/h. US ventilation uses CFM; Europe uses m³/h. For HVAC sizing, 400 CFM per ton of cooling.

What's the difference between volumetric flow and mass flow?

Volumetric flow (m³/s, GPM) measures volume per time. Mass flow (kg/s, lb/h) measures mass per time = volumetric flow × fluid density.

What is bbl/d?

Barrel per day is the standard oil production unit. 1 barrel = 42 US gallons ≈ 159 liters. Saudi Arabia produces ~10 million bbl/d.

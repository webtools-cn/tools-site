📖 About Sleep Cycles

A complete sleep cycle is approximately 90 minutes (1.5 hours), including light sleep, deep sleep, and REM. Waking naturally at the end of a cycle leaves you refreshed; waking during deep sleep causes grogginess. Aim for 5-6 cycles per night (7.5-9 hours).

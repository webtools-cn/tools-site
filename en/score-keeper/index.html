How to Use

Add Players: Enter a name and click "Add Player" or press Enter.

Scoring: Use the +/- buttons next to each player to adjust scores by ±1.

Remove Player: Click the ✕ button to remove a player and their score.

Rankings: Players are automatically ranked by score from highest to lowest.

Use Cases: Board game scoring, sports matches, classroom competitions, trivia nights, and more.

FAQ

What's the difference between mmol/L and mg/dL?

mmol/L (millimoles per liter) is the international standard used in most countries. mg/dL (milligrams per deciliter) is used in the US and a few others. Conversion: 1 mmol/L = 18.018 mg/dL.

What does HbA1c measure?

HbA1c (glycated hemoglobin) reflects your average blood glucose over the past 2-3 months. It's the gold standard for diabetes diagnosis and long-term management — unaffected by a single meal.

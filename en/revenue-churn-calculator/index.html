A free online revenue churn calculator designed for SaaS and subscription businesses. Enter starting MRR, ending MRR, and new MRR added to automatically calculate gross revenue churn, net revenue retention, and monthly churn rate. Monitor your business health in real time and identify revenue leakage trends — an essential tool for CFOs and operations teams performing financial analysis.

📖 What is Revenue Churn?

Revenue churn rate is a critical metric for measuring SaaS business health, reflecting the percentage of revenue lost due to customer downgrades, cancellations, or churn. Healthy SaaS companies typically keep monthly revenue churn below 3%. Net Revenue Retention (NRR) above 100% means expansion revenue from existing customers fully offsets churned revenue — one of the most important metrics investors track. Regular churn monitoring helps customer success teams identify at-risk accounts before they cancel.

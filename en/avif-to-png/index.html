❓ FAQ

Why convert AVIF to PNG?

AVIF is a next-gen image format with excellent compression, but some older browsers and software don't support it. Converting to PNG gives you maximum compatibility—PNG works on all platforms, browsers, and image editors.

Will conversion lose quality?

No. PNG is a lossless format. Converting from AVIF to PNG preserves all visible pixel information without further compression or quality loss. However, PNG files are typically larger than AVIF because AVIF uses more advanced compression.

Is batch conversion supported? Is it safe?

Yes! Select multiple AVIF files for batch conversion. All operations are done locally in your browser via Canvas API—images never leave your device, ensuring data security and privacy.

How to Calculate Percentages

What Is a Percentage?

A percentage represents a fraction of 100. The word comes from Latin "per centum" meaning "by the hundred." 1% = 1/100 = 0.01. Percentages are everywhere — from sale discounts and restaurant tips to stock market changes, test scores, and tax rates.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate a percentage of a number?

Multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. For example, to find 15% of 200: 200 × 15 ÷ 100 = 30. You can also think of it as 200 × 0.15. Our calculator handles this in real-time — just type and the answer appears.

How do I work out what percentage one number is of another?

Divide the smaller (part) by the larger (whole) and multiply by 100. For example: 45 out of 300 as a percentage = (45 ÷ 300) × 100 = 15%. This is perfect for test scores, budget tracking, and data analysis.

How is percentage increase or decrease calculated?

Use the formula: (new − old) ÷ |old| × 100. Positive = increase, negative = decrease. Example: your salary goes from $50,000 to $55,000 — that's a 10% raise. If your portfolio drops from $10,000 to $8,500, that's a 15% loss.

How does the tip calculator work?

Enter your bill total, choose a tip percentage (15-20% is standard in the US), and optionally the number of people splitting the bill. You'll see the tip amount, total with tip, and per-person share. Default suggestion is 18% — the most common US restaurant tip rate.

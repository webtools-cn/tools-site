About Final Grade Calculator

The Final Grade Calculator helps students determine the minimum score they need on their final exam to achieve their target overall grade. Enter your current grade, your desired final grade, and the weight of the final exam to instantly see what you need to score. The calculator also shows your safety margin. All calculations run locally in your browser.

Final Grade Calculator in 3 steps

  1. Enter your current grade (%)
  2. Enter your target overall grade (%)
  3. Enter the final exam weight (%)
  4. Click "Calculate" to see the score you need

Is this tool accurate?

The calculation uses the standard weighted grade formula, results are precise.

Do I need to download anything?

No download needed — it runs entirely in your browser.

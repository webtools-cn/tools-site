❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to install any software?

No. Time Zone Converter is a web-based tool — just open it in your browser and start using it. No downloads, plugins, or extensions required.

Is Time Zone Converter free to use?

Yes, Time Zone Converter is completely free to use. No registration or login required — all features are available immediately.

Does Time Zone Converter upload my data to a server?

No. Time Zone Converter uses client-side processing — everything runs in your browser. Your data never leaves your device, and the tool works even without an internet connection.

Are there any usage limits for Time Zone Converter?

Time Zone Converter has no usage limits — it's free and unlimited. Since processing is done client-side, speed depends on your device. For very large files, we recommend processing in batches for the best experience.

Is my data safe when using Time Zone Converter?

Absolutely. Everything runs locally: Source Time Zone and Convert Time are processed on your device, never sent to a server. No data is sent to any server, so there's zero risk of data leaks. Your information stays completely under your control.

Can I use Time Zone Converter on my phone?

Yes. Time Zone Converter is fully responsive and works on phones, tablets, and desktops. The interface adapts automatically to your screen size.

Are converted files compatible with other software?

Yes. Converted files follow standard format specifications and are compatible with mainstream software and platforms.

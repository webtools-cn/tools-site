About the EV Charging Time Calculator

The EV Charging Time Calculator is a free online tool that estimates charging time based on battery capacity, current charge level, and charger power. It works on phones and computers, all calculations run locally in your browser, and your data is never uploaded.

How to Use

  1. Enter battery capacity
  2. Enter current charge and charger power
  3. Click "Calculate" to see the result

Is this tool accurate?

The calculation is based on standard formulas, giving a reliable estimate for planning.

Do I need to download anything?

No. Open the page in your browser and it works immediately. It is a pure frontend calculator.

📘 What is Fat FIRE?

Fat FIRE is financial independence without lifestyle sacrifice. Target annual expenses of $100,000+, requiring more assets than traditional FIRE but allowing you to retire early while maintaining a comfortable or even luxurious lifestyle.

FIRE Comparison

  • Fat FIRE: $100k+/yr lifestyle, $3M+ target, longest timeline
  • Lean FIRE: Under $40k/yr, fastest to achieve
  • Coast FIRE: Reach tipping point, stop saving, let compounding work

Why a More Conservative Withdrawal Rate?

Fat FIRE retirements are typically longer (40-50 years) and involve larger portfolios where market volatility has bigger impact. A 3-3.5% withdrawal rate is recommended instead of the standard 4%.

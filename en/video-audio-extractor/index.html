FAQ

Q1: What video formats are supported?

Supports browser-playable video formats including MP4, WebM, and OGG. Specific support depends on your browser — Chrome and Firefox support the widest range of formats.

Q2: What is the audio quality of the extraction?

Audio quality depends on the original video's audio track and your chosen output bitrate. Default 128kbps is suitable for most uses, 320kbps provides near-CD quality, and WAV is lossless.

Q3: Is my video uploaded to a server?

No. All processing happens in your browser using the Web Audio API and MediaRecorder. Your video file never leaves your device, ensuring complete privacy.

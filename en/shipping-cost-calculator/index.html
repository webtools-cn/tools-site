FAQ

What is volumetric weight?

Volumetric (dimensional) weight = L×W×H÷5000 (cm³/kg). Carriers charge whichever is higher: actual weight or volumetric weight. Large, lightweight packages (like pillows) are charged by volume.

Why is international shipping so expensive?

International shipping includes customs duties, fuel surcharges, and remote area fees. Asian routes are relatively affordable, Europe/North America is moderate, and South America/Africa is the most expensive. Sea freight can dramatically reduce costs for bulk shipments.

How accurate are these estimates?

These are estimates based on public carrier rate tables, providing reference ranges. Actual costs may vary ±20% due to promotions, customer tiers, and fuel surcharges. Always confirm with official carrier quotes.

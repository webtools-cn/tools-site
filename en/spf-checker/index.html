❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data safe when using SPF Record Checker?

Absolutely. Everything runs locally: the domain name you enter is processed on your device, never sent to a server. Your information stays completely under your control.

Does SPF Record Checker upload my data to a server?

No. SPF Record Checker uses DNS over HTTPS — your browser queries public DNS directly. The tool itself does not store or transmit your data.

Can I use SPF Record Checker on my phone?

Yes. SPF Record Checker is fully responsive and works on phones, tablets, and desktops.

Are there any usage limits?

No limits — it's free and unlimited. Processing is done via DNS over HTTPS.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many seconds are in one hour?

1 hour = 60 minutes = 3,600 seconds. 1 day = 86,400 seconds, 1 week = 604,800 seconds.

What are milliseconds, microseconds, and nanoseconds?

1 second = 1,000 milliseconds (ms), 1 millisecond = 1,000 microseconds (μs), 1 microsecond = 1,000 nanoseconds (ns). Nanoseconds are commonly used for computer clock cycles — a 1 GHz CPU cycle = 1 nanosecond.

How many seconds are in a month?

Using the average month length of 30.44 days, 1 month ≈ 2,629,746 seconds. Different months have different numbers of days (28–31), so this tool uses the Gregorian calendar yearly average.

Is a year exactly 365 days?

An average year = 365.25 days (including leap years), so 1 year ≈ 31,556,952 seconds. The precise value is 365.2422 days/year.

Is my data sent to a server?

No. All conversions happen locally in your browser. The tool works offline once loaded and your data stays private on your device.

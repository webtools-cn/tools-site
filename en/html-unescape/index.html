❓ FAQ
What are HTML entities?
HTML entities are special codes representing characters, e.g. & for &. Used to safely display reserved characters in HTML.
Which entities are supported?
Named entities (& < > " ' ') and numeric entities (& & &).
Is decoding safe?
Completely done in your browser. No data is uploaded or stored.

FAQ

Which is better: Equal Installment or Equal Principal?

Equal Principal has lower total interest, but higher initial payment burden. Equal Installment has stable monthly payments, suitable for those with stable income. For example, a 1M loan over 30 years at 4.9%: Equal Principal saves about 170K in interest compared to Equal Installment.

Is early repayment worth it?

Equal Installment has higher interest portion in early periods — the earlier you repay, the more interest you save. Equal Principal interest decreases over time, so early repayment later saves less. It's recommended to make early repayments within the first 1/3 of the loan term for maximum benefit.

How is monthly rate calculated?

Monthly Rate = Annual Rate ÷ 12. For example, with an annual rate of 4.9%, the monthly rate is approximately 0.4083%. This calculator automatically converts internally.

About Base64 Encoder / Decoder

Free online Base64 Encoder / Decoder that supports Base64 encoding and decoding for both text and files. You can encode text or file content into a Base64 string, or decode a Base64 string back to its original form. All processing happens locally in your browser — nothing is uploaded to any server.

What is Base64 Encoder / Decoder?

Base64 Encoder / Decoder is a free online tool that runs entirely in your browser. No download or signup needed — enter Output and other values to get results instantly. All data is processed locally.

📖 About PE Ratio

PE Ratio (Price-to-Earnings Ratio) is the ratio of a company's stock price to its earnings per share (EPS). It is one of the most widely used valuation metrics, reflecting how much the market is willing to pay for each dollar of earnings.

Formula: PE = Stock Price ÷ EPS

Valuation Reference: PE<10 Potentially Undervalued | 10-20 Reasonable | 20-30 Elevated | >30 Caution. ⚠️ Reasonable PE varies by industry — always compare within the same sector.

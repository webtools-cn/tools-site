What is Money Counter Calculator?

Money Counter Calculator is a free online tool that runs entirely in your browser. No download or signup needed — select a currency and enter quantities for each denomination to get results instantly. All data is processed locally.

Use Cases

  • Count cash after a register shift or business day
  • Verify bank deposits before visiting the bank
  • Track petty cash and expense reimbursements
  • Quick denomination breakdown for travel currency

Key Features

  • Completely Free: No registration, no limits
  • Privacy Safe: Data never leaves your browser
  • Multi-Currency: USD, EUR, GBP, CNY, JPY supported
  • Cross-Platform: Any modern browser

About Binary to Decimal Calculator

The Binary to Decimal Calculator is a free online tool that converts binary numbers to their decimal equivalents. Ideal for computer science students, programmers, and digital circuit designers. Works on mobile and desktop, all calculations run locally in your browser.

How to Use

  1. Enter a binary number (only 0s and 1s)
  2. Click "Calculate"
  3. View the decimal equivalent

FAQ

Amortized vs interest-only?

Amortized: fixed monthly, higher total interest. Interest-only: low initial but principal doesn't decrease.

Pay off early?

If rate >6% or excess cash: yes. If rate <4% and better investments: maybe not. Check prepayment penalties.

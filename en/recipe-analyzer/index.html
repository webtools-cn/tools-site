Features

Free online recipe nutrition analyzer. Enter ingredients and amounts to calculate total calories, protein, fat, carbs, and more. Supports custom ingredient database.

All computation happens on your device — Ingredient Name, Amount and other inputs stay in your browser — no files are uploaded to any server.

Completely free, no registration or login required. Ready to use instantly.

Recipe Nutrition Analyzer FAQ

Is my data safe?

All computation happens on your device — Ingredient Name, Amount and other inputs stay in your browser. Data is never uploaded to any server.

Do I need to register?

No registration or login required. Just open and use.

Does it work on mobile?

Fully responsive — works perfectly on phones and tablets.

What ingredients are supported?

The tool includes a built-in nutrition database with common foods like rice, chicken, eggs, vegetables, and more.

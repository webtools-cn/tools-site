FAQ

What is the breakeven point?

The breakeven is the underlying price at expiration where the option trade neither makes nor loses money. Buy Call = Strike + Premium. Buy Put = Strike - Premium.

What is the risk of buying options?

Option buyers risk is limited to the premium paid. Buy Call has unlimited profit potential. Buy Put profit is capped at (Strike - Premium).

What is the risk of selling options?

Option sellers' max profit is the premium received. Sell Call has unlimited loss potential (stock rises). Sell Put loss is capped at (Strike - Premium).

How many shares per contract?

Standard US equity options represent 100 shares per contract. This calculator defaults to 100, with mini (10) option available.

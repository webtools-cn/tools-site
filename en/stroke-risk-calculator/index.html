💡 Health Recommendations

    📖 About Stroke Risk Assessment

    What is a Stroke?

    A stroke occurs when blood supply to part of the brain is interrupted or reduced, causing brain tissue damage. Globally, 1 in 6 people will experience a stroke in their lifetime. Stroke is a leading cause of death and disability worldwide.

    Types of Stroke

    Ischemic Stroke (~87%): A blood clot blocks a vessel supplying the brain.
    Hemorrhagic Stroke (~13%): A weakened blood vessel ruptures and bleeds into the brain.

    FAST Recognition

    Remember FAST: Face drooping, Arm weakness, Speech difficulty, Time to call emergency services. Time is brain!

    Risk Factors

    Key risk factors assessed: Age (risk doubles every 10 years), Hypertension (most important modifiable factor), Diabetes, Smoking, Cardiovascular disease history, and Atrial fibrillation. Controlling blood pressure below 120/80 can reduce stroke risk by about 40%.

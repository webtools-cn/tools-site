Frequently Asked Questions

What is the density of water?

The density of water at 4°C is 1 g/cm³ = 1000 kg/m³ = 62.4 lb/ft³. Seawater density is approximately 1025 kg/m³.

How do I convert g/cm³ to kg/m³?

1 g/cm³ = 1000 kg/m³. g/cm³ is commonly used for solid and liquid densities, while kg/m³ is the SI standard unit.

What is the density of iron?

The density of iron is approximately 7.87 g/cm³ = 7870 kg/m³ = 491 lb/ft³. Different metals vary widely: aluminum ≈ 2.7 g/cm³, copper ≈ 8.96 g/cm³, gold ≈ 19.3 g/cm³.

What is the difference between US and UK gallon density?

For the same lb/gal value, the UK gallon (4.546L) is larger than the US gallon (3.785L). 1 lb/gal(US) = 119.826 kg/m³, while 1 lb/gal(UK) = 99.776 kg/m³.

Is my data sent to a server?

No. All conversions happen locally in your browser. The tool works offline once loaded and your data stays private on your device.

What Can the AI Content Moderation Checker Do?

The AI Content Moderation Checker is a pure frontend, locally running content safety tool. It detects content risk across 8 key dimensions and flags potentially dangerous content quickly and securely.

The 8 Dimensions

  • 🚫 Hate Speech — Detects hate speech, racist, sexist and other discriminatory content
  • ⚠️ Violence — Detects violent threats and harmful content
  • 🔞 Sexual Content — Detects explicit and adult content
  • 😤 Harassment — Detects personal attacks and online bullying
  • 💔 Self-Harm — Detects self-harm and suicide-related content
  • ⚖️ Illegal Activity — Detects content related to illegal activities
  • 📰 Misinformation — Detects fake news and misleading information
  • 🔒 Privacy Leak — Detects personal and private information

FAQ

Is the AI Content Moderation Checker free to use?

Yes. The AI Content Moderation Checker is completely free. It detects harmful content such as hate speech, violence and harassment across 8 content safety dimensions. It is a pure frontend, local analysis tool with no signup required, 100% browser-based.

Is my text sent to a server?

No. All detection runs locally in your browser. Your text is never uploaded to any server, so your content stays private.

How do I use the AI Content Moderation Checker?

Paste your text in the input area, click the check button, and review the results. All analysis happens on your device.

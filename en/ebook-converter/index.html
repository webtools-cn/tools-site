Can EPUB be read on Kindle?
Generated EPUBs follow EPUB 3.0 spec. Kindle doesn't natively support EPUB — use Calibre to convert to MOBI.
Are files uploaded to a server?
No. All processing is client-side. Your files never leave your device.
File size limits?
Files should be under 50MB. Large EPUBs with many images may take longer to process.

What is Free Online Ebook Format ConverterBase?

Free Online Ebook Format ConverterBase is a free online tool that runs entirely in your browser. No download or signup needed — enter Ebook Format Converter and other values to get results instantly. All data is processed locally.

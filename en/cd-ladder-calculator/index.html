📖 What is CD Ladder Strategy?

CD ladder strategy (Certificate of Deposit Ladder) spreads funds evenly across CDs of different maturities. For example, split $25,000 into 5 equal parts, buying 1-year, 2-year, 3-year, 4-year, and 5-year CDs.

When the shortest CD matures, roll the principal and interest into a new long-term CD, creating a continuous ladder. This gives you one CD maturing each year — enjoying long-term CD rates while maintaining annual liquidity.

Key Benefits

  • Higher Yields：Long-term CDs typically offer higher APY than short-term. The ladder strategy gives you a higher blended yield.
  • Maintain Liquidity：Each year a CD matures, giving you access to funds for emergencies or reinvestment.
  • Interest Rate Risk Management：If rates rise, maturing funds can immediately capture higher rates.
  • Simple and Easy：No frequent trading needed. Set up the ladder and let it run automatically.

Ideal For

  • Retirees seeking stable cash flow
  • Conservative investors seeking better returns than savings accounts
  • Savers with near-term cash needs who want higher interest
  • Optimal placement for emergency funds

❓ FAQ

What's the difference between CD ladder and a 5-year CD?

A 5-year CD locks in the rate for 5 years, but early withdrawal penalties can be severe. A CD ladder has CDs maturing each year, giving you access to long-term rates while maintaining annual liquidity.

How much capital is needed to start a CD ladder?

Most banks require $500-$1,000 minimum per CD. A 5-rung ladder needs at least $2,500-$5,000. We recommend $10,000+ for meaningful interest returns ($2,000+ per rung).

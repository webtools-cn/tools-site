Frequently asked questions

What are the densities of different waxes?

Soy wax ≈ 0.86 g/ml, beeswax ≈ 0.96 g/ml, paraffin ≈ 0.87 g/ml, palm wax ≈ 0.91 g/ml, coconut wax ≈ 0.86 g/ml. Higher density means more wax by weight for the same volume.

How much fragrance oil should I add?

Candle fragrance is typically added at 6%–10% of the wax weight, up to 12% maximum. This calculator uses your input percentage to compute the fragrance weight and the total weight.

Why calculate fragrance by weight, not volume?

Fragrance oil density is close to but not exactly water, so weighing is more accurate, stable, and easier to replicate across batches.

Should I add extra wax for loss?

Melting and pouring usually cause a small loss. In practice prepare about 5%–10% extra wax. This result is the base amount and does not include loss.

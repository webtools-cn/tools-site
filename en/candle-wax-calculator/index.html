Frequently asked questions

How is the wax weight calculated?

Wax weight (g) = fill volume (ml) × wax density (g/ml). Fill volume = container volume × (1 − headspace%). Wax density is close between liquid and solid, so volume × density is a reliable estimate; headspace avoids overflow.

What fragrance load is appropriate?

Typical fragrance load is 6%–10% of wax weight. Above 12% can cause poor burning, frosting or a stuffy smell. Soy wax works well at 8%–10%, paraffin at 6%–8%. This tool computes the exact oil grams from your input.

How do I choose a wick size?

Wick size follows the wax diameter: under 3.8cm uses a small single wick, 3.8–5cm medium, 5–6.4cm large, 6.4–7.6cm extra-large, and over 7.6cm should use a double wick for even melt pools.

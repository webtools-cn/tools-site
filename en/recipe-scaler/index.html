📖 About Recipe Scaling

Recipe Scaler helps you adjust recipe portions proportionally. Enter original ingredient amounts and servings, set target servings, and get automatically scaled amounts for each ingredient.

Use cases: cooking for a larger gathering, reducing portions for weight management, batch baking, and more.

Note: Some ingredients (spices, baking powder) don't scale perfectly linearly - adjust to taste.

FAQ

How are bonuses taxed?

In the US, employers typically use the percentage method: 22% flat withholding for bonuses under $1 million, or 37% for amounts over $1M. Alternatively, bonuses can be combined with regular wages and taxed at your marginal rate.

What other taxes apply?

Bonuses are also subject to FICA: Social Security (6.2% up to $168,600 for 2026) and Medicare (1.45%, plus 0.9% additional over $200K). This calculator includes FICA estimates.

Which method is better?

The flat 22% withholding may over-withhold if you're in a lower bracket, or under-withhold if in a higher bracket. Your actual tax is determined when you file — this calculator helps you estimate your true take-home.

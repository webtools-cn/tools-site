FAQ

What is Single Elimination?
Lose one match and you're out. Ideal for tournaments with many participants. The bracket forms a tree structure where winners advance until a champion is crowned.
What is Double Elimination?
Lose twice to be eliminated. One loss drops you to the losers bracket — you can fight back to the finals. More fair than single elimination, reducing the luck factor.
What is Round Robin?
Every team plays every other team. Rankings by total wins. The fairest format but requires more matches — best for smaller groups.
Can I print the bracket?
Yes! Click the "Print" button or use Ctrl+P. The bracket auto-adapts to print layout.

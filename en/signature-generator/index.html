❓ FAQ
How do I use the signature in Word or PDF?

Download the transparent PNG signature and drag it directly into your Word document or PDF. The transparent background ensures it blends perfectly without covering text. You can also use "Insert Image" in your document editor.

Handwritten vs typed — which is better?

Handwritten signatures look more natural and personal, ideal for documents requiring authentic-looking signatures. Typed signatures are cleaner and more elegant, perfect for email signatures and business cards. Try both!

Why does my downloaded signature look blurry?

The canvas renders at 2x resolution (Retina) for high-quality output. If you need an even larger signature, select a wider canvas or increase the font size in typed mode.

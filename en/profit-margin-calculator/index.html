📚 Formulas

Gross Margin: Measures core business profitability

Gross Margin = (Selling Price - Cost) ÷ Selling Price × 100%

Markup: Shows how much you added on top of cost

Markup = (Selling Price - Cost) ÷ Cost × 100%

Net Margin: Bottom-line profitability after all expenses

Net Margin = (Revenue - COGS - Expenses - Taxes) ÷ Revenue × 100%

FAQ
Are the passwords secure?
Yes, we use the Web Crypto API (crypto.getRandomValues) for cryptographically secure randomness — far safer than Math.random().
How long should a password be?
At least 12 characters. A 12-char mixed password takes thousands of years to brute-force. Our default is 16 characters.
Are passwords stored on a server?
Never. All passwords are generated locally in your browser — no data is ever uploaded.

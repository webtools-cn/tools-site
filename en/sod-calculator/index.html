❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How much area does one pallet of sod cover?
A standard pallet of sod covers about 450 square feet (about 42 m²) and typically contains 50 sod pieces of roughly 9 sq ft each.

How much area does one roll of sod cover?
A standard roll of sod covers about 10 square feet (about 0.93 m²), usually measuring 2 ft x 5 ft or 1.5 ft x 6 ft.

What waste factor should I use?
A 5-10% waste factor is generally recommended for cutting, edge trimming and irregular shapes. Use 10-15% for complex layouts.

What is a 401k?

A 401k is the most common US retirement savings plan. You contribute pre-tax dollars from your paycheck, and employers often match a percentage. The 2025 contribution limit is $23,500 ($30,500 with catch-up for age 50+). Traditional 401k: tax-deferred contributions, taxed at withdrawal. Roth 401k: after-tax contributions, tax-free withdrawals.

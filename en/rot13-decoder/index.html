About This Tool

ROT (Rotate) is a simple letter substitution cipher that shifts letters in the alphabet. ROT13 shifts letters by 13 positions (A→N), ROT5 shifts digits only, ROT18=ROT13+ROT5, ROT47 shifts printable ASCII characters. ROT13 is self-inverse since 13 is half of 26. All processing is done locally in your browser.

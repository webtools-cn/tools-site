❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use Pdf Page Numbers on my phone?

Yes. Pdf Page Numbers is fully responsive and works on phones, tablets, and desktops. The interface adapts automatically to your screen size.

Do I need to install any software?

No. Pdf Page Numbers is a web-based tool — just open it in your browser and start using it. No downloads, plugins, or extensions required.

Does Pdf Page Numbers upload my data to a server?

No. Pdf Page Numbers uses client-side processing — everything runs in your browser. Your data never leaves your device, and the tool works even without an internet connection.

Is Pdf Page Numbers free to use?

Yes, Pdf Page Numbers is completely free to use. No registration or login required — all features are available immediately.

Is my data safe when using Pdf Page Numbers?

Absolutely. Everything runs locally: Position and Start Number are processed on your device, never sent to a server. No data is sent to any server, so there's zero risk of data leaks. Your information stays completely under your control.

Are there any usage limits for Pdf Page Numbers?

Pdf Page Numbers has no usage limits — it's free and unlimited. Since processing is done client-side, speed depends on your device. For very large files, we recommend processing in batches for the best experience.

Will the PDF quality decrease after processing?

We preserve original quality whenever possible. For compression, you can choose the compression level to balance file size and quality.

Does it support encrypted PDF files?

Some tools support encrypted PDFs. If the file is password-protected, you'll need to provide the password first.

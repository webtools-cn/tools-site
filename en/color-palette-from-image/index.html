❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use Color Palette From Image on my phone?

Yes. Color Palette From Image is fully responsive and works on phones, tablets, and desktops. The interface adapts automatically to your screen size.

Do I need to install any software?

No. Color Palette From Image is a web-based tool — just open it in your browser and start using it. No downloads, plugins, or extensions required.

Is my data safe when using Color Palette From Image?

Absolutely. Everything runs locally: Color count and CSS Variables are processed on your device, never sent to a server. No data is sent to any server, so there's zero risk of data leaks. Your information stays completely under your control.

Is Color Palette From Image free to use?

Yes, Color Palette From Image is completely free to use. No registration or login required — all features are available immediately.

Are there any usage limits for Color Palette From Image?

Color Palette From Image has no usage limits — it's free and unlimited. Since processing is done client-side, speed depends on your device. For very large files, we recommend processing in batches for the best experience.

Does Color Palette From Image upload my data to a server?

No. Color Palette From Image uses client-side processing — everything runs in your browser. Your data never leaves your device, and the tool works even without an internet connection.

What image formats are supported?

Common formats including PNG, JPEG, WebP, GIF, BMP, and SVG are supported. The exact formats depend on the specific tool.

Will image quality decrease after processing?

For lossless formats (like PNG), quality is preserved. For lossy formats (like JPEG), you can choose the output quality.

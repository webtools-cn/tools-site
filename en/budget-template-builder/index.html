How to Use

1. Enter your monthly after-tax income and any other income.

2. Fill in budget amounts for each category. Add or remove items as needed.

3. Click "Refresh Analysis" to see your 50/30/20 rule compliance and remaining balance.

4. Export to CSV for Excel or Google Sheets, or print as PDF.

5. Data is saved in your browser's local storage — it persists between visits.

FAQ

Do I have to follow 50/30/20 strictly?

Not necessarily. This is a general guideline by Elizabeth Warren. High-rent cities may require 60%+ on needs, high earners may save 30%+. The key is finding a balance that works for you.

Where is my budget data stored?

All data is stored only in your browser's local storage (localStorage). Nothing is uploaded to any server. Data persists when you close the page but is lost if you clear browser data. Export CSV to back up.

How do I set reasonable budget amounts?

Track your actual spending for 1-2 months first, then create a budget. Use the 50/30/20 rule as a starting point and adjust based on your real spending patterns.

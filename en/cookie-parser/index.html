About Free Cookie Parser - Online Cookie String Decoder

Free online cookie parser. Decode cookie strings into key-value tables with URL decoding, expiry display, and security flags (HttpOnly/Secure/SameSite). 100% client-side. Try now!

How to Use Free Cookie Parser - Online Cookie String Decoder?

Enter your content in the input area, click the action button, and view the result. All processing is done locally in your browser.

What are HttpOnly and Secure?

HttpOnly: JS cannot access the cookie. Secure: sent over HTTPS only. Both are critical security flags.

What does SameSite do?

Controls cross-site cookie sending: Strict=same-site only, Lax=partial cross-site, None=all cross-site (requires Secure). Used for CSRF protection.

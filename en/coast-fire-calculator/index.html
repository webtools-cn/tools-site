📘 What is Coast FIRE?

Coast FIRE is a branch of the FIRE movement. Core idea: once your investment principal reaches a certain point, even if you stop saving, compound interest alone will grow it to your FIRE target by traditional retirement age.

Formula

Coast FIRE Number = FIRE Target / (1 + annual return)^years to retirement

Example

  • FIRE Target: $1,000,000
  • Years to retirement (age 65): 25
  • Annual return: 7%
  • Coast FIRE Number = $1,000,000 / (1.07)^25 ≈ $184,000

If you have $184,000+ today, congratulations — you've achieved Coast FIRE! No need to save another dollar; compound interest will get you to $1M in 25 years.

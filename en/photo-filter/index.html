📸 Online Photo Filter Tool — Free Image Effect Editor

The Photo Filter Tool is a completely free online image effect editor. Upload JPG, PNG, or WebP images and apply 20+ professional filter effects in real-time. All processing happens locally in your browser — images are never uploaded to any server, ensuring your privacy.

✨ Available Filter Effects

  • Vintage Filters: Sepia, Vintage, Warm Tone, Cool Tone
  • Black & White: Grayscale, High-Contrast Noir
  • Color Enhancement: Saturate, Vibrant, Fade, Dramatic
  • Special Effects: Invert, Pixelate, Blur, Sharpen, Emboss
  • Tone Adjustment: Sunset, Ocean Blue, Forest Green
  • Creative: Duotone, Glitch, Posterize

🔧 Key Features

  • Real-time Preview: See filter effects instantly as you switch
  • Adjustable Strength: 0-200% intensity slider for fine control
  • Brightness/Contrast: Independent brightness and contrast adjustment
  • One-Click Download: Download processed images as PNG instantly
  • Privacy Safe: Pure browser Canvas processing, no server upload
  • Completely Free: No registration, no limits, no watermarks

📋 FAQ

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. All image processing is done locally in your browser using the Canvas API. Your images never leave your device.

What image formats are supported?

You can import JPG, PNG, and WebP images. Exported images are in PNG format for best quality.

Can I apply multiple filters at once?

Each filter is applied independently. You can try different effects one at a time. Brightness and contrast adjustments can be combined with any filter.

Do filters affect image quality?

Filter effects are rendered on Canvas and exported as lossless PNG. For large images, consider resizing first for a smoother experience.

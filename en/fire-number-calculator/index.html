📖 About FIRE & the 4% Rule

What is FIRE?

FIRE = Financial Independence, Retire Early. The core philosophy: achieve a high savings rate (50%+) and invest wisely to accumulate enough assets before traditional retirement age, achieving freedom from mandatory work.

The 4% Rule Explained

The Trinity Study found: if you withdraw no more than 4% of your initial portfolio annually (inflation-adjusted), your assets have a 95%+ probability of lasting 30+ years.

Formula: FIRE Number = Annual Expenses ÷ Safe Withdrawal Rate

FIRE Multipliers by Withdrawal Rate

  • 3.0% SWR → 33.3× multiplier (extremely conservative)
  • 3.5% SWR → 28.6× multiplier (fairly conservative)
  • 4.0% SWR → 25× multiplier (standard FIRE)
  • 4.5% SWR → 22.2× multiplier (moderately aggressive)
  • 5.0% SWR → 20× multiplier (aggressive, higher risk)

FIRE Types

  • Lean FIRE: Minimalist lifestyle, expenses <$25,000/year, target ~$625,000
  • Regular FIRE: Normal lifestyle, expenses $40,000-60,000/year, target $1-1.5M
  • Fat FIRE: Comfortable lifestyle, expenses >$100,000/year, target $2.5M+
  • Coast FIRE: Enough principal saved, no further contributions needed, let compound interest work
  • Barista FIRE: Semi-retired, part-time work covers some expenses

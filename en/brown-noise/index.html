What is Brown Noise?

White noise contains all frequencies (20Hz-20kHz) with stronger low frequencies (decreasing at 6dB per octave). It sounds like a deep rumbling sound like a waterfall or distant thunder. White noise can effectively mask background sounds, being especially effective for relaxation, anxiety relief, and deep sleep.

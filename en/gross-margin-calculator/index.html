FAQ

What is the difference between Gross Margin and Markup?

Gross Margin = Profit ÷ Price × 100%, while Markup = Profit ÷ Cost × 100%. Example: Cost = $80, Price = $100, Gross Margin = 20%, Markup = 25%.

What is a good gross margin?

Good gross margins vary by industry: SaaS 70%-85%, software 60%-70%, retail 30%-50%, construction 20%-40%. The average is around 50%.

How do I calculate gross margin?

Enter your Cost and Price, and the calculator returns Gross Margin, Markup and Profit automatically.

Is my data uploaded to a server?

No. All calculations are performed locally in your browser. Your data is never uploaded.

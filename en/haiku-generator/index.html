📖 About Haiku

Haiku is a traditional Japanese poetry form consisting of three lines with a 5-7-5 syllable pattern.

  • Line 1: 5 syllables
  • Line 2: 7 syllables
  • Line 3: 5 syllables

Traditional haiku often include a kigo (seasonal word) and nature imagery. This generator uses preset word banks to compose haiku.

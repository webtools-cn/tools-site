Frequently Asked Questions

How to calculate annual income from hourly rate?

Annual Income = Hourly Rate × Hours per Day × Days per Week × Weeks per Year. Typically: 8 hours/day × 5 days/week × 52 weeks = 2,080 hours/year. So annual income = hourly rate × 2,080.

How to convert monthly salary to annual?

Annual salary = Monthly salary × 12. Conversely, monthly salary = Annual salary ÷ 12. Note that some companies offer 13th or 14th month pay, which increases actual annual compensation.

How to convert weekly pay to annual?

Annual pay = Weekly pay × 52. This is a simplified calculation based on 52 weeks per year, useful for freelancers paid weekly.

What is $30/hour in annual salary?

At $30/hour, working 8 hours/day, 5 days/week, 52 weeks/year: Annual salary = $30 × 8 × 5 × 52 = $62,400. If working 10 hours/day, annual salary = $78,000.

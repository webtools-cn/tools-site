📖 About Image Merge Tool

A completely free online image merge tool. No registration, no software download required. Combine multiple images into one directly in your browser. Supports horizontal, vertical, and grid layout modes.

All computation happens on your device — ️ Image Merge Tool, other values and other inputs stay in your browser — images are never uploaded to any server, fully protecting your privacy and data security.

✨ Key Features

• Three Merge Modes: Horizontal, vertical, and grid layout

• Drag & Drop Sorting: Freely reorder images before merging

• Custom Spacing: Set the gap between images

• Background Color: Customize the background color

• Alignment Options: Top/Center/Bottom for horizontal, Left/Center/Right for vertical

• Multiple Output Formats: Export as PNG, JPEG, or WebP

❓ FAQ

Do images get uploaded to a server?

No. This tool processes everything locally in your browser. All images are merged on your device and never uploaded to any server.

What merge modes are supported?

Three merge modes: Horizontal (side by side), Vertical (top to bottom), and Grid layout (specify images per row).

What image formats are supported?

JPEG, PNG, GIF, WebP, BMP, and SVG for input. Output defaults to PNG, with JPEG and WebP also available.

Can I reorder the images?

Yes. After adding images, you can drag and drop to reorder them. Images are merged in the displayed order.

What if images have different sizes?

The tool provides alignment options for handling size differences. In grid mode, all images are centered within uniform cells.

❓ FAQ
What are the benefits of memory games?
Memory games help improve short-term memory, attention span, and focus. Suitable for all ages for brain training.
What difficulty levels are available?
Three levels: Easy (4×4 = 8 pairs), Medium (6×6 = 18 pairs), and Hard (8×8 = 32 pairs). Start easy and work your way up.
How to play?
Click to flip two cards. If they match, they stay face up. If not, they flip back. Find all pairs with the fewest moves possible.

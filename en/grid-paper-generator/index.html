📖 About Grid Paper Generator

Our free online grid paper generator lets you create printable graph paper, dot grid paper, and cross grid paper in seconds. Whether you're a student doing math homework, a teacher preparing worksheets, a designer sketching layouts, or a bullet journal enthusiast, you can generate perfectly customized grid paper with just a few clicks.

🎯 Common Uses

  • Math & Science: Plot functions, draw geometric shapes, create coordinate systems
  • Education: Create worksheets, exam papers, and classroom materials
  • Engineering: Architectural sketches, circuit diagrams, mechanical drafts
  • Bullet Journaling: Create custom dot grid or square grid inserts
  • Pixel Art: Design pixel art and cross-stitch patterns
  • Calligraphy: Practice sheets with custom grid layouts

📐 Grid Type Guide

  • Square Grid (Graph Paper): Standard square cells. Most versatile — ideal for math plotting and engineering sketches.
  • Dot Grid: Small dots instead of solid lines for a cleaner look. Perfect for Bullet Journaling and note-taking.
  • Cross Grid: Crosshairs at intersections instead of full lines. Clean and professional — great for precision drawing.

🖨️ Printing Tips

For A4 paper: select the "A4 (300DPI)" preset (2480×3508 pixels), with grid size 20-30px recommended. For US Letter: select "Letter (300DPI)". For screen use, 800×600 or 1200×800 is sufficient.

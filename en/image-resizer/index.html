❓ FAQ

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. All image processing happens in your browser using the Canvas API. Your images never leave your device. 100% private and secure.

What image formats are supported?

Input: JPG/JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP, SVG. Output: JPEG, PNG, and WebP.

Does resizing reduce image quality?

Upscaling may cause blurriness. Downscaling generally maintains good quality. You can adjust the JPEG/WebP compression level via the quality slider.

Can I batch resize multiple images?

This tool handles one image at a time. For batch processing, try our Batch Image Resizer.

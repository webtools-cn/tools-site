How to Use

The Spinner Wheel is a fully-featured random selection tool. Here's how to get the most out of it:

Edit Options: Type your options in the text box on the right, one per line. The wheel updates automatically. You need at least 2 options, and can have up to 100.

Spin the Wheel: Click the "Spin" button or click the center of the wheel. The wheel spins with a randomized duration (3-5 seconds) and lands on a random option.

View Results: After the wheel stops, the result is displayed below. The last 10 results are saved in the history.

Use Presets: Click any preset button on the right to instantly load a pre-made option list.

Use Cases

Raffles & Giveaways: Use the wheel at company events, class parties, or social gatherings for fair random drawings. Add participant names or prize names as options and spin.

Classroom Activities: Teachers can add student names or question numbers to randomly select participants, making lessons more engaging and interactive.

Decision Making: Can't decide where to eat or what to do? Put your options on the wheel and let fate decide. No more endless debates!

Game Night: Use the wheel as a random event generator for board games and team-building activities to add unpredictability and fun.

FAQ

Are the wheel results truly random?

Yes. The wheel uses JavaScript's Math.random() function to generate a random stopping angle, ensuring each option has an equal probability of being selected. The spinning animation is purely for visual effect.

Can I customize the wheel options?

Yes. You can freely edit the option list in the input box, one option per line. Supports up to 100 options, minimum 2 options required. The wheel updates automatically when you modify the options.

Does the wheel have sound effects?

Yes. The wheel plays a ticking sound while spinning and a celebratory chime when it stops. You can toggle sound on or off using the switch on the page.

Is my data uploaded to a server?

No. This tool runs entirely in your browser. All option data and spinning calculations are performed locally — no data is ever sent to any server.

Are there preset templates available?

Yes. We provide several preset templates including 'Yes/No', 'Letters A-F', 'Color Picker', 'Restaurant Picker', and more. Click any preset to instantly load it.

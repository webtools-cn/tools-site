❓ FAQ

How to calculate triangle area?
Area = base × height ÷ 2. Or use Heron's formula: with sides a,b,c, s=(a+b+c)/2, area=√(s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)).

What is Heron's formula?
Used when you know all three sides. Semi-perimeter s=(a+b+c)/2, area=√(s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)). Works for any triangle.

What is Discount Calculator?

Discount Calculator is a free online tool that runs entirely in your browser. No download or signup needed — enter Original Price and Discount to get results instantly. All data is processed locally.

Use Cases

  • Compare percentage, fixed-amount, and tiered deals to find the best value
  • Work out the final price before heading to checkout
  • Calculate how much you save on sales and seasonal promotions
  • Estimate equivalent discount rates across different offers

Key Features

  • Completely Free: No registration, no limits
  • Privacy Safe: Data never leaves your browser
  • Cross-Platform: Any modern browser
  • Zero Dependencies: Open and use

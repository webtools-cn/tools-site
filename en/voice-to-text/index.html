📖 How to Use

This tool uses the browser's built-in Web Speech API. All processing is done locally — no data is ever uploaded.

  • Select the recognition language and click the microphone button
  • Speak into your microphone — text appears in real-time
  • Click the microphone again or say "stop" to end recording
  • Copy, clear, or download your transcription

Browser Support: Chrome 25+, Edge 79+, Safari 14.1+. Firefox not supported.
